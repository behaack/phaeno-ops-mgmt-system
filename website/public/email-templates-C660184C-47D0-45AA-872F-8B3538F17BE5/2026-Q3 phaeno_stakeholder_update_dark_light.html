Chemistry-to-data traceability
The papers describe how PSeq allows individual molecules to be chemically processed from biological samples, preserving their individual identity and reconstructing their entire structure, on a very large scale. Uniquely, PSeq creates an evidence bundle for each molecule which links the final structure backwards through each process beginning with the raw data uploaded from the sequencer.

The initial validation (fourth) paper presents detailed evidence for foundational capabilities of PSeq, including the special molecular architecture of PSeq DNA libraries, and each step in deducing the structure of an individual molecule is described in a Case Study for an RNA exemplar. This illustration is feasible because each step in the platform can be traced back, ultimately, to the NGS sequencer deciding the identity of each nucleotide, in each raw data record.
