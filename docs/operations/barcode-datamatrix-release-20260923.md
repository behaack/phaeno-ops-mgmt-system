## Database change and activation boundary

The single new migration is `20260923223407_AddContainerBarcodeNamespaces`. It adds barcode namespaces to stock tubes, stock kits, return kits, registered sample tubes and laboratory containers; creates `lab_ops.lab_container_barcodes`; backfills supplier namespaces from selected supplier products or a uniquely matching historical supplier name; gives POMS-generated containers the `PHAENO` namespace; and preserves unresolved history under `LEGACY`. It replaces global barcode uniqueness with namespace-plus-value uniqueness. Existing global unique indexes guarantee that the backfill cannot introduce a namespace collision among existing rows in each table. Historical barcode symbology is recorded as `Unknown` because it was not persisted.
