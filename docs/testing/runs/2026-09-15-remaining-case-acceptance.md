## Approved policy and implementation

The Product Owner approved: **accepted processing that ultimately fails remains billable at the accepted price; any credit or replacement is a separate reviewed remedy.** Failed processing, rejected intake and cancelled work retain different meanings.

- Final Lab facts now project Completed only for successful processing with an eligible scientifically approved package; confirmed exhausted-material failure projects Failed. Intake/processing holds do not produce terminal facts. Receipt and accession retain their own history. Replays do not rewrite recorded outcomes or reasons.
- Job completion rechecks current Lab outcomes and blocking exceptions in its transaction. Failed samples remain billable; the accepted quote and approved billing rules determine the invoice. Completion, invoice, notice and replay receipt remain atomic, including PDF recovery after failure.
- Partial cancellation accepts exact sample IDs, checks the Job version and current Lab eligibility, and requires a finalized authorized roster. Received samples and invalid, duplicate, foreign or whole-active-roster selections are rejected. Dispatched or mixed selected/unaffected shipments require shipping review/separation. Eligible wholly selected unshipped shipments are cancelled; bound physical stock and unaffected reservations remain protected. The decision does not silently change accepted pricing.
- Trial/result notices use the configured Portal base address and the owning record ID. No bearer token, private investigation text or additional recipient is introduced.
- Billing/cancellation help and the database reference document are updated. The existing status column stores strings; no schema migration or persisted model shape changed.
