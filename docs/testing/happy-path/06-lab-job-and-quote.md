# HP-06 — Lab request, quote acceptance and sample roster

**People:** Customer organization administrator; Phaeno pricing operator and the configured independent price reviewer.

**Ready before starting:** Research Department with Ready Lab service, shipping configuration, active approved workflow from HP-05, online administrator and approved billing/tax. This script uses manual pricing for two samples of one approved biological source.

| Step | Person and action | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Customer: open Research → Lab services → Request lab service. Enter the run-prefixed Job name, approved biological source with sample count 2, storage requirements and safety declaration. Choose Create job. | A Job number and view-first Job workspace appear. |
| 2 | Review Job pricing details and choose Submit for pricing. Confirm the prohibited-data statement. | The pricing profile is submitted; Your next step identifies Phaeno's pricing responsibility. |
| 3 | Pricing operator: open that Job in Order operations and prepare its quote using the active specimen-priced service item and quantity 2. Enter the approved fixture price and terms. | The quote preview agrees with the submitted scope, currency, subtotal, approved tax and total. |
| 4 | Complete the configured independent price review, then issue the quote. | A numbered, dated quote revision is available to the Customer. |
| 5 | Customer: open the Job's Quote and billing details and download the quote PDF from Actions. | On-screen terms and the PDF identify the same Job, scope, revision and amount. |
| 6 | Review and Accept quote, supplying the required PO and affirmations. | Accepted terms are retained and sample entry opens. |
| 7 | Open Samples and shipping. In the approved source band, add SAMPLE-A with one tube and SAMPLE-B with two tubes. | Counts show 2 samples and 3 tubes in the correct source group. |
| 8 | Choose Review and finalize list. Review the exact sample/tube summary, affirm the statement and finalize. | The roster is finalized and shipping is the next task. |
| 9 | Reopen the Job from Lab services. | Accepted quote, sample identities and next-step guidance are retained. |

**Done:** Record Job number, quote revision/total and both sample references. Continue [HP-07](07-transportation-kit-supply.md) on this Job.
