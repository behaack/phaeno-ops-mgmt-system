# HP-17 — Publish curated data and grant Department access

**People:** Phaeno source/publication administrator; provisioning administrator; Customer Research member.

**Ready before starting:** Approved Phaeno-owned, de-identified source evidence and clean allowed files with known checksums; active Customer Research membership. Use a dedicated curated dataset.

| Step | Person and action | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Source administrator: open Data provisioning → Source registry and register the source with its ownership and de-identification evidence. | The source workspace retains its identity and provenance. |
| 2 | Build its draft revision, upload the approved files and wait for clean scan results. Review the manifest. | File identities, sizes and checksums match the prepared source. |
| 3 | Mark that exact source revision Ready. | The ready revision is immutable and available for curated use. |
| 4 | Open Curated catalog and create the curated draft from that source revision. Enter its title, summary and scientific context. | The draft retains the exact source snapshot. |
| 5 | Review and Publish the package version, then mark it eligible for the Customer audience. | A published, eligible immutable version is listed. |
| 6 | Provisioning administrator: open Organization grants. Select the Customer, Research Department and that exact version. Review the intended dates/source/reason and grant access. | The saved grant identifies the intended Department and pinned version. |
| 7 | Research member: open Data Library and the granted dataset. | The published title, context, version and file manifest are visible. |
| 8 | Download the package and inspect its files against the manifest. | The authorized package is complete and readable. |
| 9 | Provisioning administrator: reopen the grant and review its download history. | Publication, access scope and recorded download remain connected. |

**Done:** Record source revision, dataset/version, grant and downloaded package references.
