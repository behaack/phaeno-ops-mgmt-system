# HP-08 — Pack and send samples

**Person:** Customer administrator preparing the shipment.

**Ready before starting:** Same Job and acknowledged kit from HP-07, three prepared sample tubes and the approved packing instructions.

| Step | Action | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Open the Job → Actions → Prepare containers and select the Research delivery location. Scan the received kit's physical container barcode, review the three-tube allocation and confirm preparation. | One return shipment covers SAMPLE-A's one tube and SAMPLE-B's two tubes. |
| 2 | Open Match tubes / Scan tubes into this shipment. Scan the registered permanent barcode for each displayed sample/tube slot. | Each saved scan shows the correct sample and physical identity; progress reaches 3 of 3. |
| 3 | Choose Review and confirm shipping insert. Check destination, handling instructions, container and sample-to-tube mapping; confirm. | A current shipping-insert revision is saved for this shipment. |
| 4 | Use Print shipping insert. Inspect the printed receiving sheet's readable identities, totals and PH-P- barcode. Review Full packing instructions and sample / tube list on screen. | The printed sheet and full on-screen crosswalk identify the same shipment. |
| 5 | Follow the approved packing instructions and place the current insert inside the assigned container with the three matched tubes. Confirm in the Portal that it was successfully printed and packed. | Physical contents match the saved crosswalk; Your next step becomes Record shipment. |
| 6 | Hand the package to the controlled carrier, then choose Record shipment and save its carrier, tracking and dispatch time. | The shipment is Sent and appears under After you send. |
| 7 | Reopen the Job and shipment from the list. | The Job number, sent state, current packet and tracking are retained. |

**Done:** Record shipment and packet references and return tracking. Give the same insert and shipment reference to the Lab receiver for [HP-09](09-receipt-and-accession.md).
