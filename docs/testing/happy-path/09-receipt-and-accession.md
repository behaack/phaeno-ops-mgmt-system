# HP-09 — Receive and accession the sample shipment

**Person:** Authorized laboratory receiver/operator.

**Ready before starting:** Arrived shipment from HP-08, its current printed insert, three intact suitable tubes and known freezer-box locations.

| Step | Action | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Open Lab operations → Receipt & accession → Receive shipments. Find the expected shipment by its saved reference/tracking. | Customer, Job and expected tube count agree with the physical package. |
| 2 | Scan the insert's PH-P- code in Shipping insert barcode and choose Receive shipment. | Arrival is recorded; the confirmation identifies this shipment and offers Accession samples. |
| 3 | Choose Accession samples and open the received container. | Its three expected tube rows appear. |
| 4 | Inspect each tube, scan its Supplier tube barcode and choose Identify tube. | The identified selection contains all three correct tube identities. |
| 5 | Choose Accept all remaining (3), review the identities, enter each actual freezer-box barcode and confirm inspection. Select Accept 3 tubes. | All three intake decisions are Accepted with saved locations and accession lineage. |
| 6 | Choose Done, then open the Job in Lab work → Tubes. | Received tubes display their accepted state, barcodes and storage information. |
| 7 | Open a tube's detail and follow its sample/Job context. | The original sample, receipt and physical tube remain traceable. |

**Done:** Record accession and tube references. Continue [HP-10](10-library-preparation.md). The Customer's same Job now shows the recorded receipt progress.
