# HP-01 — Public discovery and a technical-brief request

**People:** Visitor; Phaeno Web Operations administrator.

**Ready before starting:** Published Website, known public paper, enabled form/email delivery and a tester-controlled recipient.

| Step | Person and action | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Visitor: open the homepage and follow the technology navigation. | The proposition and intended research use are understandable. |
| 2 | Use site search to find the prepared public paper, then open it. | The result opens the correct readable publication. |
| 3 | Open the contact/technical-brief form. Enter the prepared details, select the technical-brief request, give the displayed consent and complete verification. | Required information and what will be sent are clear. |
| 4 | Submit the form once. | A confirmation acknowledges the request and describes the next step. |
| 5 | Web Operations administrator: open the saved intake and its notification/delivery record. | The requested brief, recipient and delivery progress match the submission. |
| 6 | Visitor: open the delivered message in the controlled inbox and follow the brief link. | The requested brief is readable and agrees with the Website request. |

**Done:** Brief received and opened. Record intake reference and the recipient alias, plus the clearest UX observation.
