# HP-02 — Lead to Company, Contact and Opportunity

**Person:** Phaeno commercial user.

**Ready before starting:** A new test lead with Company/contact details, an active pipeline and an agreed successful sales scenario.

| Step | Action | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Open CRM → Leads and create the test Lead with its source and owner. | The saved Lead opens with a recognizable name and New status. |
| 2 | Record the initial conversation and move the Lead to Working. | Activity and ownership are visible in the same record. |
| 3 | Create a follow-up task with an owner and due date; complete the prepared follow-up and mark the task complete. | The task and completion appear in context. |
| 4 | Record the qualification details and mark the Lead Qualified. | The Lead is ready for conversion. |
| 5 | Choose Convert and review creation of the new Company, Contact and Opportunity. Confirm. | The converted Lead links to the resulting records. |
| 6 | Open the Company and Contact, then return to the Opportunity. Confirm the contact's Company relationship and buying-team information. | Relationship information is consistent across the records. |
| 7 | Advance the Opportunity through the configured stages, recording the prepared sales facts, and mark it Won. | The saved Opportunity shows Won and retains its activity and ownership. |
| 8 | Find the Opportunity again from the pipeline and open its Company. | The records remain easy to locate and ready for the access handoff. |

**Done:** Record the Lead, Company, Contact and Opportunity references. Continue [HP-03](03-customer-onboarding.md) with this Company. Won is a commercial outcome; access is completed in that next script.
