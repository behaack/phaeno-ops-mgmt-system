# HP-12 — Review, release, download and complete the Job

**People:** Laboratory operator; independent Scientific Reviewer; Result Release Manager; Customer member; Commercial Operator.

**Ready before starting:** Completed HP-11 work, all required scientific evidence and one upstream-registered final package per sample. Packages contain all expected artifacts with checksums and clean scans. Reviewer has contributed none of the receipt/execution/QC/batch/sendout work. Result release is enabled and billing is approved.

| Step | Person and action | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Laboratory operator: open Lab operations → Results & review, select the Job and move the completed work into Scientific review. | The Job is available for independent review with its evidence. |
| 2 | Scientific Reviewer: inspect each sample's source-to-library lineage, completed work, QC, provider facts and final-package manifest. | Every required result can be traced to its submitted sample. |
| 3 | Choose Record scientific approval, select the exact complete package and record the approval and prepared Customer-safe QC summary. Repeat for the second sample. | Each approval pins its package; the work reaches Ready for release. |
| 4 | Release Manager: open Order operations → Result release. Open each of this Job's sample packages, review its scientific approval and files, and confirm publication. | Both approved sample packages become available to the Customer. |
| 5 | Customer member: open the Job → After you send and its released results. Review sample names, versions, file lists and displayed download deadlines. | Results Available agrees with the two released sample packages. |
| 6 | Download each complete package. Open the saved ZIPs/files and the retained package receipt. | Expected files are readable; completed downloads and manifest metadata are recorded. |
| 7 | Commercial Operator: open the same Job in Order operations → Complete Job. Review the two final Completed sample outcomes and billing details; choose Confirm completion. | The Job completes and one numbered invoice with an immutable PDF is created. |
| 8 | Customer administrator: review the Job's invoice and download its PDF. | Scope, amount, tax, terms and due date agree with the accepted terms and completion. |

**Done:** Record approvals, release versions, completed-download evidence and invoice number/total. Continue [HP-13](13-payment-and-reconciliation.md). Customer Lab result delivery does not depend on payment balance.
