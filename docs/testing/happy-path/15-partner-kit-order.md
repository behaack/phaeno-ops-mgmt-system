# HP-15 — Partner PSeq Kit purchase and fulfillment

**People:** Partner organization administrator; Phaeno commercial and Kit-fulfillment staff.

**Ready before starting:** Active entitled Partner, approved billing and Assembly credit, negotiated offering allowing quantity 1, frozen included Assembly profile, valid delivery address and physical Kit with lot/expiry.

| Step | Person and action | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Partner administrator: open PSeq Kit orders and create an order for one unit of the prepared offering. Enter the delivery and PO details. | The order identifies the intended Kit and included Assembly scope. |
| 2 | Choose Review PSeq Kit order. Review quantity, price, terms, address and included profile; place the order. | One purchase and one included Assembly case are retained. |
| 3 | Commercial staff: open the PSeq Kit order in Order operations, review and complete its commercial acceptance. | The order can proceed to fulfillment under the reviewed terms. |
| 4 | Choose Open Lab work. Prepare the physical Kit and record lot, expiry, carrier and tracking when shipped. | The original unit is shipped with its saved Kit identity. |
| 5 | Partner administrator: reopen the order and its Included assembly cases. | Shipment facts, Kit label and submission deadline are visible; Prepare inputs is available. |

**Done:** Record original purchase, shipment, unit and included-case references. Continue [HP-16](16-included-assembly.md) with that exact case. This is a purchased Partner reagent Kit, separate from Customer transportation supplies.
