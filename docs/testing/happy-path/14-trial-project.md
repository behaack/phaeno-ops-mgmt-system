# HP-14 — Prospect Trial from scope to delivered results

**People:** Trial coordinator; separate Commercial and Scientific Operations approvers; Prospect organization administrator; Lab team, independent reviewer and release manager.

**Ready before starting:** Separate active Prospect/Opportunity, signed-in Prospect administrator, approved extracted-RNA Trial definitions and a two-sample allowance. Both samples use one tube each. A registered Trial shipping kit and approved scientific/file facilities are prepared.

| Step | Person and action | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Coordinator: create the Trial Project request from the active Opportunity, then choose Start Trial on that request. | Trial creation retains the Company and request context. |
| 2 | Draft the objective, allowance, analyses/workflow, deliverables, submission window and material terms from the prepared scope. Submit scope for approval. | One frozen scope revision awaits the two decisions. |
| 3 | Commercial approver and Scientific Operations approver: independently review and approve that same revision. | Both approvals are recorded and Prospect acceptance becomes available. |
| 4 | Prospect administrator: open the Trial, Review and accept scope and affirm the RUO/no-PHI terms. | The accepted revision and sample-submission action are visible. |
| 5 | Choose Submit samples. Enter both coded samples, one tube each and all approved extracted-RNA metadata. Review and submit. | Two authorized samples and their shipment are linked to the Trial. |
| 6 | Open Shipping and packets and select the Trial shipment with its prepared, registered shipment-specific kit. Follow HP-08 steps 2–7 for the Trial's two tubes and current insert. | The Trial shipment has matched tubes, printed insert and recorded dispatch. |
| 7 | Lab team: perform HP-09, HP-10 and HP-11 using these Trial records and their two-tube counts. | Accepted material advances through successful preparation and sequencing with Trial lineage. |
| 8 | Upstream owner supplies complete registered packages; independent reviewer performs HP-12 steps 1–3 for both Trial samples. | Scientifically approved packages satisfy the Trial's frozen deliverables. |
| 9 | Release manager: return to the Trial release workspace, select the approved package for every submitted sample and release Complete. | The Trial completes and its full archive and retention dates become available. |
| 10 | Prospect administrator: download the complete Trial archive and inspect the Trial manifest and files. | Scope revision, sample/file mapping, checksums and RUO statement agree with the accepted Trial. |
| 11 | Coordinator: review the completed Trial milestone in CRM and record a commercial follow-up owner and date. | Scientific completion and the next commercial conversation are both clear. |

**Done:** Record Trial, scope revision, sample/shipment, approval and complete-release references. This journey ends with Trial delivery; it has no quote or payment step. Record linked Lab-script results under this Trial's references separately from the Customer run.
