# HP-04 — Department settings and member access

**People:** Customer organization administrator; Department administrator; existing Customer member.

**Ready before starting:** Onboarded Customer from HP-03. The Department administrator is assigned to Research; the member already has an active Customer account. Research is the Department used for the Lab journey.

| Step | Person and action | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Organization administrator: open Departments and create a Department named with the run prefix. | The Department appears and opens as a saved record. |
| 2 | Review the organization's purchase-order setting and the Research Department's inherited value. | The effective setting and its source are understandable. |
| 3 | Department administrator: open Research's member management and find the prepared existing member by exact email. | The correct person is identified before access is saved. |
| 4 | Review and add the member's Research access. | The membership appears with the intended scope. |
| 5 | Open Research's delivery locations and create the prepared test delivery address. Reopen the location. | The full saved address and Department are correct. |
| 6 | Member: sign in and open Research, then its available service workspace. | The member can see the intended Department and its permitted work. |

**Done:** Record the new Department, Research membership and delivery-location references. Use Research and that delivery location in HP-06 and HP-07.
