# HP-07 — Order, fulfill and receive transportation supplies

**People:** Customer administrator; Phaeno fulfillment administrator.

**Ready before starting:** Finalized Job from HP-06, Research delivery location, one eligible configured kit size holding all three tubes and physical stock materials for a full kit.

| Step | Person and action | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Customer: open the Job → Actions → Prepare containers, select the Research delivery location and choose Order transportation kits. Review one kit of the prepared size and the saved Department delivery address. | Capacity covers the three tubes and the destination is correct. |
| 2 | Choose Confirm kit order. | One request is saved; transportation supplies and outbound delivery are included. |
| 3 | Fulfillment administrator: open Lab operations → Receipt & accession → Kit requests, then the request. Choose Prepare kits. | The request context and required size are recognizable. |
| 4 | Prepare standard kit using the approved size, tube supplier/product and shipper product. Open the kit and Register tubes by scanning its full configured capacity. | Every physical stock tube is registered; the kit becomes ready stock. |
| 5 | Print the kit label and inspect the readable number and barcode on the physical kit. Return using Back to kit request. | The physical kit and saved record identify the same container. |
| 6 | Choose Record kit shipment, select that kit, enter the controlled carrier/tracking/dispatch facts and Record dispatch after handoff. | The request is fulfilled and the kit is On the way to the saved location. |
| 7 | Customer: after delivery, open the kit receipt action and Confirm kits received for that kit. | The kit becomes Available at Research's delivery location. |

**Done:** Record request, kit, location and outbound tracking references. Continue [HP-08](08-pack-and-send-samples.md). A transportation kit supplies the Lab Job; it is distinct from the purchased Partner PSeq Kit in HP-15.
