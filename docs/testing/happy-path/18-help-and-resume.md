# HP-18 — Find help and resume the same Job

**Person:** Customer administrator working on the connected Lab Job.

**Ready before starting:** Customer session, known Job from HP-06 and published Customer help.

| Step | Action | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Open the Job and note its number and Your next step. | You can explain what is needed and who owns it. |
| 2 | Open the user menu → Documentation and enter sample shipping in Search documentation. | Relevant Customer guidance is easy to identify. |
| 3 | Open the shipping guide and read the section matching the Job's current step. | Terminology and instructions agree with the Job workspace. |
| 4 | Follow a related guide link to laboratory services, then return to the shipping guide. | Titles and navigation retain your reading context. |
| 5 | Return to Lab services and reopen the same Job. | Saved progress and the next action are unchanged and easy to find. |

**Done:** Record the guide title and whether it answered the question without assistance. Capture one wording/navigation improvement if needed.
