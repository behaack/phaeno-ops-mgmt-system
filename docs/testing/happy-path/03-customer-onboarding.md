# HP-03 — Customer access and first sign-in

**People:** Phaeno platform administrator; invited Customer organization administrator.

**Ready before starting:** Company and Contact from HP-02, controlled recipient and Research Department access to offer. Service and billing fixtures are approved for this Customer.

| Step | Person and action | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Platform administrator: open CRM → Companies → the Company → Requests → Create request. Select Online access and Customer; provide the onboarding context and submit. | One request appears in the Company's history. |
| 2 | Open the request in CRM → Requests. Review and choose Approve and enable Portal access, recording the decision. | The Company has its Customer access scope and remaining setup is visible. |
| 3 | Open Departments & services. Complete the Research Department's prepared Lab-service entitlement and readiness settings. | The selected Department shows the intended service and readiness. |
| 4 | Open Company → People, select the Contact and choose Invite to Portal. Review the email, organization-admin role and Research membership, then send. | The saved invitation displays the intended access and pending acceptance. |
| 5 | Invited administrator: open the delivered invitation in your browser profile, register/sign in and complete authenticator enrollment privately. | Your identity is authenticated and the invitation can be reviewed. |
| 6 | Review and accept the offered access. | The Portal opens for the correct Customer and Research Department. |
| 7 | Sign out, then sign back in with the account password and current authenticator code. | The same Customer workspace and permitted navigation return. |
| 8 | Platform administrator: review the accepted membership and complete the onboarding request with a short completion note. | Membership is active; the request records completed work. |

**Done:** Record Company, Department and member references. Continue [HP-04](04-department-and-member.md). Keep invitation links and authentication secrets out of results.
