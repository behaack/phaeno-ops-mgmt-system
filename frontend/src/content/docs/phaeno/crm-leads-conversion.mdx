# Leads and conversion

Use a Lead for an unqualified commercial signal that is not yet ready to become a durable Company, Contact, or Opportunity.

## Capture the Lead

Open **Leads** and select **New lead**. Choose Individual or Company and enter the required display name. A Company Lead also requires a company name. Add contact facts, source, owner, next action, and tags when known.

Do not create a Contact merely because an email address arrived, and do not create a Company merely because a web domain was observed. The Lead preserves the original signal while qualification is incomplete.

Lead statuses are:

- **New:** captured but not yet worked.
- **Working:** active follow-up has begun.
- **Qualified:** the signal is suitable for conversion.
- **Disqualified:** the signal will not proceed; the reason is retained.
- **Converted:** the selected CRM records were created or linked and the original Lead is immutable history.

Use status and search filters to find the relevant queue. **All statuses** includes converted history; select **Converted** to reopen a completed Lead and its recorded results. Save a view when the same filter supports recurring work.

## Qualify or disqualify

Open the Lead and use its **Actions** menu to edit it or change its status. Select **Start working** before active follow-up when it is still New. Qualification and disqualification require an explanation so another Phaeno user can understand the decision.

The full-width Qualification record shows only the applicable decision: qualification notes for Qualified or Converted Leads, the disqualification reason for Disqualified Leads, or a concise pending state while no decision exists.

Qualify only when the organization or person, commercial fit, and next action are understood well enough to avoid speculative records. Disqualify when follow-up should stop; record the actual commercial reason without protected scientific or patient information.

## Convert a qualified Lead

Select **Actions > Convert lead** from a Qualified Lead. Review each requested result:

- choose an existing Company or **Create company** in the **Company** dropdown; **No company** supports a Contact-only conversion
- optionally create a Contact
- optionally create an Opportunity in an active pipeline

If an existing Company is chosen, POMS links conversion to that CRM identity. **Create company** uses the Lead's recorded company name when available. If the Lead has no company name, enter the required **Company name** in the conversion modal. This names the new Company without changing the original Lead's captured details. Opportunity creation requires an existing or new Company and uses the selected or default active pipeline.

Likely Company or Contact duplicates stop unsafe creation or are returned as warnings. Review the suggested records and retry with an existing authoritative identity when appropriate. Do not alter spelling or identifying data merely to bypass duplicate detection.

Conversion records the resulting Company, Contact, and Opportunity identifiers on the original Lead. It never enables Portal access or creates a user, entitlement, order, shipment, or Lab work order.

## Correct mistakes safely

Converted and Disqualified Leads are terminal for ordinary editing. Preserve them as decision history. If commercial work later resumes, use the resulting records or create a new Lead that represents the genuinely new signal; do not rewrite the old decision.

If a save reports stale data, reload the Lead, review the newer change, and submit a deliberate update. If conversion partially appears to have succeeded, refresh the Lead and open its recorded conversion results before retrying.
