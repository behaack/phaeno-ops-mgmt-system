# Companies and Contacts

Companies and Contacts are durable relationship records. They do not represent Portal Accounts or Portal users, and changing them does not change access.

## Create and find a Company

Open **Companies** from the CRM sidebar. Search by the known organization identity and review possible duplicates before selecting **New company**. **Company name** is required. You may also record:

- website and normalized domain
- lifecycle: Target, Engaged, Active customer, Partner, Former relationship, or Other
- source, industry, employee count, phone, and postal address
- tags and a relationship summary

POMS assigns the creator as owner unless another supported owner is selected later. The Company name opens a stable, view-first workspace. Use **Edit** for commercial facts; use activities, tasks, associations, Opportunities, and Portal handoffs in their dedicated sections.

Changing a CRM lifecycle to Active customer or Partner is descriptive only. It does not create or reclassify a Portal Account, grant membership, activate services, or authorize orders.

## Create and manage a Contact

Open **Contacts** and select **New contact**. First name and last name are required. Record email, phone, job title, Phaeno owner, tags, and the communication controls that apply:

- **Unknown** means permission has not been established.
- **Permitted** means the recorded commercial basis permits contact.
- **Opted out** records a preference not to receive the applicable communication.
- **Do not contact** is the strongest operational stop.

Record the lawful-contact basis and communication notes when known. These fields are commercial compliance context, not legal advice and not a substitute for any required campaign-specific consent process.

A Contact is not a Portal user. Email equality does not link identities, issue an invitation, or grant access.

## Associate Contacts and Companies

Open the Company workspace and use the Contacts area to associate an existing Contact. Record the relationship role, whether this is the Contact's primary Company, and the effective-from date. A Contact may be associated with more than one Company.

When a relationship ends, edit the association and record an effective-to date. Do not delete the Contact or overwrite the former Company simply to hide historical employment. POMS retains the effective-dated relationship.

## Deactivate, reactivate, and merge

Deactivate a Company or Contact when it should leave active directories while its history remains available. Reactivation restores the same CRM identity. Neither action changes a linked Portal Account or user.

Use **Merge** only for a confirmed duplicate:

1. Open the duplicate record that should be retired.
2. Choose the record that should remain authoritative.
3. Record a required merge reason.
4. Review the consequence and confirm.

POMS retains the source as an inactive alias, moves supported relationships and history, and writes a permanent merge record. Ordinary CRM UI does not undo a merge. If the records describe different legal entities or people, do not merge them; correct their fields or effective-dated relationships instead.

## Complete required custom data

Active custom fields configured for Company or Contact appear in the record workspace. Option fields accept only configured choices. A missing required value is shown on the record and contributes to CRM Home data-quality warnings. Complete the value on the authoritative record rather than suppressing the warning with unrelated text.
