# Companies and People

Companies and Contacts are durable relationship records. The Company is also the canonical Customer, Partner, or Prospect identity. A Company does not become accessible to external users until Portal access is reviewed and enabled; a Contact never becomes a Portal user automatically.

Commercial staff can maintain identity, ownership, Company relationships, Sales, and ordinary Activity. Company People shows Contact relationships for Commercial staff. Portal identities, invitations, memberships, Departments & services, Requests, merge, and Company/Contact deactivation or reactivation are administrator-only.

## Create and find a Company

Open **Companies** from the CRM sidebar. Search by the known organization identity and review possible duplicates before selecting **New company**. **Company name** is required; Website and relationship stage remain visible for quick entry. Open **Additional details** only when you need to record:

- an explicit normalized domain; when left blank, POMS derives it from Website
- source, industry, employee count, phone, and postal address
- tags and a relationship summary

Company, Contact, Lead, Opportunity, and Task lists show total counts and pages. Searches update after a short pause; **Clear all** resets active filters. Opening a record and using its Back link retains the list search, filters, and page. Company sections remain in the URL.

POMS assigns the creator as owner unless another supported owner is selected later. The Company name opens a stable, view-first workspace. Use **Overview** for the Company profile and custom data, **People** for Contacts and Portal identity/access state, **Sales** for Opportunities, **Departments & services** for approved operational access and configuration, **Requests** for request creation and history, and **Activity** for the commercial timeline and Tasks. Use CRM's central **Requests** queue for approve, decline, recovery, application, and completion decisions.

Changing a CRM lifecycle to Active customer or Partner is descriptive only. It does not enable or reclassify Portal access, grant membership, activate services, or authorize orders.

Company and Contact editors keep your entered details when the record refreshes in the background. Company ownership and deactivate/reactivate confirmations also retain the record you reviewed. If saving reports that the record changed, your entries remain available; review the current record before making another attempt. Closing and reopening an editor starts from the latest loaded details.

## Create and manage a Contact

From a Company’s **People** section, select **New person** to create the Contact and its Company association together. The global **Contacts** directory also provides **New contact** when no Company context is available. First name and last name are required. Record email, phone, Phaeno owner, and tags. **Sales and marketing outreach** shows a separate decision:

- **Not established** means staff have not recorded a supported outreach decision.
- **Allowed** means staff have recorded the supporting source, evidence date, and explanation of the outreach covered for this email address.
- **Suppressed** records an unsubscribe, direct request to stop, or internal restriction.

Select **Record or update outreach decision** only when making that decision. Choose its status and supporting source, enter the evidence date and an explanation of its scope, and give a reason for suppression. Saving an email or other profile detail does not establish permission. Changing an Allowed email resets the decision to Not established unless you explicitly record a new decision for the replacement address. Cancelling an edited draft asks whether to discard it; a failed save retains your entries.

The Contact workspace and directory show the current outreach status. The Activity timeline retains each changed decision, its previous and new evidence, and the staff member and time. System history cannot be edited or deactivated. Contact exports include the status and evidence. Earlier Opted out and Do not contact values appear as Suppressed. Earlier Permitted values need evidence review before appearing as Allowed; historical values and contact-basis text remain retained.

These decisions are recorded for staff reference. CRM does not currently send sales or marketing outreach, and this setting does not automatically block messages sent through other tools. Staff must check the current decision before using an external sending tool. Requested Portal invitations, order updates, results, and security messages are managed separately. Requesting a Portal invitation does not grant sales or marketing permission.

A Contact is not a Portal user. Email equality does not link identities, issue an invitation, or grant access.

From a Company’s **People** section, use **Invite to Portal** for a reviewed Contact and choose Organization-administrator or explicit Department access. **Manage access** provides role changes, Department access, membership deactivation, and invitation resend/revoke. Successful acceptance creates the Portal membership and the explicit Contact/User identity link. If POMS finds an existing Portal user with the same email, it shows **Identity review**; select **Review and link**, verify that both records identify the same person, and record the reason. Never treat the suggestion as proof. Unlinked Portal users and invitations remain visible until staff intentionally resolve them.

The **Invite to Portal** dialog shows the reviewed Contact and Company as a recipient summary. Under **Access after acceptance**, review the role descriptions and selected Departments; the indented administrator option applies only to its Department. **Send invitation** sends the email invitation. It does not immediately grant Portal access. For a Member, selecting no Department disables Send invitation and immediately explains that at least one Department is required.

## Associate Contacts and Companies

Open either record workspace to create the association: use **Add existing person** in the Company's People section to search incrementally for an existing Contact, or use **Associate** in the Contact's Company relationships area to search incrementally for an existing Company. Record the Contact's free-form job title at that Company and choose the relationship role—Decision maker, Scientific lead, Procurement, Executive sponsor, Champion, Influencer, Technical evaluator, or Other—then record whether this is the Contact's primary Company and the effective-from date. A Contact may be associated with more than one Company, with a different title and relationship role at each.

Job title and relationship role are intentionally different. **Job title** is the person's organization-specific position, such as Director of Genomics, and remains free-form. **Relationship role** describes how the person participates in the commercial relationship and uses the controlled choices above. The Contacts directory displays the title and Company from the active primary relationship.

Each relationship remains visible from the Company's **People** section and the Contact's **Company relationships** card. When a title, role, primary designation, or effective date changes, edit the Company relationship rather than the Contact's identity. When a relationship ends, record an effective-to date. Do not delete the Contact or overwrite the former Company simply to hide historical employment. POMS retains the effective-dated relationship and marks ended rows consistently in both workspaces.

## Deactivate, reactivate, and merge

Deactivate a Company or Contact when it should leave active directories while its history remains available. Reactivation restores the same CRM identity. Deactivating a Company suspends its attached Portal access scope; reactivating restores it. Contact lifecycle actions do not change Portal users.

On the Contact workspace, **Deactivate** or **Reactivate** opens a confirmation naming the Contact. Review it before confirming; **Cancel** leaves the Contact unchanged. If the change fails, the dialog shows the error. Manage this person's Portal membership and invitations separately from Company **People**.

Use **Merge** only for a confirmed duplicate:

1. Open the duplicate record that should be retired.
2. Search the active Company or Contact directory and choose the record that should remain authoritative. The duplicate you opened is excluded from the choices.
3. Record a required merge reason.
4. Review the consequence and confirm.

The merge dialog names the record being retired. A failed search offers **Retry**; a failed merge keeps the selected target and reason for review. Leaving an edited merge draft asks whether to discard the choices, and the dialog stays open while a merge is being processed. If another user changes the source record, reload and review the current version before submitting again.

POMS retains the source as an inactive alias, moves supported relationships and history, and writes a permanent merge record. If only one Company has Portal access, that access scope follows the surviving Company. POMS blocks a merge when both Companies have Portal access because choosing the surviving tenant requires an explicit data decision. Ordinary CRM UI does not undo a merge. If the records describe different legal entities or people, do not merge them; correct their fields or effective-dated relationships instead.

Contact merging preserves suppression if either record is suppressed. It does not transfer an Allowed decision from the retired Contact to a different surviving email address.

## Complete required custom data

Active custom fields configured for Company or Contact appear in the record workspace; Company custom fields are under **Overview**. Option fields accept only configured choices. A missing required value is shown on the record and contributes to CRM Home data-quality warnings. Complete the value on the authoritative record rather than suppressing the warning with unrelated text.

## Create an Opportunity from its Company

Open **Sales** and select **New opportunity**. The shared Opportunity form retains this Company, so you can capture the pursuit without leaving its workspace. The resulting record appears in Company Sales and the global Opportunity list.
