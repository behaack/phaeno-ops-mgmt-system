# Phaeno operations guide

The Phaeno organization workspace is the operating surface for first-party customer relationship management, organization administration, data provisioning, Customer and Partner order work, laboratory execution, public Website intake, commercial configuration, and integration recovery.

## Navigate workspaces and guides

The POMS dashboard, CRM, Lab operations, Order operations, Data provisioning, and Documentation use a shared sidebar anchored beneath the toolbar at the far-left edge of the screen. Its header identifies the current workspace and, on wider screens, provides the pin or unpin icon. The sidebar is pinned by default, leaves a centered page in place when the left margin has room, and reflows the page only to prevent overlap. Pointer users can preview an unpinned sidebar by approaching the edge; keyboard and touch users open the same non-modal rail from the tab.

Documentation automatically lists only the guides for the current organization, with an icon identifying each topic. Data provisioning, Order operations, and Laboratory operations expand to show an overview and detailed workflow guides. The group containing the current guide opens automatically; use its disclosure button to expand or collapse it.

## Work in the Phaeno context

Phaeno users enter the Phaeno organization context through their authenticated session. The user menu does not provide an act-as control for switching into Prospect, Customer, or Partner organizations. Manage those Companies and their access through **CRM**.

Platform capabilities come from an active administrator membership in the active Phaeno organization. Authentication-provider roles are not used for portal authorization.

## Protect your account

After your first sign-in step, connect an authenticator app and confirm the code it displays. Store the one-time backup codes separately from the authenticator device. Never send an authenticator or backup code in email, chat, support notes, or an audit description.

If both the authenticator and all backup codes are lost, contact an authorized Phaeno platform administrator. The administrator verifies your identity, resets the MFA enrollment, and revokes active sessions. You must connect a new authenticator before Portal access resumes. Email can initiate recovery but does not automatically bypass MFA.

## Find platform work

- **Dashboard** uses the sidebar to show one Order Operations, Lab Operations, Accounts, or Web Operations panel at a time. Counts summarize the work or intake represented by each panel.
- **CRM** opens the complete first-party relationship, sales, and customer-access workspace. Its sidebar groups Companies and Contacts under **Relationships**; Leads and Opportunities under **Sales**; Tasks and Requests under **Follow-up**; Reports under **Insights**; and configuration under **Administration**. The Company is the canonical Customer, Partner, or Prospect record. Its workspace separates **Overview**, **People & sales**, **Requests**, **Access & services**, and **Activity** so you can focus on one kind of work at a time. Creating a Company does not grant access. Use the central **Requests** queue to review pending requests; use the Company's **Access & services** section for approved access, users, readiness, services, and retention. Online-access approval creates only the internal tenant scope required for sign-in; product and service entitlements are managed separately, and approval never creates another customer record or a user.
- **Web Operations** is a Phaeno-administrator view of active public Website intake. Use the **Mailing List** and **Demo Requests** buttons to select one list panel at a time. Mailing List shows the newest active signups and identifies technical-brief requests; use **Unsubscribe** and confirm to remove a contact from that active list. Demo Requests shows the inquiry contact, organization, email, and submitted description; use **Mark complete** and confirm after the inquiry has been handled. Completed or unsubscribed items leave their active list immediately, but the original Website intake and action audit remain in POMS. Each panel paginates independently in groups of 10; its footer paginator appears only when the list has more than one page. These actions do not create an Account, Portal request, HubSpot contact, or operational order.
- **User management** is the durable employee-access workspace. Invite a named Phaeno user with their intended Platform administrator and additive laboratory roles, edit accepted users and their roles, deactivate or reactivate accounts, and manage pending invitations there.
- **Data provisioning** manages source samples, curated packages, grants, and governance.
- **Order operations** contains first-party CRM custom-work and Trial Project handoffs awaiting review, authorized laboratory work awaiting specimen intake, Customer lab, Partner PSeq kit, Partner assembly, and integration-recovery queues. Customer selectors display canonical CRM Company names.
- **Lab operations** contains Phaeno-only work orders for receipt, accession, protocol execution, materials and equipment, libraries and batches, outsourced sequencing, exceptions, and scientific approval. Its sidebar contains operational work rather than role administration. Access requires an effective Lab role or platform-administrator bootstrap access.
- **Order configuration** contains commercial, scientific, Partner-offering, credit, and integration settings.
- **Docs** opens the guide set for the current organization.

## Separate safe and internal information

Tenant timelines and messages must contain only tenant-safe reasons and actions. Internal notes, scientific investigation evidence, credentials, retry details, and other restricted facts stay in authorized Phaeno surfaces.

Never place passwords, OAuth credentials, webhook secrets, connection strings, protected health information, or customer file contents in notes, audit descriptions, notification messages, or accounting memo fields.

## Support users safely

Confirm the selected organization, user membership, capability, record reference, and visible status before changing data. Use the Prospect, Customer, or Partner documentation view to understand what the caller can see. Do not ask users to share credentials or authentication tokens.

## Search and browse guides

Use **Search documentation** at the top of Docs to find a guide or a matching section. Results update after a short pause; use at least two characters. Try “scientific approval” or “Department administrator”.

Choose **Filters** to narrow results by Topic, Workflow, or Guide type. **Clear filters** removes those selections. **Browse by topic** opens guides for a topic without requiring search words. Workflow links and **Related guides** at the end of an article provide other ways to find relevant instructions.

Select a result title to open its matching section. Your browser’s Back action restores the search and filters. Search uses the guides for your current organization; it does not include the public Website or operational records. A guide’s instructions do not change your account permissions.

If no guides match, try fewer words or clear the filters. If search is temporarily unavailable, use **Try again** or **Browse all guides**. A documentation update may require **Refresh page**; guide browsing remains available while the update finishes.
