# PSeq billing, cash, and result release

POMS owns operational accounts receivable for PSeq Lab Service. Result release is scientifically governed and never gated by payment balance or credit. Partner PSeq Kit and Partner data-assembly payment/release rules remain unchanged.

## Configure and issue PSeq documents

- Record billing contact/address and Net 30 terms by default.
- Record `Taxable`, `Exempt`, or `NonTaxable`, including the approved rate or exemption evidence, then obtain Finance approval before quoting.
- Issue the POMS quote only after full operational readiness. Its billing, tax, terms, currency, and configuration version are immutable snapshots.
- Job completion idempotently creates one numbered invoice and immutable PDF from the accepted quote. Due date is completion plus the snapshotted terms.

POMS calculates the approved tax rate without a tax engine. Never edit an issued invoice; use an append-only credit, debit, or write-off adjustment.

## Release PSeq results

A Customer result may be published only when the specific sample package is complete, checksummed, malware-clean, and scientifically approved. The Result Release Manager releases or withdraws a version. Other samples may remain in progress. Do not consult invoice balance or credit status for this decision.

Corrections create a new package, scientific approval, and release version. Preserve notification, Customer download, retention warning/cutoff/grace/deletion, withdrawal, and authorized reissue evidence.

## Record and reconcile cash

Manual receipt entry requires payer, amount, USD currency, received date, method, bank reference, evidence, and external ID. CSV intake is preview-only until explicit confirmation and must include source, external ID, date, amount, currency, payer, reference, and memo.

Matching suggestions never apply cash. Allocate explicitly so partial, one-receipt/many-invoice, many-receipt/one-invoice, overpayment, and unapplied cash remain visible. Reversals retain actor and reason. A Cash Operator submits a balanced reconciliation; a different Cash Reconciler who contributed none of its activity approves the immutable closeout report.

## Apply Partner assembly release gates

An approved output release must reference the accepted input revision and active assembly profile. Its accounting source record is created at output approval. A Partner with approved assembly credit may receive the output under its terms; otherwise the files remain on payment hold until a separately approved payment-confirmation workflow exists.

## Diagnose a blocked release

Review package completeness, checksum/scan state, scientific approval, release decision, notification, and retention independently from invoice/receipt/allocation/reconciliation. Never bypass a failed package gate by attaching a file manually or changing an unrelated workflow status. Use the owned attention queues for overdue invoices, unapplied cash, and reconciliation differences.
