# PSeq accounts receivable and result release

POMS owns PSeq Lab Service billing snapshots, invoices, receipts, allocations,
reconciliation, and governed result-release metadata. Result release is never
gated by invoice balance, credit, or payment. Partner PSeq Kit and Data Assembly
continue to use the manual accounting-source and payment-hold workflow.

## Configure and freeze PSeq billing

Before PSeq quote issuance, a Billing Operator records the billing contact and
address, payment terms (default 30 days), and effective **Taxable**, **Exempt**,
or **NonTaxable** decision. Finance approval, date, notes, and the approved rate
or exemption evidence are required. Quote issuance freezes that configuration
version and calculates USD tax without an external tax engine.

Job completion issues one idempotent numbered invoice from the accepted quote,
with due date equal to completion plus the frozen terms. The immutable invoice
PDF is visible to Finance and the owning Customer. Older manual PSeq billing
rows remain labeled **Legacy billing source - Finance review required** and are
not converted into invoices or paid state.

## Record cash and reconcile

A Cash Operator records a USD receipt with payer, received date, method, bank
reference, evidence reference, external ID, and memo. CSV imports must be
previewed and error-free before confirmation. Matching may suggest invoices but
never allocates automatically. Partial and many-to-many allocations, unapplied
cash, reversals, credits, debits, and write-offs retain their history.

A Cash Reconciler who did not record or alter the batch's cash activity approves
the reconciliation and immutable closeout report. Use aging, open invoice,
receipt, unapplied-cash, and reconciliation reports for active operations.

## Release governed PSeq results

A PSeq package may advance only when the provider-authenticated pipeline has
registered its immutable manifest and every final artifact has a valid checksum
and clean malware scan. A Scientific Reviewer pins and approves the exact
package/version. A Result Release Manager then controls Customer visibility per
sample. Do not check credit, invoice status, balance, aging, or receipt state as
a release gate.

Corrections register a new package/version that points to the package it
corrects. Withdrawal preserves the package, reason, actor, time, and evidence.
The current UI/API exposes governed metadata and release control but not result
bytes or outbound result-available email; automatic retention processing records
warning/cutoff/grace evidence and attention without deleting bytes.

## Keep Partner rules separate

Partner assembly output still requires its accepted input/profile, accounting
source, and existing credit/payment release rule. Partner reagent shipments
still create accounting-source rows. Do not apply native PSeq AR or the
payment-independent PSeq release rule to those Partner workflows.
