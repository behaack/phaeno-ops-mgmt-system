# PSeq billing, cash, and result release

POMS owns operational accounts receivable for PSeq Lab Service. Result release is scientifically governed and never gated by payment balance or credit. Partner PSeq Kit and Partner data-assembly payment/release rules remain unchanged.

## Find the Finance record

Finance actions depend on your assigned responsibility: Billing Operators maintain billing and invoice adjustments, Cash Operators record and allocate receipts and submit reconciliations, and Cash Reconcilers independently approve reconciliations. Result Release Manager and file-administration authority remain separate.

Open **Order Ops → Finance**. Choose **Invoices and aging**, **Receipts**, **Customer billing**, **Import receipts**, or **Reconciliation**. Open a record by its invoice, receipt, Customer or reconciliation number. The detail shows its current state and permitted actions. Return to Finance to keep the previous Customer filter and section. The Customer filter narrows the displayed records; aging summarizes all Customers, and **Export all invoices** and **Export all receipts** include all Customers.

If a section or its supporting Customer or aging information cannot load, use its **Retry** action. Other available Finance sections remain usable. A new receipt or import needs the Customer choices to load; a new reconciliation also needs its eligible receipts.

An open adjustment, allocation, or reversal keeps the invoice and receipt information you reviewed. If another change prevents saving, the dialog automatically loads the current record and preserves your entries. Review the updated status and balances, then choose **Use reviewed record**, check your entries and save again. If that refresh fails, use **Retry current record**. An unavailable invoice must be reselected, and a receipt with no available amount cannot be allocated.

## Configure and issue PSeq documents

- Record billing contact/address and Net 30 terms by default.
- Record `Taxable`, `Exempt`, or `NonTaxable`, including the approved rate or exemption evidence. Finance approval is required before the tax determination can be used.
- If the profile is complete and approved at quote issuance, POMS includes calculated tax and freezes billing, tax, terms, currency, and configuration version in the quote. Zero calculated tax is valid.
- If the profile is not ready, issue an explicitly pre-tax quote. Complete and approve the profile before invoicing; POMS then calculates tax and freezes the current billing terms in the invoice.
- Job completion idempotently creates one numbered invoice and immutable PDF. Due date is completion plus the applicable snapshotted terms.

POMS calculates the approved tax rate without a tax engine. Never edit an issued invoice; use an append-only credit, debit, or write-off adjustment.

## Release PSeq results

Open **Order Ops → Result release**. The queue starts with **Ready for release** packages; use **Package state** to view another state. A link with a selected state preserves that filter. Open the package identified by its Customer, Job and sample. The detail connects scientific review, manifest, release, retention and receipt. A Customer result may be published only when the specific sample package is complete, checksummed, malware-clean, and scientifically approved. The Result Release Manager releases or withdraws a version. Other samples may remain in progress. Do not consult invoice balance or credit status for this decision.

Corrections create a new package, scientific approval, and release version. Preserve notification, Customer download, retention warning/cutoff/grace/deletion, withdrawal, and authorized reissue evidence.

## Review governed result retention

New PSeq releases use the effective versioned policy from **File retention**, including an active Organization override. The package and laboratory release share the same first release instant and freeze their policy and deadlines together. Existing schedules keep their original dates; do not change them by editing current configuration.

The Customer package shows full-response download completion and the frozen standard/final dates. Grace remains usable when any artifact was undownloaded at the standard deadline; later completion does not shorten grace. New download requests close at the applicable deadline even if cleanup has not run. A request-only historical download event is not completion evidence. Success saved after the standard deadline cannot cancel grace. If download timing cannot be verified, ask technical support to recover the retained timing evidence; do not recreate the release or change its dates. This timing-recovery error is separate from the Retention notices queue.

Where governed retention processing is enabled, POMS queues one warning for an incomplete package and one grace notice if grace applies. A delayed warning is skipped once all files have completed or the standard deadline has passed. Queued notices use current active Organization admins; they contain the package reference, deadline, and an authenticated Portal link.

Use **Owned attention queues → Retention notices** to find urgent missing-recipient or delivery failures. Review the organization and administrator access, correct delivery configuration when needed, then retry the existing record in the notification workspace. Recreating a release or notification is not a recovery step. A failure can reopen the Operations item until the underlying notice is recovered. Deadlines remain unchanged.

Physical cleanup remains separately controlled. A closed download window is not proof of byte deletion, and a queued message is not proof of receipt. Access withdrawal or deactivation stops a governed transfer; restoring access does not resume that transfer.

## Record and reconcile cash

In **Receipts**, choose **Record receipt**. Enter Customer, payer, amount, received date, payment method, bank reference and external reference. Attach evidence as PDF, PNG, JPEG or text, up to 10 MB. The file must pass the configured scanner before the receipt is recorded; an unavailable scanner leaves the receipt unsaved and preserves your entries. An optional note adds context. Open a saved receipt to download its evidence, allocate it to a same-Customer invoice, or reverse it when no allocation remains.

In **Import receipts**, select Customer and source, then choose a CSV file or paste its contents. Required headers are source, external_id, date, amount, currency, payer, reference and memo. **Preview import** validates the file. Review the frozen Customer, source, rows and total, then confirm. Only the operator who reviewed that preview can confirm it. If another operator already owns the file review, ask them to complete it; do not change the file identity to bypass that ownership. If the Customer, totals, or saved version changed, review a fresh preview before confirmation. **Change input** returns to editing and requires a fresh preview. An earlier response cannot replace a preview for changed input. Repeating your unconfirmed preview resumes the same batch; confirmed files cannot be imported twice. Imports create unapplied receipts and retain their source evidence.

Matching suggestions never apply cash. Allocate explicitly so partial, one-receipt/many-invoice, many-receipt/one-invoice, overpayment, and unapplied cash remain visible. Reversals retain actor and reason. A Cash Operator submits a balanced reconciliation; a different Cash Reconciler who contributed none of its activity approves the immutable closeout report.

In **Allocate to invoice**, search by invoice number and use **Next invoices** or **Previous invoices** to find more open invoices for the same Customer. The selected invoice and its reviewed balance remain visible while you browse other results. Selecting or searching never records an allocation.

The receipt's **Allocation history** shows each allocation, its invoice, amount, actor and any reversal. Use **Reverse allocation** on an active allocation, review its receipt and invoice, and enter the correction reason. Reversal restores the receipt's unapplied amount and the invoice's outstanding balance while preserving the original allocation. A fully reversed allocation cannot be reversed again. **Reverse receipt** remains available only when no allocation remains.

In **Reconciliation**, open the batch number to review its selected receipts and other contributing records. Before submission, a Cash Operator may choose **Edit draft** to correct the period end, bank total or selected receipts, or **Cancel draft** to retain an unused batch as cancelled. Both actions require a reason and the current version. Editing recalculates totals and retains the before-and-after selections. **Draft change history** shows the actor, reason and changes to the period and totals. Balancing or cancelling the draft resolves its open reconciliation-difference attention item. Cancellation does not reverse receipts or allocations.

Submit the batch only when balanced. Submitted and approved batches cannot be edited or cancelled through these draft actions. Independent approval excludes the creator, submitter, contributing actors and every recorded draft editor wherever dual control is enforced. A conflict preserves your entries: review the refreshed batch before applying them. The immutable closeout remains the evidence for the approved batch.

## Apply Partner assembly release gates

An approved output release must reference the accepted input revision and active assembly profile. Its accounting source record is created at output approval. A Partner with approved assembly credit may receive the output under its terms; otherwise the files remain on payment hold until a separately approved payment-confirmation workflow exists.

## Diagnose a blocked release

Review package completeness, checksum/scan state, scientific approval, release decision, notification, and retention independently from invoice/receipt/allocation/reconciliation. Never bypass a failed package gate by attaching a file manually or changing an unrelated workflow status. Use the owned attention queues for overdue invoices, unapplied cash, and reconciliation differences.


## Support general Lab and Assembly downloads

Where general retention enforcement is enabled, individual files and whole ZIPs
use the release's frozen policy and verified completion. A closed window prevents
new requests, while an admitted transfer can finish within its time limit. Current
file withdrawal, scan failure, Organization/Department access removal, or release
withdrawal stops active downloads. A failed ZIP does not count its files from that
attempt. Partner payment-release rules still apply.

Recover missing timing evidence through technical support without recreating the
release or changing dates. General download enforcement does not itself schedule
warning/grace emails or activate storage cleanup. Preserve the original contract
for undated historical releases.

Where general Lab/Assembly retention processing is enabled, the same warning,
grace, and **Retention notices** recovery workflow applies to those releases.
The Lab notice opens the laboratory job; the Assembly notice opens its assembly
request. Recipients are current active Organization admins, including admins
appointed after the notice was queued. Department-only members and notification
routing aliases are not recipients. Retry the existing failed notification after
correcting the cause, then review the Operations item. Preserve the original
release and dates. General and governed PSeq notice processing are activated
separately; a disabled process does not create or dispatch its scheduled notices.

Released package schedules link to retained receipts. Receipts preserve file
manifests, checksums, deadline/completion/deletion facts and reissue lineage after
byte deletion. For preservation, quarantine, or linking a newly approved reissue,
use [Configuration and recovery](/docs/phaeno/configuration-and-recovery).
