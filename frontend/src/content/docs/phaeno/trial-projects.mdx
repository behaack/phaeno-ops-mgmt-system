# Operate Trial Projects

Trial Projects provide no-charge, closed-ended PSeq evaluations of extracted RNA. POMS owns the scientific scope, approvals, sample authorization, laboratory execution, and release. Business Development, Commercial leadership and Platform administrators can create the Trial directly. Business Development submits scope for leadership review; Commercial leadership and Platform administrators approve a completed scope when they submit it. CRM retains the Company and commercial follow-up. Trial work creates no order, quote, invoice, payment gate, or QuickBooks transaction.

**For Research Use Only. Not for use in diagnostic procedures.** Never request PHI. Use coded sample references, and keep suspected prohibited information out of ordinary notes, notifications, and CRM summaries. Place affected Trial work on hold and follow the authorized incident process when prohibited information is suspected.

## Find Trial projects

Open **Order ops**, then choose **Trial projects** under **LAB SERVICES** in the workspace sidebar. Search by Trial number or company, filter by status or Sales owner, and use **Clear all** to reset the list. An empty list is shown separately from a search with no matches. Select a Trial number to open its workspace; existing Trial links continue to work.

## Create a Trial and define scope

An administrator assigns **Business Development** to Sales representatives and **Commercial leadership** to users who decide Trial scopes in Phaeno user management. Platform administrators can also create Trials without adding either role. These business roles remain separate from Commercial operator and laboratory/result-release roles.

A Business Development, Commercial leadership or Platform administrator user opens **Trial projects → Create Trial**, searches for the Company by name or domain, and selects a result. With multiple active Departments, choose the Department explicitly; with one, POMS selects it automatically. A Company with no Departments can start a draft, but an administrator must prepare its Prospect access and Department before scope submission. No Opportunity or Company request is required. The creator is recorded as the Sales owner.

Enter a **Trial name**, **Objective / Description**, and **Sample type**. Use **Source / quantity** as for orders: enter each biological source and its positive whole-number **Samples** quantity. Describe the organism/species and tissue or cell type without personal identifiers. Use **Add source** or the row's remove control for as many sources as needed; each source must be distinct. **Total samples** is calculated from the quantities. Enter the **Submission opens** and **Submission closes** dates. The closing date is included; a single-day window is allowed. Select **Create Trial project** to save those details as the initial shared scope draft and open its workspace. A draft grants no approved allowance, Prospect access, samples or laboratory authorization. Existing CRM request links may still open their historical Trial.

Select **Resume draft** to continue with the saved name, Objective / Description, sample type, source quantities and submission dates. The scope page calculates the original sample allowance and also captures the current production PSeq workflow, active PSeq analysis definitions, and versioned deliverables. FASTQ, FASTA, and BAM are configurable initial defaults. Select the deliverables required for this evaluation; approved scopes preserve the selected definitions and versions, including the sample type and source quantities.

Record RNA submission instructions, success criteria, Prospect terms, residual-material retention and disposition, and internal estimated retail value and anticipated cost. Return arrangements require destination, handling, and shipping payer. They become immutable after the first sample submission. Internal costs never appear in the Prospect view.

Choose **Save draft** to keep unfinished scope work and return to the Trial. Drafts may omit fields required for approval, including dates, scientific selections, return arrangements, terms, and the revision reason. Entered numbers and text still need to be valid. The draft is shared with authorized Phaeno Trial staff; the workspace shows who last saved it and when. Choose **Resume draft** to continue. Prospect users cannot see the draft. Saving does not change the current scope, existing approvals or acceptance, or authorize samples or laboratory work.

When all required fields and scientific choices are complete, Business Development selects **Submit scope for approval**. Commercial leadership and Platform administrators select **Approve and submit scope**; this records their approval immediately and moves the scope to Prospect acceptance without another decision. Both actions create a scope revision and clear the saved draft. An amendment needs a new approval and renewed Prospect acceptance. A privileged submitter can approve the amendment in the same submission; other staff submit it for leadership review. A failed save or submission preserves your entries; if someone else changed the Trial or shared draft, reload the current record and review it before retrying. Scope planning remains available during a hold, but the hold still blocks approval and operational work. Closed Trials cannot save or submit scope changes.

## Commercial leadership approval

After **Submit scope for approval**, a user with active **Commercial leadership** opens the Trial and selects **Actions → Record decision**. Choose **Approve**, **Request changes**, or **Decline**, and record a reason. One leadership approval moves the current scope to Prospect acceptance. Scientific Operations approval is not required for Trial scope.

Request changes requires a fresh scope revision; decline closes the Trial. Removing Commercial leadership prevents further leadership decisions or direct approval on submission, unless the user independently retains Platform administrator access. Historical named Commercial approval authority does not grant these permissions. Platform administrators approve scope through **Approve and submit scope**; the separate **Record decision** action remains for Commercial leadership. Amendments require a new approval and renewed Prospect acceptance, through direct approval on submission or leadership review as permitted. Previously submitted scopes with a recorded decision must be resubmitted rather than overwriting history.

**Order settings → Trial configuration** retains named Scientific Operations authorities for laboratory controls, residual-material decisions, and the deliverable catalog. Laboratory processing, scientific result review, and result release retain their own permissions.

## Execute the approved work

A Prospect organization administrator or an administrator of the assigned Department accepts the approved RUO/no-PHI terms and submits samples during the agreed window. **Submit samples** uses the approved sample type and lets the administrator choose each sample's approved biological source and a shipping destination. Submitting the roster creates one authorization and shipment. Each source's quantity is enforced across submissions; spare slots for a different source cannot be used instead. A source change requires a revised scope with approval and renewed acceptance; quantities cannot be reduced below originals already submitted. A changed or unavailable sample type also requires scope review. The form checks extracted-RNA metadata, approved analysis inputs, configured quantity units and limits, and active shipping instructions, and identifies concentration and other required inputs. Trials then use the shared order workflow for packing, dispatch, physical receipt, laboratory progress and governed result release, without billing gates.

Changed forms ask before discarding entries through Cancel, Escape, or browser navigation. Reloading the browser also warns about unsaved changes. During a save or explicit reload, editing, repeat actions, dismissal, and navigation are blocked until the operation finishes. Required fields in approval, configuration, and other short action dialogs show an issue when you leave them, and clear that issue as you correct it. A failed Trial configuration refresh preserves an open assignment or catalog draft; **Retry configuration** reloads the configuration. **Reload current Trial; keep my entries** refreshes scope-dependent choices and preserves entries for review, including when the reload fails; a changed scope requires a fresh acceptance confirmation.

The shared Lab Operations provider creates Trial-authorized work pinned to the approved workflow version. Existing registered-tube, packet, receipt, accession, protocol, QC, sequencing, and scientific-review controls apply. Use **Shipping and packets** and each sample's **Open laboratory work** link. Trial holds and approval changes stop scientific progress and shipping mutations; custody facts and exception handling remain recordable.

**Schedule estimate** communicates an estimate without changing scope. **Authorize replacement** records the failed sample, responsibility, reason, and approving authority. It permits exactly one replacement and never increases the frozen original allowance. Prospect-caused problems need the same explicit exception approval.

## Review and release results

The governed PSeq pipeline supplies immutable output packages tied to the Trial, submitted sample, and Lab work order. Complete checksums and clean scans precede scientific review. Scientific approval and release retain the existing PSeq separation of duties. A release manager selects one scientifically approved package per sample, with every frozen deliverable and no unapproved output role.

Choose **Partial** when only some approved results are ready. Choose **Complete** only when the full approved package for every submitted sample, including approved replacements, is available. A complete release closes the Trial and freezes the shared global/organization retention policy. No payment or credit gate applies.

The result archive includes a Trial manifest with the exact RUO statement, scope revision, sample/file lineage, and checksums. After complete release, earlier partial archives remain historical and direct members to the current complete package. Download controls show progress and availability, including closed retention access, and refresh after transfer attempts. Internal downloads do not satisfy the organization's retention condition. **Retention and receipt** supports the shared warning, grace, hold, deletion, and reissue workflow. A complete reissue after byte deletion requires newly transferred, scientifically approved packages for the same Trial samples and retains the original closure/material dates.

Release and physical shipping remain subject to configured operational readiness. A visible workflow or a passing software test does not activate real storage, scanning, automated byte deletion, or validate laboratory packing instructions.

## Close and follow up

Successful completion is recorded by complete result release. Otherwise use **Close Trial** with the appropriate outcome and a Prospect-safe reason. Closure blocks new submissions and requests cancellation of unfinished Lab authorizations while preserving custody and history.

Residual RNA retention starts at terminal closure. **Record material disposition** records actual exhaustion, return, or destruction once under the frozen terms; a due date does not record physical destruction automatically. A hold blocks disposition.

**Commercial outcome** is separate: schedule owned and dated follow-up, record closed without conversion, or record conversion only after the explicit CRM relationship workflow changes the same organization to Customer or Partner. Conversion never extends Trial file deadlines or starts new Trial work. Use the existing organization closeout workflow for any deliberate deactivation after reviewing other active Trials, grants, and relationships.

Trial milestones publish to CRM as relationship-safe summaries and a deep link. Scientific details and sample identifiers stay in the Trial/Lab workspaces. Failed publication retries without blocking Trial operations; the Trial workspace shows pending milestones and provides **Retry CRM publication**.

## Close Prospect access

After all Trial and commercial evaluation work is closed without conversion, a platform administrator can use **Close Prospect access** in the Trial. The action requires a reason and checks all Company Trials, holds, open Opportunities, data grants, and service access. Resolve those relationships before retrying. This deactivates the Company and Prospect organization while preserving Trial history and existing retention deadlines. Byte deletion never performs this deactivation automatically.
