# How to onboard a Customer

Use this walkthrough to take a Company from CRM setup to accepted online access
and an approved, usable **PSeq Lab Service** permission. It follows two separate
Company requests: **Online access** and **Products and services**. Keep their
request numbers so you can verify both outcomes.

## Before you start

Work in the Phaeno organization. A platform administrator creates and reviews
Company access/service requests and manages invitations. Commercial staff can
prepare the Company and contact information within their permissions; involve an
administrator for the controlled access steps. Finance staff with billing access
own the billing profile and tax approval. The invited Customer administrator
accepts their own access.

Have the correct Company, intended administrator's name and email, Department
scope, approved service and dates, billing contact/address, agreed payment terms
and Finance's tax decision. Check for existing Companies, people, access and
service permissions before adding records.

Illustrations were refreshed and reviewed on October 24, 2026. They show focused
parts of the workflow; surrounding navigation may be outside the image.

Every screenshot below renders actual application components with explicitly
fictional demonstration records. The numbered markers correspond to the written
explanations immediately below each image. No invitation or email was sent and
no Customer records were changed to make these illustrations. Example names,
dates, terms and tax choices are illustrations, not values to copy into real work.

## 1. Prepare the Company, people and Departments

Open **CRM → Companies**, search for the Company, and open its name. Use **New
company** only when it does not already exist. Save its identity and commercial
context, then open **People**.

![Fictional Company People workspace showing Company identity, New person, an existing contact, Departments and Requests.](/docs/phaeno/screenshots/onboarding-20261015/01-company.jpg "Company setup is available before online access is enabled.")

1. **Company identity:** verify the active Company and its current Portal access
   status. Creating a CRM Company alone grants no sign-in or service access.
2. **New person:** add the intended contact when missing, including the correct
   name and email. Use **Add existing person** to associate an existing contact.
3. **Person record:** review the Company relationship and intended administrator.
   A CRM Contact is not automatically a Portal user.
4. **Departments:** prepare the operational units and settings before requesting
   online access; no access request is required to add a Department.
5. **Requests:** this Company tab is where the two requests in this guide begin.

In **Departments**, review existing units or choose **Add department**.

![Add department form for the fictional Research Department, showing name, description, inherited settings guidance and Add department.](/docs/phaeno/screenshots/onboarding-20261015/01-department.jpg "Department setup does not send invitations or enable Portal access.")

1. **Name:** enter a recognizable operational Department name.
2. **Description:** record its purpose when helpful. The reference is assigned
   automatically on save.
3. **Optional settings:** leave overrides blank to use applicable Company/system
   defaults; enter only reviewed Department-specific instructions.
4. **Add department:** save the unit and review its record. Later access approval
   reuses the Company's setup and preserves its Department identities/settings.

## 2. Request Customer online access

Open **Company → Requests → Create request**.

![Create Company request form with online access, Onboarding, Customer relationship, a fictional summary and Create pending request.](/docs/phaeno/screenshots/onboarding-20261015/02-access-request.jpg "This request enables the Company's online-access scope; services are requested separately.")

1. **What does this Company need?:** select **Enable or change online access**.
2. **Request type:** choose **Onboarding** for new Customer access.
3. **Requested relationship:** choose **Customer**. If an existing Prospect must
   become a Customer, use its reviewed relationship-change process rather than
   creating a second Company or a duplicate onboarding request.
4. **Summary:** optionally explain the business purpose and intended contact.
   Keep credentials, patient identifiers and scientific results out of CRM notes.
5. **Create pending request:** save one request and retain its number. It is
   awaiting a decision; no invitation, service or order is created.

If the outcome is uncertain, check the Company's request history before retrying
with another new request. For an already enabled Company, review its existing
access and use the appropriate change request.

## 3. Approve and enable the Company's access

Open **CRM → Requests → Needs decision**, identify that Company and request
number, and choose **Actions → Approve and enable access**.

![Approve and enable Portal access dialog for the fictional online-access request, with its consequence, optional note and approval action.](/docs/phaeno/screenshots/onboarding-20261015/03-access-approval.jpg "Approval enables Company access; people, services and orders remain separate.")

1. **Decision:** confirm you are approving online access for the intended Company.
2. **Consequence and request identity:** review the Company and request number.
   Approval enables the access scope with pending readiness; it does not activate
   a person, grant service permission or create an order.
3. **Approval note (optional):** add context when useful; a note is not required
   for this approval. Declining or cancelling instead requires a reason.
4. **Approve and enable access:** record the decision. Continue in
   **Approved / needs work** and follow **Work needed**.

If approval is recorded but access setup did not finish, use the request's
**Complete access enablement** recovery action. If an existing access scope is
offered for reuse, verify the named scope and preserve its users/history. Do not
create a replacement Company to work around setup failure.

## 4. Invite the approved administrator

Open **Company → People**, find the intended person, and choose **Invite to
Portal** from their actions. If they already have accepted access, review
**Manage access** instead of issuing a duplicate invitation.

![Invite Alex Example to Portal form showing the fictional recipient, Member role, Research Department, Department administrator selection and Send invitation.](/docs/phaeno/screenshots/onboarding-20261015/04-send-invitation.jpg "This example grants Department administrator access to Research only.")

1. **Recipient:** verify the name, invited email and Company before sending.
2. **Role:** choose the approved scope. An **Organization administrator** can
   manage all Departments; this is optional. **Member** restricts access to the
   selected Departments and can include Department administrator permission.
3. **Department access:** select each approved active Department.
4. **Department administrator:** enable this for the Department the person must
   administer. An ordinary Department member does not satisfy administrator
   acceptance readiness.
5. **Send invitation:** queue the invitation email. This does not activate access.

Review invitation and email-delivery status in People. Provider acceptance is
not proof that the person received or accepted the invitation. **Resend invite**
issues a renewed link and invalidates the previous link. If an invitation expired,
renew it; for a hard bounce, correct the contact through the supported revoke and
new-invitation flow. Do not ask the recipient to share passwords or sign-in codes.

## 5. Have the Customer administrator accept

The recipient opens the newest invitation email and follows its link. They review
the proposed access and follow the prompted acceptance/sign-in or account-setup
steps using the invited email, including required verification or authenticator
setup. An acceptance chosen before sign-in can finish automatically afterward
when the reviewed invitation is unchanged.

![Customer invitation acceptance review for Alex Example, showing the fictional Company, Research Department administrator access, signed-in review and Accept invitation.](/docs/phaeno/screenshots/onboarding-20261015/05-accept-invitation.jpg "Demonstration of the recipient's review after sign-in; no real invitation was accepted.")

1. **Company and recipient:** verify the invitation is for the intended Company
   and email. Use the correct account if another email is signed in.
2. **Access after acceptance:** review the Organization or Department scope and
   administrator permissions.
3. **Ready to join:** confirm the invited account is signed in. Complete missing
   name fields if requested.
4. **Accept invitation:** accept the reviewed access. Viewing the link alone
   grants nothing. If permissions changed, review the updated access and accept
   again; do not rely on an earlier review.

The recipient then enters Portal with their accepted access. Phaeno can verify the
person's active role in Company People. Department administrators remain limited
to their assigned active Departments.

## 6. Verify the online-access request completed automatically

Return to **CRM → Requests → Completed / history**.

![Completed online-access request showing Company identity, Completed status, automatic completion explanation and No service change.](/docs/phaeno/screenshots/onboarding-20261015/06-online-access-complete.jpg "Access-only onboarding completes after Company access is enabled and an eligible administrator accepts.")

1. **Completed / history:** open the completed-request view; search by Company or
   request number if needed.
2. **Completed:** verify the exact online-access request reached this state.
3. **Completed work:** review the automatic-completion explanation. Company access
   must be enabled and an Organization or active-Department administrator must
   have accepted and gained active access.
4. **No service change:** this access request did not enable a product/service.

Access-only onboarding has no manual **Complete request** action. While an
invitation is pending, it remains **Waiting for acceptance**. Expired/revoked
invitations or inactive access require attention. If active administrator access
already exists, approval can complete the request immediately. Progress normally
refreshes every 15 seconds while Requests is open and when you return; use the
displayed retry action if progress or automatic completion failed.

## 7. Request the approved product or service

Return to **Company → Requests → Create request** for a separate service request.

![Create Company request form with products and services selected, Customer relationship, P-Seq Lab Service, a fictional scope summary and Create pending request.](/docs/phaeno/screenshots/onboarding-20261015/07-service-request.jpg "For this Customer walkthrough, the requested service is PSeq Lab Service.")

1. **What does this Company need?:** choose **Add or change products and services**.
   This creates a service-change request.
2. **Requested relationship:** verify the existing Customer relationship.
3. **Requested products and services:** select the approved Customer service,
   **P-Seq Lab Service** in the picker. Customer commercial access currently uses
   PSeq Lab Service; PSeq Kit permissions belong to the Partner workflow.
4. **Summary:** describe the approved purpose, Department scope and timing so the
   reviewer can configure the correct permission.
5. **Create pending request:** save and retain this second request number. The
   service remains unavailable until its reviewed permission is saved and usable.

## 8. Approve and save the service permission

In **CRM → Requests → Needs decision**, open this request's **Actions → Approve
and set up services**.

![Approve and set up services dialog showing Research scope, effective dates, Ready to use, optional approval note and Approve and save services.](/docs/phaeno/screenshots/onboarding-20261015/08-approve-services.jpg "Approval and the dated service permission are saved together when Company access is enabled.")

1. **Applies to:** select the approved Department, or **All departments (Company
   default)** when Company-wide permission is authorized.
2. **Effective from:** enter the approved start date and time. A future start
   leaves the request waiting until that date.
3. **Effective to (optional):** enter the approved end, or leave it blank if none
   was agreed. An expired permission cannot clear current service readiness.
4. **Service configuration:** select **Ready to use** when setup is complete.
   **Pending setup** or **Blocked** keeps the service step unfinished.
5. **Approval note (optional):** retain useful decision context if needed.
6. **Approve and save services:** record the approval and permission, linked to
   this source request automatically. A usable permission does not create an order.

When a matching **Existing permission** is offered, review/update it instead of
adding an overlapping permission. If the decision was approved before Company
access existed, finish access setup, then use **Actions → Set up approved
services → Save services** on the approved request. Use that same action to finish
an older Pending/Blocked permission. Review permissions in **Company → Services
→ Entitlements**; avoid an unlinked duplicate that cannot clear this request.

## 9. Resolve the request's Work needed checklist

Open **Approved / needs work** and expand the relevant **Instructions**.

![Approved service request with the correct request number, verified Company access and service permission, remaining offering/billing blockers and expanded Finance instructions.](/docs/phaeno/screenshots/onboarding-20261015/09-work-needed.jpg "The checklist reflects saved facts. This demonstration shows examples of remaining requirements.")

1. **Approved status:** approval and completion are separate states.
2. **Request number:** follow the service request, not the completed access request.
3. **Service permission — Done:** verify the permission is current, Ready, linked
   to this request and scoped to the Company or an active Department.
4. **Shared offering setup:** a Phaeno configuration owner resolves missing shared
   service definitions; changing the Company permission alone cannot do this.
5. **Company billing:** Finance completes the named Customer-specific fields.
6. **Expanded Instructions:** follow the owner, destination, fields, save action
   and completion condition for each remaining item.

The exact blockers depend on current saved configuration. For Customer PSeq
requests, review all displayed requirements:

- **Company and people:** active Customer access, an accepted eligible
  administrator and current service permission; verify the intended Department.
- **Order settings → Service catalog:** an active, approved PSeq Lab Service
  offering using the supported sales unit. **Quote & workflow** supplies valid
  quote settings and supported sample/result workflows.
- **Samples & shipping settings:** effective Active Sample types, their usable
  Shipping procedures and kit configuration, an available Default receiving
  destination, and saved Order submission guidance.
- **More → Finance → Customer billing:** complete billing contact/address,
  agreed payment terms, effective tax decision and Finance approval.
- **Manual operational block:** resolve the recorded reason with its owner and
  have authorized staff clear the block. Completing other fields does not remove it.

Use [How to configure samples and shipping](/docs/phaeno/how-to-configure-samples-shipping)
for shared shipping setup. Customer readiness in **Company → Services** also
separates **Before starting pricing**, **Before issuing a quote** and **Before
invoicing**. Billing may be finished after order acceptance but must be ready
before invoicing; closing this service request requires its full readiness
checklist. Do not treat a started pricing draft as proof onboarding is complete.

## 10. Complete and save Customer billing and tax

Finance opens **More → Finance → Customer billing**, opens the Customer name,
then chooses **Edit billing and tax**.

![Customer billing and tax editor showing fictional contact/address, payment terms, Exempt tax decision and demonstration evidence.](/docs/phaeno/screenshots/onboarding-20261015/10-billing-setup.jpg "Use the Customer's confirmed billing facts and Finance's decision; the illustrated values are fictional.")

1. **Billing contact name:** record the billing person or team.
2. **Billing contact email:** use the confirmed billing address. A CRM contact or
   invited user does not automatically populate this profile.
3. **Invoice address:** complete Address line 1, City, State or region, Postal
   code and two-letter Country code. Address line 2 is optional. Billing and
   shipping addresses may differ.
4. **Payment terms (days):** enter the agreed whole number from 0 to 365.
5. **Tax decision:** select Finance's **Taxable**, **Exempt** or **Non-taxable**
   decision. For Taxable, enter the approved rate; do not infer a tax decision
   from the form's initial selection.
6. **Exemption evidence:** required when Exempt is selected. Record the applicable
   evidence; the demonstration text is not a valid exemption record.

Choose **Save changes**. Resolve validation errors and confirm the save before
approving tax. Every billing/tax edit resets Finance approval, including a later
correction to an already approved profile.

## 11. Approve the saved tax decision in Finance

Review the saved profile in the same **Customer billing and tax** dialog. Save
any corrections before approving.

![Lower part of the saved Customer billing and tax form, showing tax decision, evidence, Finance approval notes and Approve current tax decision.](/docs/phaeno/screenshots/onboarding-20261015/11-finance-approval.jpg "Finance approval is a separate action on the saved billing-profile version.")

1. **Saved tax decision:** verify it against the Customer's reviewed billing facts.
2. **Rate or evidence:** check the applicable approved rate or exemption evidence.
3. **Finance approval notes:** record the required basis for Finance's review.
4. **Approve current tax decision:** approve the saved version and confirm
   **Finance approved** is shown afterward. Unsaved billing edits must be saved
   first. A stale-version rejection requires reloading and reviewing the current
   facts, not repeatedly approving the older screen.

Close the dialog and return to the service request. Allow the checklist to refresh.
Billing approval does not accept a Customer quote, create an invoice or record
payment; those are separate commercial actions.

## 12. Record completion of the service request

Return to **CRM → Requests → Approved / needs work** and review the saved progress.

![Service request showing Company access, current service permission and Customer operational readiness all Done, with the Complete request action.](/docs/phaeno/screenshots/onboarding-20261015/12-ready-to-complete.jpg "Complete the service request only after its current checklist passes.")

1. **Approved status:** verify that this is the same approved service request.
2. **Company Portal access — Done:** the access scope is active.
3. **Service permission — Done:** its dates, scope, configuration and source link pass.
4. **Customer operational readiness — Done:** the required saved setup passes.
5. **All checklist items are complete:** review any **Needs review** items as well;
   manual reviews cannot be attested by an automated check.
6. **Actions → Complete request:** open the final completion dialog. If disabled,
   resolve the remaining items or retry progress verification first.

![Complete Company request dialog with the exact service request, completion consequence, optional completed-work note and Complete request button.](/docs/phaeno/screenshots/onboarding-20261015/12-record-completion.jpg "This records the completed work; it does not perform missing setup.")

1. **Complete Company request:** confirm the intended action.
2. **Company and request:** verify the service request's identity and the work
   being confirmed.
3. **Completed work (optional):** add useful closeout context; blank notes are allowed.
4. **Complete request:** record completion. POMS rechecks current prerequisites.
   If another change blocks completion, refresh, resolve it and review again.

## 13. Verify both requests and hand off to ordering

Open **Completed / history** and find the Company or retained request numbers.

![Completed request cards with the separate online-access and PSeq Lab Service requests, their Completed statuses and retained completion details.](/docs/phaeno/screenshots/onboarding-20261015/13-completed-requests.jpg "Online access and service authorization have separate completion records.")

1. **Completed / history:** verify the final history view.
2. **Search completed requests:** use the Company name or request number.
3. **Online access — Completed:** acceptance of eligible administrator access is recorded.
4. **Service change — Completed:** approved service setup and readiness are recorded.
5. **Completed work:** review the retained closeout context for the correct request.

Confirm the person's active Department/admin access in Company People, the
current permission in Services, and the Finance-approved billing profile. The
Customer is now prepared for its permitted service workflow. Follow
[How to place a Customer order](/docs/phaeno/how-to-place-customer-order) for
Draft/pricing/quote/acceptance steps; onboarding completion creates no order,
specimen, laboratory execution or scientific-data grant.

For recovery, see [Company requests and approvals](/docs/phaeno/crm-portal-handoffs),
[POMS user administration](/docs/phaeno/organization-and-user-administration),
[Customer lab authorization](/docs/phaeno/order-customer-lab-authorization) and
[Billing, cash and result release](/docs/phaeno/order-billing-payment-release).
