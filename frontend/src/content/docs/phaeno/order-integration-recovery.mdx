# Accounting and notification recovery

A failed notice or integration remains attached to the original business record with its attempts and error history. Use Company **People** for invitation access and delivery recovery, **Attention** for operational follow-up, and the owning Lab, Trial, or release record for scientific publication. Native PSeq accounts receivable is maintained in **Finance**; retained accounting connectors serve historical or non-PSeq work.

## Triage the failure

Open **Attention** for the owned cross-workflow exception or **Legacy integrations** for a retained connector/notification event. Identify the affected record, current delivery state, owner, age, attempts, next action, and tenant-visible consequence. Determine whether the local operation committed successfully before taking recovery action.

Common states include:

- **Invitation queued/failed/bounced/needs attention** while access remains separately pending, accepted, expired, or revoked;
- **Projection or scan failed** while the immutable result package remains unreleased;
- failed notification delivery after the underlying portal action already succeeded.

## Retry safely

Refresh the workflow before investigating a missing accounting row. Do not recreate the order, quote, shipment, output approval, or result completion to force another row. Use the stable source ID when reconciling repeated report downloads.

If an invitation hard-bounced, revoke and reissue to a corrected address rather than retrying it. If a result or delivery already exists, inspect that original record instead of creating another package or delivery. If a notification failed, retry it without repeating the underlying business transition.

If QuickBooks is not configured, catalog, document, or payment recovery reports the connector as unavailable and records **Needs attention**. It does not create an invoice, payment, or successful zero balance. Preserve the existing record and ask the responsible administrator to resolve the connector; repeating the request cannot configure it. Maintain the active Service catalog directly in **Order configuration → Service catalog**. Partner Assembly credit settings remain applicable to their own workflow.

## Communicate the boundary

Keep operational state, accounting-source readiness, credit, file release, and notification delivery separate. An accounting question does not undo an issued quote or scientific completion. A failed email does not reverse the order action recorded in the portal.

Escalate only after the operational record, report range, stable entry ID, and notification history have been reviewed. Include safe identifiers; never include credentials, payment data, or customer-confidential scientific detail in support notes.
