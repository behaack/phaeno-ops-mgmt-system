# Service workflows, protocol control, and execution

Lab Ops uses a controlled service workflow to arrange reusable, versioned protocols into the approved production process. Commercial orders and jobs identify the purchased service. Preparation batches and specimen attempts retain the exact workflow, stage, and protocol versions used to deliver it. A job’s historical workflow assignment does not restrict new attempts. Explicitly approved Trial workflow constraints remain part of that Trial’s scope.

## Reusable Lab steps

Open **Lab operations → Lab configurations → Lab steps** to find reusable procedures. Open a step name to view its versions and **Used by protocols**. Protocol Administrators create the identity, then choose **New version** or **Edit draft**. The dedicated editor captures instructions, typed values and their scopes, optional PDF attachments, resources, outputs, operator confirmation, roles and QC criteria.

New configurations default to batch preparation. New captured values and QC gates default to one batch entry or outcome. Choose individual tube capture or shared values with tube exceptions where the procedure requires it; barcodes remain tube-specific. Recording a step automatically includes every eligible tube; individual tubes cannot be deselected. Existing saved definitions retain their configured scopes.

Save the draft, review its configuration preview, and choose **Approve version** from its Actions menu. A different Protocol Administrator approves it. A platform administrator approving their own draft must explicitly record an override reason. Approval locks that version. A new version leaves every earlier approved version and existing protocol snapshot unchanged.

In a protocol editor, choose an exact **Approved Lab step version** and select **Add Lab step**. Its content is read-only. Set its required, optional or conditional placement and arrange the occurrences in order. Earlier occurrences remain prerequisites. Repeating a step creates a separate occurrence with independent evidence. Existing embedded protocol steps remain supported.

When a newer approved Lab step version exists, the pinned occurrence offers **Adopt approved v…**. Adoption changes only the open protocol draft. Save and independently approve the assembled protocol before using that new version. Workflows and active batches keep their existing protocol versions. **Retire Lab step** prevents new selection; existing pinned occurrences, versions, usage and history remain available.

In the editor, **Fields to record** defines the information operators enter. **Recorded for** specifies whether an entry applies to the batch or individual samples. Use **Units and symbols** beside a unit field to choose a common unit, or type a custom unit. **Insert symbol** beside text fields inserts **µ, Δ, °, ±, ×, ≤, ≥** at the cursor, replacing selected text when present. Selecting a common unit replaces the unit field. These controls preserve the entered symbols without converting measurements.

In each step editor, **Batch report** lets you omit the upload control or include a QC report or preparation worksheet. When a report is included, **Report required** determines whether a performed step can be saved without its PDF. Required reports show a required marker and validation message in both preview and live recording. Allowed skips do not require a report. New steps start without a report; older definitions retain their existing report behavior until explicitly changed in a draft.

**Batch only** fields apply to every eligible tube without showing a sample-entry list. For **Same entry with sample exceptions**, select **Record exception** to reveal the collapsed sample cards. Enter only the overrides that differ from the batch entry and give an exception reason. Turning **Record exception** off clears those overrides and their reasons. Fields configured for individual samples remain visible even when no shared exception is being recorded.

Use **Material used**, **Equipment used**, and **Output created** in **Fields to record** to collect these entries with the step. In configuration, choose a vendor/product or an **Internally prepared reagent** under **Configured material**. For entries without lot tracking, you can instead define the material manually with its name and optional vendor. The saved version retains those details. Set **Quantity unit** in configuration using free text or **Units and symbols**. During recording, the material and unit are fixed; the operator enters only the quantity. Enable **Include lot number** in configuration to request a released inventory lot at run time; only lots linked to the exact configured product, or prepared lots of the exact configured material definition, are offered. Older purchased lots need **Assign product** in Materials before they can be used in a product-specific step. Manual descriptions do not support lot tracking in new configurations. Saving the step records its stock use. Batch quantities default to an amount per sample; authors can instead specify a total batch amount. **Include equipment barcode** links equipment use to an eligible registered instrument; without it, record the equipment name.

The configured unit appears in the quantity label, for example **Quantity per sample (µL)**. Tracked lots must use the same unit; units are not converted automatically. Enter the amount in that unit; the batch total appears below. Earlier approved configurations without a unit continue to use the selected lot's unit, or manual entry for untracked materials.

When the step includes a QC or preparation report, attach it at the end of the step entries, before the final confirmation. The report applies to the included batch samples.

An output field records a separate library container for each included sample. Enter common quantity, unit and storage values once, with individual values where needed. Existing outputs retain their identity. **Save step record** saves these entries together. A correction retains previous resource use rather than consuming stock again; a repeat records new material/equipment use. Existing resource requirements can be explicitly converted to these fields in a configuration draft using **Convert existing requirements to fields**.

## Configuration preview

Use **Configuration preview** in a Lab step or protocol editor to inspect unsaved configuration, or from a saved Lab step version. It opens the Library prep capture form with clearly fictional example tubes. Preview does not approve a configuration or establish scientific validity.

Select any step directly, or use **Previous step** and **Next step**. No prior specimen or QC state is needed. Choose performed, repeat (when permitted), or correction presentation; optional and conditional steps also expose the performed/skip decision. These selections inspect form presentation and do not simulate workflow progression.

Enter example values and select **Validate entry** to see missing or invalid fields. Shared and individual fields, units, choices, confirmations, QC criteria, optional attachments and resource controls follow the same form configuration as Library prep. Resource controls show fictional choices; output validation creates no containers or barcodes. File selections remain local and are never uploaded.

**Reset example values** clears the current form. Changing steps or presentation also clears its example values. **Return to configuration** discards all example entries while preserving your configuration draft. Edit the draft and reopen preview to inspect the updated definition. Nothing entered in preview becomes laboratory evidence, inventory activity, approval, sequencing work or customer status.

## Preparation batches

Open **Lab operations → Library prep** to create a preparation batch. Choose an active tray format and an approved workflow whose protocol versions explicitly support preparation batches. POMS generates the batch identifier from the service and creation timestamp in UTC, for example **PSeq-20260912-143052**. A numeric suffix distinguishes batches created at the same time. You do not enter a batch name; add optional notes for context. The identifier stays with the batch through preparation and sequencing handoff. Existing batches retain their original names. Jobs must request the same service and have a confirmed tube-use instruction. Accepted, available tubes without an existing attempt can join the batch even if the job was created under an older workflow. Adding a tube records this batch’s exact workflow version on its attempt. An existing unstarted attempt must already use that same version; cancel it with a reason before selecting a different procedure. Started attempts cannot be moved to another workflow.

In **Find eligible tubes**, use **Freezer box barcode** to scan a box or enter part of its recorded barcode. Combine it with the tube barcode or job search to narrow the list further. Each result shows its recorded freezer box. The list shows up to 10 tubes per page, with a total count and **Previous**/**Next** controls below it. Changing either filter returns to page 1. **Clear filters** returns to the first page of all eligible tubes; filtering and paging do not change eligibility or add a tube to the tray. Within the **Tray** card, beneath the **Required** legend, select **Find eligible tubes** to expand or collapse the list. It starts collapsed, and collapsing it preserves your filters and current page.

The progress strip shows **Prepare tray → Prepare libraries → Complete batch → Sequencing handoff**. Prepare tray includes scanning the physical tray, loading tubes and confirming the contents. The Next step panel changes its guidance as you progress through those tasks. When recording protocol evidence, its title shows the protocol number and the step number within that protocol, followed by the step name. Loading tubes alone does not complete Prepare tray: partial trays are allowed, and you choose **Confirm tray** when ready. Future work stays hidden until relevant; the strip still shows where the journey leads.

Hover over a step, focus it with the keyboard, or tap it to see its purpose, completion condition and saved progress. Press Escape or select outside the panel to dismiss it. These panels provide information only; use the visible Next step panel and Tray controls to perform the work.

**Prepare libraries** shows **X of Y steps completed** and **A of B protocols completed** across the pinned workflow. Each workflow step and protocol stage counts once, regardless of tube count. A step completes when all continuing tubes have resolved it; partial coverage, unresolved QC and evidence needing review after an earlier step changes keep it incomplete. A protocol completes when its stage has been completed for the continuing tubes. Counts include permitted step and stage skips. Failed tubes remain visible but do not block progress for the remaining tubes; failing a tube does not itself complete a step. **Complete batch** separately tracks tube outcomes.

In the **Confirm tray** dialog, check **I reviewed the tray identities and positions**, then choose **Confirm tray**. The checkbox starts unchecked and is required before confirmation can be saved.

Select an occupied tray position to see its tube details, including **Specimen type** (for example, Human liver) and **Declared safety information** from the specimen's current saved authorization. Missing declarations appear as **Not recorded**. These are submitted declarations; a missing safety declaration does not mean that no hazards are present.

In the **Tray** header, scan the label on the physical tray into **Scan physical tray barcode**, then press Enter or **Save tray**. This is a one-time assignment. POMS saves that tray identity separately from the batch identifier. The same physical tray cannot belong to two Draft or In progress batches. Completion or cancellation makes it available for another batch while preserving the earlier history. Existing batches without a tray identity require a physical tray scan before adding tubes or confirming the assembly. The saved barcode appears read-only when you return or refresh. Continue loading or confirm the assembled tray without rescanning it. Saving the physical tray identity does not start preparation or approve the contents. The Next step panel gives guidance; use the nearby Tray controls directly.

**Print tray label** reprints the saved physical tray barcode if its label needs replacing; it does not create a new batch identity. An empty, unconfirmed Draft batch can be assigned a different tray using **Change tray**, scanning the replacement barcode and choosing **Save tray**. Cancelling a change retains the saved tray. A populated batch must retain its assigned tray; remove the tube assignments before changing trays.

Occupied cells show the position and a small QR code. Planned status is summarized at tray level rather than repeated in every cell. Select an occupied cell to show the readable tube barcode, job, specimen, actions and preparation evidence below the grid. Only the selected tube's details appear. The screen QR is a reference; identity confirmation requires scanning the label on the actual tube.

Scan an accepted, available tube directly into an empty tray cell. The scanner’s Enter key or the cell’s **Save** button saves the scan. After a successful save, focus moves left to right, row by row, skipping filled and unavailable cells. If you started partway through the tray, it returns to earlier empty cells after reaching the end. You can select a different empty cell at any time. An invalid or duplicate tube stays in its cell with an error for correction; saving does not start preparation. If another operator has filled the position, review the saved tube and dismiss your unsaved entry before continuing. Tubes may come from different jobs, and positions may remain empty. A specimen can reserve only one source at a time. When the tray is assembled, review the identities and positions and choose **Confirm tray** in its header. Save or clear unsaved barcode entries first. Confirmation locks tray editing, survives reload, and surfaces **Start preparation** in the Next step panel. Before starting, **Actions → Edit tray** reopens it with a reason; confirm it again after making changes. **Prepare libraries** becomes current once the tray is confirmed. Choose **Start preparation** in the Next step panel when laboratory work begins. This starts the first workflow stage immediately, records the start time and permanently locks the positions for this batch; there is no additional confirmation dialog. After start, the **Tray** card collapses to leave room for preparation work. Select its chevron heading to inspect tubes and their details; the scannable tray QR code, its printed identifier and **Print tray label** remain visible. **Review library outputs** opens the tray automatically. Reloading a started batch also begins with the tray collapsed. After start, failed tubes remain in its history and reserves must enter a new batch.

Supervisors and Protocol Administrators manage tray formats in **Lab operations → Lab configurations → Tray formats**. Choose **New tray format** or **Edit tray format** to set rows, columns, position labels and unavailable positions. Preview the layout before saving. Retiring a format prevents its use for new batches while preserving existing batch layouts. Return to **Library prep** to select a format when creating a preparation batch.

Record steps from the batch in workflow order. The page shows the current stage, with available and already-recorded steps. Later stages and blocked future steps appear as their prerequisites are resolved; recorded evidence remains available through tube details and batch history. Each entry shows **Applies to X tubes** and an excluded count when relevant. Expand **View tube coverage** to inspect included tubes and exclusion reasons. Every eligible tube is included automatically, with no tube-selection checkboxes; confirm the coverage before saving. Use an explicit recorded failure action when a tube must stop processing. The approved protocol determines whether a value is one batch entry, an individual tube measurement, or a shared value with identified tube exceptions. Record an exception reason whenever a tube differs from a shared value or QC outcome. Failed tubes cannot inherit later success. A Hold remains unresolved until permitted repeat/correction or explicit attempt failure; it does not automatically start a reserve.

When recording a preparation step, each tube shows its **Customer sample ID**, **Specimen type**, and linked **Accession reference**. The accession link opens the specimen in a new tab so the entry remains open. For the standard **Specimen reference** capture, POMS records the saved accession for each tube automatically; do not copy it into a shared field or supply exception reasons merely because tubes belong to different specimens. Scan the physical source tube barcode and compare its identity with the displayed details. Other observations, dates, confirmations and genuine exceptions still need to be recorded according to the protocol. Enter **Identity checked on** once in **Batch entries**; that date is saved with each tube covered by this entry, without a separate per-tube date field. Automatic accession recording does not confirm that a physical identity check was performed.

If a tube fails an identity check and its preparation attempt must be disqualified, choose **Close attempt as failed** in that tube's section of the step-entry modal. The confirmation names the tube and requires a **Failure reason** and **Reason and evidence**. **Back to step** returns without closing the attempt. After a successful failure, the tube remains visible in its original tray position and in the modal, marked **Failed**, with its identity, failure reason and recorded evidence available as read-only information. It cannot be selected for new step evidence. The shared date and other tubes' values remain in place; review and confirm the remaining tube coverage before saving. You can also access the failure action by selecting the tube in **Tray** (under **Actions** when other actions are available).

The active and failed counts distinguish tubes that can continue from those still occupying the tray. Closing an attempt does not record physical removal. Unsaved entries for a newly failed tube may remain visible for reference while the modal stays open; they are not saved as step evidence. Failed tubes remain visible when you reopen the modal.

For **Review a prior input QC hold** in the established test protocol, POMS automatically records a skip when every active sample has resolved prior steps, passing input QC, and no Hold or Fail anywhere in its input QC history. Failed tubes are excluded. The check runs after saving step evidence or a tube failure, and when an authorized user opens an already eligible batch. The existing step role is still required. **Batch history** identifies the automatic skip, covered tubes and reason, and the Next step panel moves forward. If any active sample has a prior Hold or Fail, even after a passing repeat, the review remains pending. Missing evidence also prevents an automatic skip. Other written conditions still need an explicit assessment. An earlier correction may require fresh downstream evidence; automatic skipping never replaces an existing review or skip.
For the conditional review step, enter **Review rationale** once. When recording the performed review, POMS also saves that text as the condition assessment. Skipping, repeating or correcting the step still requires a separate reason.

Under **Sample entries and exceptions**, each sample card shows its position and barcode, with **Close attempt as failed** at the right end of the header. All sample cards, including failed tubes, start collapsed when the modal opens. Select the position and barcode to expand or collapse the sample details. The standard identity check omits **Tube exception or QC reason** when there are no shared-value exceptions or tube QC decisions to explain. Other steps retain this field for non-fatal exceptions and QC holds.

Closing an attempt as failed preserves its history and prevents its output from supplying a successful sequencing library. An exception note alone does not fail a tube; excluding it from an entry only leaves that step unrecorded for it. Record failure before continuing the remaining tubes.

In preparation QC steps, **QC report (optional)** appears after the other step-entry fields, before the final configured confirmation. It lets you choose one PDF up to 10 MB for the tubes covered by the entry. The report uploads only when you choose **Save step record**. You may remove the selection. Saving without a report is permitted only when the step makes it optional. POMS records the report reference, operator and timestamp automatically. After saving, download it from **Batch history** or the tube's **Current sample records**. It is internal laboratory evidence. Attaching a report does not select a QC outcome or replace required measurements and confirmations. An unsuccessful upload or file scan leaves the step unsaved so you can retry or remove the file. The test protocols no longer require copying a synthetic QC record reference; earlier saved references remain in history.
For library preparation, **Preparation report or worksheet (optional)** appears after the other step-entry fields, before the final configured confirmation, in place of the manual preparation record reference. Choose one PDF up to 10 MB, or continue without a file. POMS records its reference automatically when you save the step record. Download it later from batch history or current sample records. Preparation mode, protocol-required materials and equipment, and output barcode evidence remain required.

In the step modal, record configured material and equipment fields directly in **Batch entries** or the appropriate sample card. These values save together with **Save step record**. Resource choices exclude expired material lots and equipment past its calibration due date; POMS rechecks eligibility when you save. Output fields offer common quantity, unit and storage values with individual overrides. Saving creates a separate output barcode for each tube; existing outputs are shown without being recreated. Unavailable tubes cannot receive an output. Output creation does not confirm physical identity; scan each output barcode using **Confirm output identity** when ready. After all required steps and QC resolve, choose **Complete protocol**. The confirmation names the batch and protocol and explains what completing it will do; no additional data entry is required. The final stage requires a confirmed output barcode for every successful tube and reuses recorded QC to establish library eligibility. The Next step panel offers **Complete preparation batch** after every tube has succeeded with a library or explicitly failed. **Sequencing handoff** appears only after the batch is complete and passing libraries are available. Each passing library has an **Add to sequencing batch** action and shows its destination after assignment. A batch with no passing libraries retains its outcomes without offering handoff. Scientific result review remains separate. An output from a failed attempt remains visible for traceability but cannot supply a sequencing library; it does not require output confirmation.

Existing specimen and execution pages link batch-owned work back to its tray. Historical individual executions retain their original entry workflow and evidence.

### Record a material amount exception

Configure a material field as **Same entry with sample exceptions** when the protocol permits recording different actual amounts. This uses **Amount per sample**; total batch amounts do not have sample overrides. Product, lot and unit stay shared. Existing approved configurations keep their original recording scope.

Enter the common quantity and lot in **Batch entries**. Select **Record exception**, expand the affected sample, and select **Record a different amount for this sample**. Enter its actual quantity, including zero if none was added, or select **Amount unknown**. Record a **Material exception reason** and choose the **Tube outcome**:

- **Continue — permitted variation** records a known amount and lets the tube continue subject to the protocol and QC requirements. The choice does not approve a scientific deviation.
- **Hold for review** prevents further processing until a Supervisor resolves the operational hold.
- **Close attempt as failed** closes the attempt while retaining the tube in the tray and preserving its material use.

An unknown amount requires hold or failure. **Save step record** saves the quantities, exception reasons and outcomes together. The common amount applies only to samples without an override; each known override replaces that sample's share. Failed tubes still consume what was actually used. Unknown quantities place the tracked lot on hold for reconciliation in **Materials**. No amount is guessed or returned to stock. When several material exceptions affect a tube, failure takes precedence over hold.

While entering material exceptions, use the outcome field and save the step; the immediate failure button is unavailable to protect unsaved material entries. Clearing **Record exception** discards unsaved material overrides. Corrections retain previously recorded resource use. To release an operational material hold, a Supervisor reviews the selected tube and chooses **Resolve hold**, with a reason; any uncertain lot must first be reconciled. Failed attempts cannot be resumed.

## Build the service workflow

In **Lab operations → Lab configurations**, use the **Protocols**, **Workflows**, and **Tray formats** tabs for controlled procedures, ordered service workflows, and preparation-tray layouts. Protocols is the default tab. Each tab has its own link, so refreshing or returning from the workflow builder restores the relevant tab. Retired protocols are hidden by default; select **Show retired** to include them. Protocols with only discarded versions are excluded from the working list, including when Show retired is selected. Discarded revisions remain labeled **Discarded** in the associated protocol's read-only version history, with no editing or creation actions on those revisions. Newly created protocols without a saved version remain visible.

In the **Workflows** tab, create one **Controlled service workflow** for each active marketed laboratory service. POMS takes the service identity from the active Commercial catalog; do not type or maintain a separate product key.

Choose **Add version** to open the workflow builder. Arrange stages in operating order and select the exact Approved protocol version for each stage. Mark each stage Required, Optional, or Conditional. A Conditional stage requires a plain-language condition. Add handoff criteria when the next stage depends on recorded evidence or a specific state.

Save the Draft, then use its **Actions** menu to continue editing or approve it. Approved versions offer **Promote to production** and **Withdraw approval** in **Actions**; Invalid versions offer **Review workflow**, **Revalidate and approve**, and **Discard**. **Promote to production** makes the complete workflow the default for new standalone source selections using the exact Approved protocol versions selected in its stages. The previous Production workflow becomes Retired; existing attempts and batches retain their original workflow and protocol versions. A service has only one Production workflow at a time. The workflow and every included protocol must have recorded independent approval or an explicit administrator approval override before promotion. Once those approvals exist, an authorized Protocol Administrator may promote the workflow even if they authored the workflow or one of its protocols. Promotion records the actor and time and preserves the original approvals.

## Control protocol versions

For a new preparation-compatible version, select **Use this version for preparation batches** and explicitly choose each capture's evidence scope and each QC gate's scope. Barcode identity captures must use **Tube** scope. Use individual scope for measurements that must be taken separately; shared entries do not represent individual measurements. Older approved versions do not gain scopes automatically. Follow the approval workflow below before using the new version.

A Protocol Administrator creates the protocol by entering its name and optional description, then continues directly to the structured definition builder. POMS derives a readable, immutable protocol key from the name and adds a suffix if that key is already in use. The key remains available in **Edit name and description** before approval when it is useful for support or integration matching, but the working list does not repeat it as user-facing protocol information.

Open the protocol's labeled **Actions** menu. Choose **Edit protocol** to change the Draft's ordered steps, captures, resources, or QC gates. **Edit name and description** is available only until the first approval; after approval, create a new protocol identity if the name no longer represents the controlled method. The system-assigned key never changes.

An identity without a saved definition is labeled **Setup incomplete**. Choose **Edit protocol** to build its initial Draft; the interface does not present this first definition as a new version. After a version is Approved, choose **Create new version**. The initial definition starts blank and offers an optional library-preparation example. A later version starts with the Approved definition. Build the procedure in execution order and use **Add step**, the move controls, duplicate, and remove actions to maintain that order. Replace example or copied content with the intended laboratory procedure before saving.

For each step, enter a name and operator instructions, then choose whether the step is required, optional, or conditional. Conditional steps require a plain-language condition. Record whether the step may repeat, whether explicit confirmation is required, and whether a particular laboratory role is required. **Confirmation required** adds one step-completion attestation as the last field, after the report upload; it applies to every permitted role, including Supervisor. Turning it off removes that attestation. There is no separate tube-coverage checkbox: Save step record submits the automatically included eligible tubes, which POMS rechecks.

Add captured values only when the approved procedure requires them. Captures may be numbers, text, dates, choices, file references, or barcodes. Number captures may identify a unit, and choice captures require their permitted choices. For a barcode that identifies the original specimen tube, select **Must match the selected source tube**. POMS then checks it against the attempt source. Existing approved definitions are unchanged; add this setting through a controlled new version when appropriate. Barcodes identifying recorded containers must belong to the current attempt’s lineage. Use Material used, Equipment used, and Output created fields for resource recording. Older comma- or line-separated requirements remain readable and can be converted explicitly in a draft. A QC gate records explicit Pass, Fail, or Hold outcomes and requires acceptance criteria.

Use the collapsed JSON preview only to review or copy the portable representation; do not author protocol JSON directly. **Create protocol draft** validates the complete definition and reserves the next version number. Cancelling after edits warns before discarding the unsaved definition.

Each protocol can have only one Draft. While a Draft exists, **Edit protocol** replaces **Create new version**. Saving returns to the Protocols list, where the state is shown as **Draft vN** and the Actions menu exposes **Review and approve**.

Standard approval is a formal, irreversible controlled release. **Review and approve** shows the exact ordered definition, whether preparation batches are enabled, each capture and QC scope, and source-tube matching requirements. Review these scopes to distinguish a shared observation from evidence recorded for each tube. Approval requires an explicit attestation, requires an independent Protocol Administrator who did not author the version, and records that approver and timestamp. Approval locks the version and makes it the Approved version for future use. If another version was previously Approved, it becomes Superseded; work and service workflows already pinned to it remain unchanged.

Before any version has been approved, the Actions menu offers **Delete protocol** to permanently remove the protocol and all its Draft or previously Discarded versions after confirmation. It does not offer Discard draft at this stage. Once any version has been approved, deletion is unavailable; **Discard draft** lets you abandon a proposed revision while retaining that version as Discarded in history. Approved, Superseded, and Discarded versions remain immutable and available to explain protocol history. A protocol referenced by a laboratory record cannot be deleted.

### Administrator approval override

Independent approval remains the normal path. If you authored the version and are an active platform administrator with protocol-management access, its **Actions** menu also offers **Approve with administrator override**. Review the exact protocol definition or workflow stages, enter a required **Override reason**, and explicitly confirm responsibility for bypassing independent review. Choose **Approve with override**. The saved version shows **Administrator override**, the reason and approval time; POMS retains your actual identity and the change in its audit history. It never records another person's approval.

An override applies only to the selected version. It does not waive valid definitions, preparation evidence/QC scopes, current protocols, permissions or version checks. A workflow still requires separate **Promote to production** before it becomes the default for new standalone source selections. Recorded overrides satisfy that promotion's approval check; older self-approvals without an override record do not. **Withdraw approval** removes the current workflow approval and override, while retaining the audit history. Approving again requires a new review and either independent approval or a new explicit override. Scientific review and result-release rules are unchanged.

## Select a specimen and source

Record actions appear as a named button when only one action is shown, or in **Actions** when multiple options are shown. Instructions written as **Actions → action name** refer to the direct button when it is the only option.

Use **Specimens → Tubes → Execution → Libraries → Exceptions → Review** in that order as work progresses. **Actions → Manage specimen attempts** returns to the specimen list. Each sample opens its own page with recognizable sample ID, received/expected counts, intake decision and separate processing progress.

Complete tube intake during accessioning; **Tubes** shows the recorded decisions. On the specimen page, choose **Actions → Select source tube**, choose an accepted available tube and scan its exact barcode. For a new standalone attempt, POMS uses the service’s current Production workflow; existing matching Planned execution records retain their recorded procedure. Saving reserves that source and assigns the first workflow stage together. To use an Approved preparation workflow, add the tube directly through that preparation batch instead. It does not start processing. Unreceived, unreviewed, held, rejected, unavailable or previously failed tubes cannot be selected. Extra tubes remain reserves.

On an older unstarted job with no recorded tube-use instruction, a Supervisor must first choose **Confirm tube-use instruction**, review the order and record a reason. Selecting the source adopts a matching existing Planned first-stage execution; it does not duplicate it. Historical started work is never assigned a source retrospectively and is flagged for review.

An unstarted attempt can be cancelled with a reason to release its reservation. After start, the source is fixed. Completing one protocol does not release the source: assign the next stage from the same specimen's Actions menu. Complete stages in order within that attempt. Required stages cannot be skipped; Optional and Conditional stages need an explicit decision and reason. A replacement tube begins a new attempt at the first stage of the workflow selected for that attempt; earlier evidence is not reused.

## Failure, reserve use and exhaustion

A QC Hold or failed repeatable reading keeps the current attempt unresolved; it does not automatically authorize a reserve. Repeat a permitted step or resolve the evidence in that attempt. **Hold attempt** records a reason and the next action owned by the person recording it. **Resolve attempt hold** requires a resolution before work resumes.

When the attempt cannot continue, choose **Close attempt as failed**. Select a predefined processing-failure reason, a started execution and the evidence/reference. This closes the attempt while retaining completed work, consumed materials, equipment records and output lineage. It does not dispose of the tube. Failed-attempt outputs cannot proceed into a replacement attempt, batching or release.

If another eligible tube remains, choose **Use reserve tube** and explicitly select/scan it. Review the failed source, failure reason and restart point. A new linked attempt begins at the first workflow stage; earlier completed stages do not count for the replacement. Only one attempt may be active, and a successful attempt prevents further source selection.

When no reserve is currently eligible, processing waits **On hold** with a material-resolution explanation and next action. Expected tubes or potentially usable tubes awaiting review must be resolved first. After terminal failure with no material remaining for further permitted analysis, use **Confirm material exhausted**, record evidence and confirm the specimen **Failed** outcome. Its earlier intake acceptance remains intact. Close any resulting follow-up exception separately after review. No receipt, review or exception resolution automatically starts a reserve.

## Retire a protocol

A Protocol Administrator can choose **Actions → Retire protocol** after a protocol has been approved. Enter a retirement reason. Discard any open protocol draft first. Retirement is final and preserves identity, definitions, approvals and execution history. Use **Show retired** to see its reason and date.

The dialog checks affected workflows and work before allowing confirmation:

- If samples are in process, retirement is blocked and the affected jobs are listed. This includes unfinished workflows whose earlier executions have started or completed.
- If work is assigned but has not started, POMS warns that retirement may strand it. Choose **Proceed anyway** only after reviewing those jobs. Cancel leaves everything unchanged.
- If workflows use the protocol, POMS warns that they will become invalid. Confirmation retires the protocol and invalidates affected workflows together. Changes to the displayed impact require a fresh review.

POMS creates a new **Invalid** recovery revision with the retired protocol removed and the remaining stages kept in order. Previous versions, stages and approval records remain historical. Open **Review workflow** to inspect or modify the remaining procedure. **Revalidate and approve** checks that at least one stage remains and every stage uses an eligible approved protocol, and applies the normal independent-review rule. Further edits are optional when the remaining procedure is already correct. Explicit **Promote to production** is still required before the revised workflow governs new work.

Queued attempts retain their original assignments and display **Execution workflow needs review** when their procedure is unavailable. They cannot begin processing under an invalidated version. Cancel an unstarted attempt with a reason before selecting a replacement procedure. A commercial job without an attempt is not tied to the retired workflow. Approving a replacement never changes started work.

If retirement removes every stage, open **Review workflow** and choose **Add stage** to add an eligible replacement. Save the revision, then have an independent reviewer revalidate it. Saving alone leaves the workflow Invalid.
## Record execution

On the laboratory job's **Execution** tab, open the execution link. The dedicated workspace shows its pinned protocol version, specimen, instructions, resource requirements, saved evidence, and remaining completion blockers. For a Planned specimen execution without an accepted available tube, **Tube acceptance required** explains the prerequisite and **Open tubes** opens the job's **Tubes** tab. **Start execution** also requires an explicitly selected source attempt. Use **Open specimen and source** if selection is missing; accepting a tube alone does not select it. Return after accessioning to refresh eligibility. Choose **Actions → Start execution** and scan the selected source barcode again. POMS rechecks current eligibility before recording any start.

Choose **Record** for the next step. Enter the measurements, dates, approved choices, file references, and barcodes required by the procedure. Units appear beside the relevant capture. A file reference records an identifier; it does not upload a file. Confirm performed work when the procedure requires it. POMS checks the saved values against the pinned definition and records your identity and the time.

Use **Material** and **Equipment** on the job to record consumed lots and quantities, output-container references, and equipment use. Before confirming a step's resources, check the listed inputs, outputs, and equipment against that traceability. Resource descriptions and QC acceptance criteria are controlled instructions assessed by the operator; POMS does not infer scientific thresholds or match free-text resource descriptions automatically.

Enter a quantity greater than zero and no more than the lot's available stock. If the quantity is unavailable, correct the entry and save again; the rejected attempt does not deduct stock or add a consumption record.

Resolve every optional and conditional step with recorded evidence or an allowed skip. The preparation review described above can record its skip automatically; other conditional steps require an explicit decision. An allowed **Skip with reason** requires the reason or condition assessment. Required steps cannot be skipped, and previously performed work cannot be changed into a skip. A step with a specified Lab role must be recorded by a user with that effective role. Earlier steps must be resolved before later evidence is recorded.

For a QC gate, deliberately choose **Pass**, **Fail**, or **Hold**. Fail and Hold require a reason. They remain visible as saved evidence, mark the execution Blocked, and prevent later steps and completion until resolved through an allowed repeat or correction.

Saved progress remains available when you leave and return. When all steps and QC gates are satisfied, choose **Actions → Complete execution**, add an optional deviation note, and confirm completion. POMS rechecks the persisted evidence and locks the completed execution. A held or finished laboratory job cannot accept new execution evidence.

## Recover safely

Do not edit an Approved definition to repair an execution. If a protocol is wrong, create and approve a new version; update and approve the service workflow separately when its stage composition must change. If execution data is wrong, use the supported correction or repeat-work path while preserving the original record and reason.

**Repeat** is available only when the pinned procedure allows it. **Correct** repairs a data-entry error and requires a Supervisor who also has the step's required role. Both require a reason, fresh confirmations, and a new saved record. Expand **Step history** to review every earlier value, QC outcome, reason, operator, and timestamp. If an earlier step changes, later evidence must be reviewed and recorded again before completion.

If another user changes the execution during a save, POMS reloads the current record and preserves your entered values. Review the latest history before saving again. If the original action is no longer available, close the form and use an allowed repeat or correction.

**Abandon execution** remains available for eligible historical, unlinked work and retains its evidence with a reason. For an attempt-linked execution, cancel the unstarted attempt or explicitly close the started attempt as failed from the specimen page; ordinary abandonment cannot authorize reserve use. Historical unstructured definitions or results remain preserved with a recovery message; a Protocol Administrator must prepare a valid new approved version for new work. POMS does not silently rewrite controlled history.

## Who can perform work

Source and attempt actions require an effective Operator or Supervisor role. Each protocol step additionally checks its required role. POMS retains the actual person recording each action; an earlier assignment never grants permission to perform a step.

If you edit an attempt action and then cancel, press Escape, or leave the page, POMS asks before discarding your unsaved details. Declining keeps your entries. While a save is pending, wait for its result before closing or navigating away. If another operator changes the record, your entries remain available; close the action, review the latest specimen state, and reopen the action that is still appropriate.
