# Customer relationship management

The POMS CRM is Phaeno's first-party commercial system of record. It runs without HubSpot or another external CRM and retains Companies, Contacts, Leads, Opportunities, Activities, Tasks, pipeline history, reporting, and reviewed handoffs in POMS.

CRM records are internal Phaeno records. Creating or changing a CRM record never creates a Portal account, grants membership, activates a service, authorizes ordering, starts laboratory work, or exposes scientific data.

## Access CRM

Open **CRM** from the Phaeno toolbar. CRM access requires an active Phaeno platform-administrator account. External organization users cannot see CRM navigation or CRM data.

Use the far-left sidebar beneath the toolbar to move among **Home**, **Companies**, **Contacts**, **Leads**, **Opportunities**, **Tasks**, **Reports**, and **Administration**. The sidebar header identifies CRM and, on wider screens, provides the pin or unpin icon. Pointer users can preview an unpinned sidebar from the left edge; keyboard and touch users open it from the edge tab. Choosing a section closes an unpinned sidebar.

## Start from Home

Home answers what needs attention. It shows overdue and due-soon Tasks, Leads without a next action, stale Opportunities, required-data warnings, your next Tasks, and recently changed Opportunities.

Use **Find anything** to search active Companies, Contacts, Leads, Opportunities, and Tasks. Enter at least two characters, then choose a result to open its owning workspace. Search does not cross into Portal Accounts, users, specimens, files, or laboratory records.

## Keep the domain boundaries clear

- **CRM** owns commercial relationship facts, qualification, pursuits, follow-up, and sales history.
- **Accounts** owns Portal organizations, memberships, invitations, readiness, and service entitlements.
- **Order operations** owns reviewed commercial orders, quotes, fulfillment, accounting source records, and release decisions.
- **Lab operations** owns physical specimens, accession, controlled execution, materials, equipment, libraries, batches, sendouts, exceptions, and scientific approval.

Use a reviewed CRM handoff when relationship context should enter Accounts or Order operations. Never copy patient information, sample identifiers, scientific results, credentials, confidential file contents, or internal laboratory investigation notes into CRM.

Use the CRM subtopics in this guide for complete field, status, workflow, administration, handoff, and recovery instructions.
