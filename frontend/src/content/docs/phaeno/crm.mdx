# Customer relationship management

The POMS CRM is Phaeno's first-party commercial and customer system of record. It runs without HubSpot or another external CRM and retains Companies, Contacts, Leads, Opportunities, Activities, Tasks, pipeline history, reporting, Portal access, and reviewed handoffs in POMS.

CRM records are internal Phaeno records. The Company is the canonical Customer, Partner, or Prospect identity. Creating it does not grant membership, activate a service, authorize ordering, start laboratory work, or expose scientific data.

## Access CRM

Open **CRM** from the Phaeno toolbar while working in the Phaeno organization. An active Phaeno account with the **Commercial operator** role or platform-administrator access can use CRM. External organization users cannot see CRM navigation or CRM data.

Use the far-left sidebar beneath the toolbar to move among the CRM sections available to your role. **Relationships** contains Companies and People; **Sales** contains Leads and Opportunities; **Follow-up** contains Tasks and, for administrators, Requests; **Insights** contains Reports; and administrators also see **Administration** for CRM configuration. **Home** remains the starting summary. The sidebar header identifies CRM and, on wider screens, provides the pin or unpin icon. Pointer users can preview an unpinned sidebar from the left edge; keyboard and touch users open it from the edge tab. Choosing a section closes an unpinned sidebar.

## Start from Home

Home answers what needs attention. It shows overdue and due-soon Tasks, Leads without a next action, stale Opportunities, your next Tasks, and recently changed Opportunities. Administrators also see required-data warnings.

Use **Find anything** to search active Companies, Contacts, Leads, Opportunities, and Tasks. Enter at least two characters, then choose a result to open its owning workspace. Search does not cross into Portal users, specimens, files, or laboratory records.

Commercial staff can maintain Companies, Contacts, relationships, Leads, Opportunities, Tasks, ordinary Internal Activities, personal saved views, and Internal custom values, and read Reports. A platform administrator manages Company and Contact deactivation/reactivation and merges, Portal identity and access, Company Requests, pipelines and field definitions, imports, exports, shared-view publication, and Restricted Activities and custom values. Administrative sections and actions are shown only to administrators.

## Keep the domain boundaries clear

- **CRM** owns the canonical Company, commercial relationship facts, qualification, pursuits, follow-up, sales history, and the central Requests review queue.
- Company detail presents the internal tenant scope, memberships, invitations, readiness, and service entitlements after Portal access is enabled. That scope is not a second customer record.
- **Order operations** owns commercial orders, quotes, cancellation and hold decisions, Finance, and governed result publication. **Lab operations** owns physical fulfillment, laboratory execution, and scientific approval.
- **Lab operations** owns physical specimens, accession, controlled execution, materials, equipment, libraries, batches, sendouts, exceptions, and scientific approval.

Use a reviewed CRM handoff when relationship context should enable or change Portal access or enter Order operations. Never copy patient information, sample identifiers, scientific results, credentials, confidential file contents, or internal laboratory investigation notes into CRM.
