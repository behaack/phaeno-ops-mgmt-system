# Portal handoffs and account links

A CRM handoff carries approved commercial context into a separate review queue. It is the supported boundary between CRM and Portal operations.

## Create the handoff

Open the CRM Company and find **Portal handoffs and account links**. Select **Create handoff** and choose one type:

- Portal onboarding
- Portal evaluation
- Trial Project
- Custom work
- Service change
- Relationship change
- Offboarding

Choose the requested Prospect, Customer, or Partner relationship when the request requires it, or retain the linked-account/workflow default. Link an Opportunity when the request arose from a specific pursuit. Select P-Seq Lab Service or P-Seq Kit only when that service is actually being requested. Enter a required tenant-safe summary and optional internal notes.

Do not include patient information, sample identifiers, scientific results, credentials, confidential file contents, or laboratory investigation detail. The handoff summary is relationship context for review, not an unrestricted notes field.

## Understand what is created

Submitting creates one pending, idempotent relationship request. It appears in Accounts and, when applicable, Order operations intake. The Company workspace shows the request number, handoff type, status, and creation time.

The handoff does not by itself:

- create or reclassify a Portal Account
- invite or activate a user
- mark an Account Ready
- activate a service entitlement or ordering authorization
- create a quote, order, sample roster, shipment, or Lab work order
- approve offboarding or revoke existing access

An authorized reviewer owns those consequences in the destination workflow. If an approved Account is created or linked, POMS records an explicit CRM-to-Portal account link with organization identity, relationship kind, reason, actor, and time.

## Review and follow up

Open **Accounts** to review onboarding, evaluation, service, relationship, or offboarding requests. Open **Order operations** for custom-work or order-intake review when the request is applicable there. Keep CRM Activities and Tasks focused on commercial follow-up; record Portal decisions in their owning workflow.

If the Company already has an active account link, choose the appropriate change or offboarding request instead of creating a second onboarding request. If the same handoff submission is retried, POMS returns the existing request through its idempotency boundary rather than creating duplicate work.

CRM lifecycle labels and Opportunity Won status never substitute for this reviewed boundary.
