# Company access and user administration

Phaeno administrators manage Portal access, readiness, service entitlements, integration requests, and global access from the canonical CRM Company. Prospect, Customer, and Partner administrators manage membership only within their own access scope. Access is invitation-based and every durable administrative change is audited.

## Know which administration surface owns each record

The CRM **Companies** directory is the only customer directory. A Company may exist before Portal access is approved. After approval, the same Company workspace displays its relationship type, readiness, services, members, invitations, requests, and retention settings. The internal Organization identifier used for tenant isolation is not a second customer record and does not appear in a separate directory.

**User management** is also connected to durable account APIs. In the Phaeno context, active accounts and invited people appear in one list alongside global account status and every enforced Phaeno role. A **Pending invitation** status means the invitation has not been accepted and has not activated the offered access; the person may already have a sign-in account. Open a card’s actions menu to edit an active user, deactivate another active user, reactivate a disabled user, or resend or revoke a pending invitation. A Phaeno administrator cannot deactivate their own account. Edit one active user to update their name, Platform administrator assignment, and additive laboratory roles together. Platform administrators retain bootstrap access to every laboratory capability even when no explicit laboratory role is assigned.

The role picker contains only roles enforced by the current authorization model: **Platform administrator**, **Lab operator**, **Lab supervisor**, **Protocol administrator**, **Scientific reviewer**, and **Lab operations administrator**. Authentication-provider metadata and unsupported job-title labels do not grant Portal access.

Cards marked **Pending invitation** still represent invitation records, not accepted user records. Enter the person’s first name, last name, and exact sign-in email, then select the Platform administrator and additive laboratory roles they should receive. The pending role intent grants no access. It becomes active only when the recipient accepts and becomes an active Phaeno member.

## Review and maintain Companies

Create the Company first, then request the approved Portal relationship when access is needed:

- **Prospect** for an external organization that may receive explicitly granted curated data or participate in an approved no-charge Trial, but cannot place paid orders.
- **Customer** for organizations that request laboratory services.
- **Partner** for organizations that submit specimens for PSeq Lab Service or purchase the PSeq Kit, which includes data assembly.

Internal employee invitations use the existing Phaeno organization in **User management**; Company requests create only the approved external relationship.

Do not use Company lifecycle or Portal readiness as a shortcut for access. Curated package access requires an exact-version grant, member access requires an accepted invitation, and commercial work requires a current service entitlement.

Open **CRM** and select **Companies** to search durable external customer records. Select the Company name to open its workspace. Use its **Requests** tab to propose online access, a product or service change, a relationship change, or reviewed work. Use CRM's central **Requests** section for requests that still need a Phaeno decision. The Company tab preserves request history but does not duplicate the decision controls. There is no standalone customer-creation action outside CRM.

For a pending onboarding or evaluation request, select **Approve and enable Portal access** and record the approval reason. POMS atomically approves the request, creates the Company's internal tenant scope with **Pending** readiness, and attaches it to the existing Company. Online-access approval never adds or removes a product or service entitlement, creates another customer record, or creates a user.

If an older approved onboarding or evaluation request shows **Portal access incomplete**, select **Complete Portal access**. POMS creates and attaches the missing tenant scope without changing product or service entitlements. This recovery does not create another Company, invite users, create an order, or complete the request.

If POMS finds an active, compatible access scope with the exact Company name that is not linked to another Company, it pauses and shows **Existing access scope found**. Review the displayed name and relationship, then select **Use existing access scope** to reconnect it. Reuse preserves existing users, orders, invitations, and history. Existing Company requests are attached to the scope so they can be fulfilled through their separate owning workflows; attaching them does not grant product or service access. POMS will not offer this shortcut when the scope is inactive, belongs to another Company, or has a different relationship kind; resolve those cases before approving access.

For a Customer, open **Departments & services** and choose the Department in **Department readiness**. The same derived checks used by Order Intake separate requirements before pricing, quote issuance, and invoicing. Follow each setup link to its owning screen. Historical readiness notes are retained as context; no manually selected Ready label authorizes work.

## Review Company requests

Use CRM's central **Requests** section to decide pending onboarding, evaluation, product and service, relationship, sales-assisted order, and offboarding requests created by the first-party CRM. Use **Needs decision**, **Approved / needs work**, and **Completed / history** to follow a request through completion. Approved requests stay actionable until completed or cancelled, and the Company's **Requests** tab links to the same queue. Historical imported requests remain readable alongside requests created in CRM; use the current queue to review their recorded state and available actions.

Approve or decline a pending request with a reason. For onboarding or evaluation, approval also creates and attaches the Company's pending-readiness access scope while leaving product and service entitlements unchanged. Other request types record the decision only. Complete each approved invitation, entitlement, or order change through its owning workflow, verify the outcome, and then select **Complete request** in the central **Requests** queue and record what was completed.

Never put specimen, scientific, downstream-customer, credential, or other sensitive operational details in an external reference or request summary.

## Invite administrators and members

When creating their password, recipients enter it once and can use **Show password** or **Hide password** to check their entry.

After accepting, recipients see a welcome message naming their organization and select **Open Portal** to continue. The Company People view shows their Portal access as active.

From Company detail, open **People**. Use **New person** to create and associate a Contact in one save, or **Add existing person** to associate an existing Contact. Select **Invite to Portal** and choose Organization administrator or explicit Department membership and Department-admin roles. The Contact identity is filled in; edit the Contact first if its email or name is incorrect. Assign organization-wide administrator access only when the person must govern every Department; otherwise use Department membership and grant Department administrator access only where needed. A CRM Contact remains separate from a Portal user and grants no access until the invitation is accepted. The invitation page shows the invited name, organization, and email before sign-in. The recipient selects **Continue with** their email, completes verification for that fixed address without entering or editing the email, and then selects **Accept invitation**. If another account is signed in, **Switch account** preserves the invitation while they sign in with the invited address. Viewing the invitation grants no access.

Use **Manage access** on the person to change their Organization role, add or remove Department access, change Department-admin roles, deactivate membership, and resend or revoke a pending invitation. Each change identifies the person and consequence before confirmation. Use **Edit relationship** to change their Company-specific title, commercial role, or effective dates. Use **Departments & services** to create or rename operational units, choose the default, and record typed configuration overrides. Every Organization begins with **General**. Organization administrators can edit the five Organization defaults for PO, billing and notification email, shipping, and result instructions. A blank Department override inherits the Organization default, then the commercial profile or system rule. The system PO rule is not required; either level can explicitly require or waive it. Existing saved shipping and accepted-quote snapshots retain their values. Result-delivery instructions are retained with the commercial commitment, not an automatic change to storage destinations. Organization administrators can access every active Department; other users must retain at least one active Department assignment. When one business unit must not be discoverable or governed by another—or it has separate contracting, tax, legal, ownership, or retention obligations—create a separate Company/Organization instead of a Department.

External Organization and Department administrators can use **Departments** in the user menu. Organization administrators own structure, default selection, lifecycle, organization defaults, new invitations, and organization roles. Department administrators own settings and member access only within assigned Departments. Add existing members by exact email lookup; review each assignment or role change. One person may administer Research, belong to Operations as a member, and have no Finance access. Rights remain independent in each Organization.

Department changes use review dialogs. Deactivation requires affected active members to have another Department assignment and retains historical records. Reactivation does not restore revoked assignments automatically. Changing the default affects future default selections, not existing records or memberships. A conflict reloads the latest Department version while preserving your form entries for review. Contact invitation access must still be valid when the invitation is resent or accepted; if the Contact or Department changed, review and reissue the invitation.

Service entitlements and curated-data packages may be organization-wide or limited to one Department. A Department-specific service entitlement takes precedence over the Organization default for that Department. In **Data provisioning**, choose an explicit access scope when assigning a package; users in other Departments cannot list or download that Department's grant.

Users with more than one available Department select the working Department from the user menu. The header shows the Organization and Department currently in force. Do not rely on a browser URL or a previously opened page to establish access; the API validates both scopes on every protected operation.

Do not create a normal membership directly. Invitation acceptance provides the user-facing consent point and the audit link between invitation, user, and membership.

For an internal employee, select the Phaeno organization and open **User management**. Use **Add Phaeno user**, enter the person’s first name, last name, and exact sign-in email, and select the narrowest roles needed for their work. Grant **Platform administrator** only when platform-wide administration is required. The card remains marked **Pending invitation** until acceptance; afterward, open its actions menu and choose **Edit** whenever the profile or roles must change.

To follow up before acceptance, find the card marked **Pending invitation**, open its actions menu, and choose **Resend invitation**. Resending rotates the private link and is subject to a cooldown. Revoke an invitation that should not be accepted.


## Convert a Prospect

In the Prospect Company’s **Requests** tab, create **Change the relationship** and choose Customer or Partner. The form shows the current relationship and records your requested target. A reviewer approves it in **Needs decision**, then selects **Apply Customer relationship** or **Apply Partner relationship** in **Approved / needs work** and records the completed work. Applying the approved request performs the conversion and completes the request together. Customer-to-Partner and Partner-to-Customer reclassification are not supported by this action. Conversion preserves the Company, internal access identifier, users, memberships, grants, and audit history. It does not create an order, assign broad data access, or configure prices automatically.

After conversion, complete the applicable operational setup: analysis availability and the billing and tax profile for a Customer, or offerings, negotiated reagent pricing, assembly profiles, and Partner assembly credit for a Partner.

## Manage service entitlements

From the Company, open **Departments & services**, then select **Services** to record dated commercial permission:

- Customers may receive **PSeq Lab Service**.
- Partners may receive **PSeq Lab Service** and **PSeq Kit**.
- Prospects and Phaeno organizations do not receive commercial service entitlements.

Set configuration to **Pending**, **Ready**, or **Blocked**. A service is usable only when its entitlement is effective and configuration is Ready. Overlapping periods for the same service are rejected.

If a service already has a non-ended entitlement, select **Edit** on that entitlement instead of adding another overlapping period. Preserve the service and existing effective date unless the commercial authorization changed, set the current configuration, attach the eligible approved source request, and save. Editing updates the existing audited record; it does not create a duplicate. Ended historical entitlements remain read-only.

Online access does not imply a product or service entitlement. Add a dated entitlement here only after the corresponding product or service authorization is approved.

When the entitlement implements an approved Portal request, select that request as its source. The selector shows only approved or applied requests for this organization that include the selected service. Leave the source blank only for a documented manual exception; do not attach an onboarding-only request to a service entitlement.

Use **End now** instead of deleting an entitlement. Review the consequence dialog and record why the entitlement is ending. The Portal retains and displays that reason with the ended entitlement.

PSeq Kit includes kit fulfillment followed by data submission and assembly. Do not create a separate data-assembly entitlement or sell data assembly without the kit.

## Deactivate and restore access

Deactivate a membership in Company detail to remove one access relationship. Deactivate the Company when none of its memberships should be usable; this suspends its attached Portal access scope. Reactivating the Company restores that scope. Each action opens a consequence dialog; verify the named Company or person before confirming.

A deactivated membership is restored through a fresh invitation and acceptance. When all Portal access for another person must stop, select **Deactivate** on that Phaeno user and confirm the named person. The account, memberships, assigned roles, and audit history remain, but the user immediately loses effective access. A Phaeno administrator cannot deactivate their own account. Use **Reactivate** to restore an authorized disabled account; then review its memberships and roles before considering access complete. Preserve historical records; do not delete users, memberships, or organizations to resolve access problems.

## Triage access safely

Confirm the person’s email, session state, selected organization, active membership, role, and organization status. Use record identifiers and audit events to trace changes. Do not ask for passwords, sign-in codes, invitation tokens, browser storage, or another tenant’s records.

For a user who has lost both the authenticator and all one-time backup codes, verify the person's identity and organization through the approved support procedure. In Clerk, reset the user's MFA enrollments and revoke every active session. Record the administrative recovery without storing codes, authenticator secrets, or recovery links. Tell the user to sign in again and connect a new authenticator; required MFA keeps the session pending until enrollment is complete. An email request starts the review but is not sufficient by itself to authorize the reset.

If two administrators update the same record, the portal can reject the stale action. Reload the latest version, review the intervening change, and then make a deliberate new update rather than overwriting it.
