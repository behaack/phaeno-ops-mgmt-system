# Company access and user administration

Phaeno administrators manage Portal access, readiness, service entitlements, integration requests, and global access from the canonical CRM Company. Prospect, Customer, and Partner administrators manage membership only within their own access scope. Access is invitation-based and every durable administrative change is audited.

## Know which administration surface owns each record

The CRM **Companies** directory is the only customer directory. A Company may exist before Portal access is approved. After approval, the same Company workspace displays its relationship type, readiness, services, members, invitations, requests, and retention settings. The internal Organization identifier used for tenant isolation is not a second customer record and does not appear in a separate directory.

**User management** is also connected to durable account APIs. In the Phaeno context, active accounts and invited people appear in one list alongside global account status and every enforced Phaeno role. A **Pending invitation** status means the recipient has not completed account registration. Open a card’s actions menu to edit an active user, deactivate another active user, reactivate a disabled user, or resend or revoke a pending invitation. A Phaeno administrator cannot deactivate their own account. Edit one active user to update their name, Platform administrator assignment, and additive laboratory roles together. Platform administrators retain bootstrap access to every laboratory capability even when no explicit laboratory role is assigned.

The role picker contains only roles enforced by the current authorization model: **Platform administrator**, **Lab operator**, **Lab supervisor**, **Protocol administrator**, **Scientific reviewer**, and **Lab operations administrator**. Authentication-provider metadata and unsupported job-title labels do not grant Portal access.

Cards marked **Pending invitation** still represent invitation records, not accepted user records. Enter the person’s first name, last name, and exact sign-in email, then select the Platform administrator and additive laboratory roles they should receive. The pending role intent grants no access. It becomes active only when the recipient accepts and becomes an active Phaeno member.

## Review and maintain Companies

Create the Company first, then request the approved Portal relationship when access is needed:

- **Prospect** for an external organization that may receive explicitly granted curated data but cannot order work.
- **Customer** for organizations that request laboratory services.
- **Partner** for organizations that submit specimens for PSeq Lab Service or purchase the PSeq Kit, which includes data assembly.
- **Phaeno** for internal platform operations.

Do not use Company lifecycle or Portal readiness as a shortcut for access. Curated package access requires an exact-version grant, member access requires an accepted invitation, and commercial work requires a current service entitlement.

Open **CRM** and select **Companies** to search durable external customer records. Select the Company name to open its workspace. Use **Company requests** to propose online access, a product or service change, a relationship change, or reviewed work. Use CRM's **Requests** section for requests that still need a Phaeno decision. There is no standalone customer-creation action outside CRM.

For a pending onboarding or evaluation request, select **Approve and enable Portal access** and record the approval reason. When the relationship is Customer, **Ordering authorized** defaults to **On**. Leave it on to create a current **Ready** PSeq Lab Service entitlement, or turn it off to enable access without ordering authorization. POMS atomically approves the request, creates the Company's internal tenant scope with **Pending** readiness, creates the selected Customer ordering authorization, and attaches it to the existing Company. It never creates another customer record or a user.

If an older approved onboarding or evaluation request shows **Portal access incomplete**, select **Complete Portal access**. POMS creates and attaches the missing tenant scope and selected ordering authorization in one operation. This recovery does not create another Company, invite users, create an order, or complete the request.

Record readiness as **Not reviewed**, **Pending**, **Ready**, or **Blocked**, with an internal note when useful. Readiness describes operational setup; it does not invite a user, activate a service, or place an order.

## Review Company requests

Use CRM's **Requests** section to decide pending onboarding, evaluation, product and service, relationship, sales-assisted order, and offboarding requests created by the first-party CRM. Approved requests remain available in the Company's **Company requests** area. An approved online-access request without its tenant scope remains in the queue only as a recovery exception. Historical HubSpot-sourced requests remain readable, but no HubSpot intake or simulation endpoint is part of the standalone workflow.

Approve or decline a pending request with a reason. For onboarding or evaluation, approval also creates and attaches the Company's pending-readiness access scope. The Customer dialog makes ordering authorization explicit and defaults it on; no user access or order is created. Other request types record the decision only. Complete each approved invitation, entitlement, or order change through its owning workflow, verify the outcome, and then select **Complete request** from the Company's request area and record what was completed.

Never put specimen, scientific, downstream-customer, credential, or other sensitive operational details in an external reference or request summary.

## Invite administrators and members

From Company detail, use **Manage users** or open the **Users** section. Enter the designated CRM Contact’s exact sign-in email after Phaeno review, and assign **Administrator** only when the person must authorize commercial work or manage membership; otherwise assign **Member**. A CRM Contact remains separate from a Portal user and grants no access. Ask the recipient to sign in with the invited email and accept the Portal invitation.

Do not create a normal membership directly. Invitation acceptance provides the user-facing consent point and the audit link between invitation, user, and membership.

For an internal employee, select the Phaeno organization and open **User management**. Use **Add Phaeno user**, enter the person’s first name, last name, and exact sign-in email, and select the narrowest roles needed for their work. Grant **Platform administrator** only when platform-wide administration is required. The card remains marked **Pending invitation** until acceptance; afterward, open its actions menu and choose **Edit** whenever the profile or roles must change.

To follow up before acceptance, find the card marked **Pending invitation**, open its actions menu, and choose **Resend invitation**. Resending rotates the private link and is subject to a cooldown. Revoke an invitation that should not be accepted.

In local development, a Company's **Users** section also provides **Create sign-in link** after Portal access is enabled. It replaces the prior invitation link and displays a fresh link that you can copy into a private window. On the invitation page, choose **Sign in or create account**; if the fake email does not yet exist in the Clerk development instance, choose **Sign up**, create it with that exact invited email, and then accept the invitation. This development control is not available in production. Use it only with fake development accounts, and do not put a real invitation link into tickets or documentation.

## Convert a Prospect

Convert a Prospect access relationship to Customer or Partner only after the business relationship is approved. Conversion preserves the Company, internal access identifier, users, memberships, grants, and audit history. It does not create an order, assign broad data access, or configure prices automatically.

After conversion, complete the applicable operational setup: analysis availability and Customer credit for a Customer, or offerings, negotiated reagent pricing, assembly profiles, and Partner assembly credit for a Partner.

## Manage service entitlements

Use the organization’s **Services** tab to record dated commercial permission:

- Customers may receive **PSeq Lab Service**.
- Partners may receive **PSeq Lab Service** and **PSeq Kit**.
- Prospects and Phaeno organizations do not receive commercial service entitlements.

Set configuration to **Pending**, **Ready**, or **Blocked**. A service is usable only when its entitlement is effective and configuration is Ready. Overlapping periods for the same service are rejected.

A Customer access scope enabled with **Ordering authorized** on already has an open-ended **Ready** PSeq Lab Service entitlement. Review it here before adding another. Turning the access-enable choice off leaves this list unchanged; add a dated entitlement here only after the commercial authorization is approved.

When the entitlement implements an approved Portal request, select that request as its source. The selector shows only approved or applied requests for this organization that include the selected service. Leave the source blank only for a documented manual exception; do not attach an onboarding-only request to a service entitlement.

Use **End now** instead of deleting an entitlement. Review the consequence dialog and record why the entitlement is ending. The Portal retains and displays that reason with the ended entitlement.

PSeq Kit includes kit fulfillment followed by data submission and assembly. Do not create a separate data-assembly entitlement or sell data assembly without the kit.

## Deactivate and restore access

Deactivate a membership in Company detail to remove one access relationship. Deactivate the Company when none of its memberships should be usable; this suspends its attached Portal access scope. Reactivating the Company restores that scope. Each action opens a consequence dialog; verify the named Company or person before confirming.

A deactivated membership is restored through a fresh invitation and acceptance. When all Portal access for another person must stop, select **Deactivate** on that Phaeno user and confirm the named person. The account, memberships, assigned roles, and audit history remain, but the user immediately loses effective access. A Phaeno administrator cannot deactivate their own account. Use **Reactivate** to restore an authorized disabled account; then review its memberships and roles before considering access complete. Preserve historical records; do not delete users, memberships, or organizations to resolve access problems.

## Triage access safely

Confirm the person’s email, session state, selected organization, active membership, role, and organization status. Use record identifiers and audit events to trace changes. Do not ask for passwords, sign-in codes, invitation tokens, browser storage, or another tenant’s records.

For a user who has lost both the authenticator and all one-time backup codes, verify the person's identity and organization through the approved support procedure. In Clerk, reset the user's MFA enrollments and revoke every active session. Record the administrative recovery without storing codes, authenticator secrets, or recovery links. Tell the user to sign in again and connect a new authenticator; required MFA keeps the session pending until enrollment is complete. An email request starts the review but is not sufficient by itself to authorize the reset.

If two administrators update the same record, the portal can reject the stale action. Reload the latest version, review the intervening change, and then make a deliberate new update rather than overwriting it.
