# Activities and Tasks

Company, Contact, Lead, and Opportunity workspaces contain one commercial activity timeline and a linked Task list. Record work in the owning record so its context remains visible.

## Log an Activity

Select **Log activity** from the record workspace. Choose Note, Call, Meeting, Email, or Status change; enter the required subject and optional details. POMS records the current time and signed-in actor.

Choose visibility deliberately:

- **Internal** is ordinary Phaeno commercial context.
- **Restricted** is narrower internal material that should not be treated as generally shareable.

Manually logged Email means a user recorded that an email occurred. It does not send or retrieve email. Connected email and calendar capture are deferred.

System, Portal, and Task events may also appear in the timeline. They are written by POMS and cannot be edited as ordinary notes. Never place patient information, sample identifiers, credentials, scientific results, file contents, or laboratory investigation detail in an Activity.

## Create a Task

Select **New task** from the owning record. A title is required. Add a description, active Phaeno owner, priority, due date and time, reminder, and optional daily, weekly, or monthly recurrence.

Priorities are Low, Normal, High, and Urgent. Use them to communicate commercial urgency, not scientific or patient severity. A Task must be linked to a Company, Contact, Lead, or Opportunity; the standalone Tasks workspace intentionally does not create context-free Tasks.

## Manage the Task queue

Open **Tasks** from the CRM sidebar to filter by status or overdue state, apply saved views, and export the current view. Statuses are:

- **Open:** not started.
- **In progress:** actively owned.
- **Blocked:** unable to proceed; a reason is required.
- **Completed:** finished.
- **Cancelled:** intentionally stopped.

Use **Update** to change status. A blocked Task requires the blocking reason. Completed and Cancelled Tasks leave the active update action; reopen through an allowed status transition when work genuinely resumes.

Completing a recurring Task creates its next occurrence according to the stored rule. Confirm the new occurrence and due date rather than changing the completed historical Task.

## Use Home attention safely

CRM Home shows overdue Tasks, Tasks due within seven days, and your next assigned Tasks. Completing a Task from Home uses the same durable status transition as the record workspace. If the Task has changed since Home loaded, refresh before retrying so a newer owner's work is not overwritten.
