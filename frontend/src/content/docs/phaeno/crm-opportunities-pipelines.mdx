# Opportunities and pipelines

An Opportunity represents one commercial pursuit for one CRM Company. It is not an order, quote, entitlement, or promise that Phaeno will perform work.

## Create an Opportunity

Open **Opportunities** and select **New opportunity**. Record the required Opportunity name, Company, and active pipeline. POMS assigns a unique, read-only Opportunity Number when the record is created, starts the Opportunity in the selected initial stage, and records the first stage-history entry. Use the Opportunity Number when referring to the pursuit outside its detail workspace. Product interest is optional and uses the defined PSeq Lab Service or PSeq Kit product domain rather than free text. You may also record:

- Phaeno owner and product or service interest
- amount and three-letter currency
- expected close date, next step, competitors, description, and tags

Amount remains in its recorded currency. POMS does not silently convert it. Keep the next step current so Home and reporting can distinguish active work from stale work.

## Work the pipeline

For a selected pipeline, the **Pipeline summary** shows each stage's configured probability beside its name, a numeric count and recorded value across all matching Opportunities, including records beyond the current queue page. Values remain separate by currency; **unpriced** means no amount has been recorded, while zero is a recorded value. Search, pipeline and the stale-work filter affect the summary. Selecting a stage filters the queue while keeping the full stage summary visible; choose **All stages** to clear the stage filter. **Clear filter** stays at the end of the filter row, after the Pipeline dropdown when shown, and is disabled when no other filters or later queue page need clearing; selecting a stage alone does not enable it. When enabled, it also clears the selected stage while keeping the pipeline.

Use the single **Opportunity queue** to compare Company, stage, amount, expected close, owner and next action. It pages through 25 records at a time and uses compact rows on phones. Search applies automatically as you type; Enter applies the current text immediately. Use the summary buttons to select a stage. The Pipeline dropdown, including **All pipelines**, appears only when multiple active pipelines are available. With one available pipeline, it is selected automatically and the dropdown remains hidden even with the 30-day filter enabled. **All pipelines** replaces stage cards with one **All opportunities** count across every matching queue page; search and the 30-day filter still apply. The combined queue shows each Opportunity's pipeline and stage. Choosing a specific pipeline restores its selectable stage cards. Saved views retain the filters; old saved board/table preferences now open the same queue. **Export current view** uses those same filters.

Open the Opportunity name for its view-first workspace. Use **Edit** for commercial facts. Associate buying-team Contacts with an optional role and one primary Contact when that reflects the pursuit.

In **Opportunity contacts**, select **Associate** and search the Contact directory by name or email. Choose a result, optionally record the person's role, and select **Associate contact**. Contacts already actively associated with this Opportunity are excluded from the choices. If the section or directory search fails, use its **Retry** action before continuing; a failed load is not an empty buying team.

Use the association's **Manage** action to change its role or primary designation. **Remove association** asks for confirmation and retains the Contact and prior association in history. **Keep association** returns to the editor. Failed saves retain your entries; leaving an edited draft asks whether to discard the unsaved changes.

## Move stages deliberately

Use **Move stage** from the Opportunity workspace. Select another active stage from the same pipeline and record the required reason or useful context. Stages are categorized as Open, Won, Lost, or Abandoned and carry the configured probability.

A configured stage may require a reason. Lost and Abandoned stages in the default pipeline require outcome context. POMS rejects a move to a stage from another pipeline or an invalid terminal transition.

Every accepted transition writes immutable history containing the prior stage, new stage, actor, time, and reason. Moving a closed Opportunity back to an Open stage clears the current closed outcome while retaining earlier history.

Do not edit pipeline configuration to falsify one Opportunity's outcome. Correct the Opportunity through a valid stage transition and leave the historical transition visible.

## Interpret values and reports

Counts include Opportunities in every currency. Aggregate amount and weighted-forecast figures are USD-only; a non-USD Opportunity is counted but its amount is not treated as converted USD. Probability is taken from the active stage for reporting.

An Opportunity marked Won is still only commercial evidence. Use a reviewed CRM handoff for Portal onboarding, evaluation, Trial Project, custom work, service change, relationship change, or offboarding. Orders and Lab work retain their separate authorization gates.
