# Laboratory operations

The dashboard's **Laboratory work** card identifies each job by its Commercial order number, submitted reference, or a unique work-order identifier when neither is available. Select a job to open its record, or use **Open Lab operations** at the top-right of the card.

Use **Transportation kits** in Lab operations to assemble and dispatch physical kits. Use **Receipt & accession** to identify arriving packages, compare registered tubes, and see authorized work awaiting specimens. Choose **Open receipt** to record custody, accession, and intake disposition in the linked Lab work order. Customer quote acceptance opens sample-list preparation; finalizing the exact compliant roster records the Commercial authorization and creates the linked Lab work order and shipment together. Do not create Lab work manually to repair an order.

When recording a requested kit shipment, review the **Phaeno ship-to destination** separately from the Customer delivery location. The Job starts with Phaeno's global default. Before the first kit for that Job is sent, you may select another current Active destination that supports every requested kit, provided the Customer has not begun packing or confirmed a shipping insert. The first kit dispatch fixes the destination for later requests and batches. If that saved destination revision later becomes inactive, confirm with receiving that it can still accept the remaining kits, then check the required confirmation before dispatch. A new default does not redirect the Job, and inactive destinations cannot be selected for new Jobs. Customers using compatible kits already at their location use the default without a separate routing queue.

Use the far-left sidebar beneath the toolbar to move through **Receipt & accession**, **Jobs**, **Library prep**, **Master mixes**, **Sequencing batches**, and **Results & review**. Separate groups contain **PSeq kits** and **Data assembly**, then **Purchased Materials**, **Reagent manufacturing**, and **Transportation kits**, then **Suppliers & products** and **Equipment**. Phaeno platform administrators use Suppliers & products to maintain supplier names, product names, descriptions and product types for reagents and shipping supplies; the seeded Phaeno record is marked as an internal producer. Within Suppliers & products, use the **Suppliers & products** tab for vendors and their products, and the **Product types** tab to manage those categories and their eligibility for transportation kits. Search and **Show inactive** are in the shaded header of the suppliers, products and product types lists. Open **Lab settings** from **Administration** in the user dropdown menu for **Lab steps**, **Protocols**, **Workflows**, **Stage durations**, **Holiday calendar**, **Library tray formats**, and **Storage locations** in its own sidebar. Workflows distinguishes library preparation, sample-independent reagent manufacturing, and physical transportation-kit assembly; tray layouts are managed in Library tray formats and selected when assembling work in Library prep. Library prep opens preparation batches, where accepted tubes from compatible jobs are assembled into a tray and processed together. Open **Jobs**, directly above **Library prep** in the sidebar, for the job and specimen list. Jobs remain open until results for every in-scope sample have been published to the customer Portal. The **Active jobs** tab is the default. **Closed jobs** contains fully delivered and cancelled jobs, including cancellations before shipment. A failed sample does not close its job. Scientific approval and Ready for release do not complete delivery. Expand **Filters** in the list header to search or narrow either jobs tab. The section starts collapsed, shows how many filters are applied, and summarizes the selected criteria in one sentence while closed; collapsing it keeps those filters in effect. Use **Clear filters** beside the Filters label to reset only the selected tab’s filters, even when the section is collapsed. Typed dates apply when you leave the date field or press Enter, so you can finish entering the year. Each tab retains its own search, date filters and page when switching tabs, refreshing or returning from a job. Results & review opens the same jobs at scientific review; a listed job is not necessarily ready for approval. Its return link preserves the section you came from. Specimen, execution, library and exception history remain accessible from either entry point. PSeq kits retains its manufacturing workflow. Data assembly has **Sequencing runs** for sample assembly jobs and **Assembly cases** for Partner manufacturing. The sequencing connection currently shows a setup notice until the processing service is configured. Each assembly attempt retains actual start/stop times and final disposition; percentage progress is shown live without a saved update history. The sidebar header identifies Lab operations and, on wider screens, provides the pin or unpin icon. Pointer users can preview an unpinned sidebar by approaching the left edge; keyboard and touch users open it from the edge tab. Choosing a section closes an unpinned sidebar.

Use **Suppliers & products → Phaeno** to define each named reagent or transportation kit product. Reagents have type Reagent; finished kits have type Transportation kit, a stable SKU, a separate finished-kit name, and inventory unit each. Configure its procedure in **Lab settings → Workflows**. Reagent workflows show their earlier procedure revisions and approval history. In **Reagent manufacturing**, select the reagent by name, run its approved procedure, record source-lot use as it happens, then enter the actual finished amount for QC. For kits, approve an ordered step workflow and exact component bill of materials, pair the current approved revision with a shipping specification, then assemble individual kits in **Transportation kits**. A different administrator approves a revision unless a platform administrator records a reason for approving their own work. Tube and outer shipper products are counted in whole `each` units. If tube inventory is tracked by material lot, use one source tube lot per kit; its lot number must match the kit record, and every scanned tube ID in that kit traces to that lot. Record actual component use and every physical tube ID, verify the complete packed roster, and complete assembly before dispatch. Deactivating the finished kit product stops new assembly and removes it from new shipping recommendations. It is separate from sample and tube preparation. Use **Purchased Materials** to receive purchased lots by supplier product and printed lot number; the product supplies the inventory unit, while staff enter the amount received. Maintain named material and equipment locations under **Lab settings → Storage locations**. These names do not represent scanned tube or freezer-box positions.

## Investigation evidence preservation

Internal sample history, traceability records, saved investigation reports and uploaded supporting preparation/QC reports are retained indefinitely for now, separately from customer download expiry. Deleting expired customer result files cannot remove a supporting report referenced by internal evidence. External scientific files remain with their producing system; a saved reference or checksum does not mean POMS holds their bytes.

## Understand the boundary

Lab Ops owns return-kit preparation, physical PSeq kit fulfillment, shipment intake, specimen receipt, accession, container lineage, Data Assembly execution, controlled protocol execution, material and equipment use, libraries, cross-order batches, sequencing sendouts, exceptions, and scientific approval. Commercial owns order intake, the Customer or Partner order, quotes, approval, cancellation review, billing, and accounting source records.

Work enters Lab Ops only through an immutable Commercial authorization. Lab Ops publishes safe milestones and action requests back to Commercial; it never exposes internal laboratory tables or investigation notes to a Customer.

## Use the correct role

Laboratory roles are additive Phaeno-only assignments:

- **Lab Operator** records receipt, accession, containers, execution, consumption, equipment use, reagent manufacturing and kit assembly runs, libraries, batching, sendouts, custody, and exceptions.
- **Lab Supervisor** can perform operator work, approve material QC, abandon an unfinished reagent run with a reason, and resolve exceptions.
- **Protocol Administrator** authors, approves, activates, and retires controlled protocol versions and reagent manufacturing and kit assembly workflows.
- **Scientific Reviewer** records scientific approval after execution and blocking-exception gates pass.
- **Lab Operations Administrator** can perform all Lab actions and assign Lab roles.

Platform administrators retain bootstrap access. Only active Phaeno members can receive or use Lab roles; disabling or offboarding a user removes effective Lab access even if an assignment record remains. Assign the narrowest roles needed for a person's work. Separation of operator and reviewer is supported but is not mandatory for every service.

Manage these assignments from **User management**. Select **Edit** on an active Phaeno user, then change the laboratory roles in that user’s single **Roles** group and save the record. The Lab operations sidebar contains operational laboratory work only.

## Follow the work sequence

1. Open the authorized work order and verify the expected specimens.
2. Record receipt and accession; POMS assigns the authoritative barcode and creates the initial physical container.
3. Apply the intake decision and preserve every derived-container relationship.
4. In Library prep, assemble accepted sources into a preparation tray using an approved workflow for the jobs’ purchased service. Confirm the tray to start.
5. Record ordered steps with their prescribed batch or tube scope, shared resource use and any tube exceptions. Create and identify outputs in the same workspace, reuse preparation QC, and close the tray before adding passing libraries to a sequencing batch. Record sendout custody separately when applicable.
6. Resolve blocking exceptions and complete scientific review.
7. Publish **Ready for release** back to Commercial.

Every transition preserves prior state. Do not repair mistakes by deleting containers, overwriting protocol versions, replacing shipment history, or duplicating work orders.

## Investigate a sample or questioned result

Open **Closed jobs**, select the job, and open the specimen. **Sample history** is also available from an active job.

Choose a **Result version** to see its saved producing analysis, sequencing inputs, result-file attribution and exact source tube. A historical result without those links is shown as unknown; POMS does not infer a tube from the latest attempt.

Expand **Protocol work** to review entries, corrections and actual performers/times separately from the person and time that recorded the entry. Other sections show materials, equipment, libraries, scientific metadata, result files, approvals, exceptions and custody. **Event history** has Previous and Next controls. Open a protocol execution to review its controlled instructions and detailed work context.

Open **Supporting reports** to download a preparation or QC report attached to a step that covered this sample. **Recorded report details** shows the retained file identity and checksum. A download checks current access, the saved clean-scan status, file size and checksum before returning the file. A missing file or checksum mismatch appears as **Evidence unavailable**; investigate the original attachment and retry after the problem is resolved. Downloading does not replace or change the saved evidence. The access record confirms that a verified download was requested, not that the browser finished receiving it.

**Sequencing outputs** and **Analysis runs** link to individual records. Operators and Supervisors can record new evidence and linked corrections from these sections. See **Libraries, batches, and sequencing** for the required run, file, QC, software and source information.

**Delivery and retention history** covers all recorded result versions for this sample: delivery events, download attempts, verified download timing, retention schedules and saved policies, preservation holds and reissues. A started or failed download is not a completed transfer, and a completed transfer does not prove that the recipient read the file. Missing completion or commit timestamps remain unrecorded. A shared Trial release describes the package containing this sample; other samples' files and downloads are omitted. Package-wide holds or retention decisions still apply to the shared package. Saving an investigation report also preserves this history as it stood at that time.

**Find related samples** searches an exact tube barcode, material lot number, equipment asset code, sequencing run, library key or preparation-tray barcode within this job’s organization. A shared resource is a starting point for investigation; it does not establish that the samples share a problem.

Use **Save investigation report** to preserve the evidence available at that time. Each saved report’s **Actions** menu offers a readable report, which can be opened and printed, and a structured evidence manifest. The checksum identifies the exact saved manifest and is checked before download. Later work does not rewrite an earlier report. Customer download expiry does not remove saved investigation reports. These reports capture the sample history; supporting PDF files and external scientific files are not embedded.

Review missing or unavailable evidence. Recorded metadata does not certify scientific validity or prove that an external source file is still available. A failed source load appears as **Evidence unavailable**. Large histories may exceed the supported report size; POMS rejects that report rather than presenting a partial history as complete. Reports are for internal investigation, not automatic customer distribution.

## Operational readiness boundary

POMS allocates checksummed Phaeno container identifiers, renders DataMatrix tube labels, records reasoned print, scan-back and failure history, and adds eligible libraries to a draft batch by scan. These software controls do not prove that a particular printer, stock, scanner, browser, or environment is fit for production.

This guide explains the portal workflow; it is not a laboratory SOP or evidence that a production process has been validated. Before production use, Phaeno must approve the actual minimum fields, labels, printers, scanners, reprint and degraded-mode procedures, external NGS provider identifiers and manifest/status exchange, and operational ownership. Use only approved procedures and configuration for real work.

## Track delivery deadlines

**Active jobs** prioritizes Overdue, At risk and Due soon work, followed by other open work. Filter by **Job or organization**, **Due date: From / To**, **Deadline status** (All deadlines, Overdue, At risk, Due soon), and **Job status**. The list includes jobs once at least one specimen shipment has been recorded as shipped, delivered or received. A recorded specimen receipt also qualifies older jobs without shipment records. Jobs still being prepared for shipment are excluded. Matching jobs appear across pages, including sent specimens still awaiting acceptance. Expand **Filters** to use the Overdue, At risk and Due soon count links. **All** clears only the deadline status, keeping the other filters. These warning counts cover every page for the current search, due-date range and job status, before the deadline-status selection.

**Closed jobs** provides **Job or organization**, **Order date: From / To**, and **Outcome** (All outcomes, Delivered, Cancelled). Order date is the original order creation date; Trials use their creation date. Older work without its originating record uses the retained work creation date. Closed jobs show their outcome; delivery timing remains available in job details.

Either date endpoint may be blank. From and To include the entire selected calendar day in the browser timezone, which is shown below the filters.

Job statuses are Awaiting receipt, Awaiting acceptance, Ready for preparation, Library preparation, Sequencing, Data processing, Quality review, Awaiting delivery, and On hold. The overall status follows the earliest outstanding sample stage; an explicit job hold takes precedence. Sequencing includes libraries awaiting sequencing in a batch. Partial publication does not move remaining laboratory work into Awaiting delivery. Sample stages remain available in the job details. No failed-job outcome is defined yet.

The due date starts with the service offering’s saved maximum turnaround in calendar days, from scientific acceptance. Future offering changes do not move existing job deadlines. Every accepted job must have a delivery due date. A configured turnaround calculates it on acceptance. If no turnaround is recorded, an operator or supervisor must use **Actions → Set due date** on the job before accepting samples; acceptance cannot be saved without one. Before acceptance, the job may show **Awaiting acceptance**. Any historical accepted job missing its required date is flagged **At risk** for correction. **Due soon** means a delivery or outstanding sample target is within three calendar days. Holds, blocking exceptions and a forecast later than the due date produce **At risk**. Passing the job’s due time without complete delivery produces **Overdue**, even if the forecast has moved later. An earlier outstanding sample target remains visible.

Open a job to see the original delivery target, current due date, expected completion, published-sample count and due-date history. Operators and supervisors can choose **Actions → Set due date** (or **Adjust due date** when one exists), enter the exact date/time and a customer-safe reason, and save. The original target and forecast are retained. Commercial jobs receive one notice of this deliberate due-date change. Adjust a staff expected-completion estimate separately through the Commercial order when needed; the calculated progress forecast is explained below. Follow any commitments in the governing agreement; the standard TAT alone is not a contractual guarantee.

The form identifies the timezone used for entry. Dates retain their exact time cutoff; they do not silently mean end of day. The list refreshes deadline warnings while open. No new periodic email reminder is sent by the Jobs list.

Partial publication leaves a job open. Download or acknowledgement is not required for completion. A withdrawal makes the affected sample outstanding again while retaining the original full-delivery event; ordinary expiration of the download period does not undo delivery. Historical published jobs without a retained first full-delivery event show **Complete — timing unverified**, rather than an invented on-time/late assessment. Failed samples remain outstanding unless an actual customer-visible deliverable is published or the scope is explicitly cancelled through the existing process.

## Calculated expected completion

Active jobs show **Calculated expected completion** based on each sample’s recorded progress and configured stage durations. Open a job and expand **Sample progress and remaining work** to see current stage, entry time, time in stage, remaining calendar days and the stages still needed for Portal delivery. The latest outstanding sample determines the job date; parallel libraries are evaluated separately. A later calculated or explicit staff estimate makes the job **At risk** when it exceeds the committed due date.

The calculation updates when the queue or job is refreshed. An hourly internal refresh also retains forecast history. The calculated forecast, original turnaround baseline, staff estimate and delivery commitment are separate. Automatic calculations do not change a commitment or send a customer message.

An unfinished stage whose estimate is exhausted receives **one additional stage day** from the current evaluation time, followed by its remaining downstream work. A business-day stage uses an eligible business day for that allowance. The original estimate and recorded entry time remain intact.

**Insufficient information** means the calculation needs configuration, a reliable stage timestamp or a required workflow decision. **Blocked** means a hold, unresolved failure or blocking exception prevents a reliable date. Coverage shows how many outstanding samples can be estimated; a partial estimate is not presented as the whole job’s expected completion. Older library and review records may lack the newly captured transition times.

## Configure stage durations and holidays

Protocol administrators and supervisors maintain the **Stage durations** and **Holiday calendar** sidebar sections under **Lab settings**. Lab steps remains the default section.

1. Open **Holiday calendar** and create the calendar first. Enter its confirmed coverage period and the actual observed dates and names of holidays or other laboratory closures. An empty calendar confirms there are no additional exclusions within that period. No jurisdiction’s holidays are assumed automatically. The year filter and revision details appear in the calendar header. Holiday dates display as **Sep 18, 2026**; the display list and existing entries in the editor are ordered by observed date.
2. Open **Stage durations**, select the workflow version and choose **Configure durations** or **Actions → Edit durations**. Set estimated days and **Calendar days** or **Business days** independently for every applicable stage. Include normal waiting time. Blank means not configured; zero means no additional downstream time. Indicate whether the workflow requires library QC and sequencing after its protocol stages.
3. Business days use Monday–Friday in America/Los Angeles and exclude configured closure dates. Calendar-day stages count every day. Fractional business days count time within eligible dates; work starting on a closed date begins counting at the next eligible midnight. Forecasts needing business dates beyond confirmed calendar coverage remain unavailable until coverage is extended.
4. Save with a reason. Saving creates a revision. New jobs use the latest configured revision for their approved or current service workflow; existing workflow assignments retain their revision. If a sample starts an attempt using another workflow version, that version receives its own timing assignment. Each sample is forecast using its actual selected workflow.
5. To change existing jobs, choose **Actions → Preview and apply to jobs**. Review current and proposed forecasts, select the jobs and provide a reason. Apply the revision only after reviewing that preview. A calendar edit also requires selecting the new calendar in the workflow’s durations and applying the resulting policy revision to existing jobs.

Timing settings support operational estimates; they do not establish or change customer delivery commitments. A scientific or operational review is still needed to decide appropriate duration values.
