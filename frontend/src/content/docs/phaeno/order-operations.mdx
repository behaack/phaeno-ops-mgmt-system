# Order operations

Order operations is the Commercial workspace for taking in demand, staging
incomplete Customer work, preparing quotes, recording Customer approval, and
managing orders. PSeq Lab Service, PSeq Kit, and Data Assembly appear as order
types in one Commercial list; PSeq Lab Service also has a feature-flagged
**Order-to-cash** workspace for governed results and native POMS accounts
receivable.

Use the far-left sidebar beneath the toolbar to move among **Order intake**, **Orders**, and **Accounting**. **Order intake** is where Commercial staff enter a new Customer order and review first-party sales handoffs. A custom-work handoff and a requested Trial Project still require Phaeno review; they are not an executable order or Lab work merely because the handoff arrived. Create the handoff from its CRM Company or Opportunity, then review the resulting request in **Portal accounts**.

The sidebar header identifies Order operations and, on wider screens, provides the pin or unpin icon. Pointer users can preview an unpinned sidebar by approaching the left edge; keyboard and touch users open it from the edge tab. Choosing a section closes an unpinned sidebar.

## Enter and find orders

Choose **New Customer order** in **Order intake** when a salesperson is taking a
PSeq Lab Service order for a stage-eligible Customer. Stage eligibility requires
an active Customer, ready entitlement, and active offering; the selector shows
all such Customers with their remaining blockers. A staged order may exist
before an active Customer administrator or complete operational/billing setup,
but the quote cannot be issued and no Customer commitment can occur until the
derived readiness checklist is complete. Approval and exact sample-roster
finalization create the immutable authorization and linked Lab work.

For negotiated work, use the reviewed CRM handoff instead of **New Customer order**. The intake queue shows whether a handoff is pending review, blocked by an exact Customer or Opportunity prerequisite, ready to start, or already linked to an order. **Start Customer order** locks the Customer from the approved handoff and asks only for the price-bearing Job scope. One handoff can start one order. The order's internal details retain links to the CRM Company, optional Opportunity, and request number; Customer users do not see that internal CRM context.

Use **Orders** to search all Commercial orders. Narrow the list by **Order type** to distinguish **PSeq Lab Service**, **PSeq Kit**, and **Data Assembly** without turning those products into separate Order Ops modules. Open the order to review its immutable Commercial facts, quote or approval state, assignment, cancellation requests, and linked Lab workflow.

**Accounting** retains the Partner/manual journal-entry source report and
notification recovery. **Order-to-cash** provides PSeq governed package status,
release actions, native invoices/PDFs, receipt/allocation work, aging, and owned
attention according to the signed-in business roles.

Use status, hold, overdue, organization, assignment, and due-date filters to narrow the queue. Assign an owner and due date when the workflow supports them. A queue assignment coordinates work; it does not grant authorization or change the workflow state.

## Keep state boundaries visible

Operational status, quote status, accounting-source readiness, payment, scientific readiness, file scanning, and publication are separate facts. Do not mark one complete merely because another succeeded. Tenant users receive a curated timeline, so record a tenant-safe reason separately from internal notes whenever an action can be seen outside Phaeno.

Order Ops ends at the Commercial boundary. Use **Lab Ops** for return-kit preparation, shipment-packet and tube comparison, PSeq kit fulfillment, specimen receipt and accession, protocol execution, Data Assembly, materials, equipment, libraries, batches, sequencing sendouts, exceptions, and scientific approval. Lab readiness never publishes a file by itself.

## Triage safely

1. Confirm the active organization and workflow before opening a record.
2. Review the current operational, Commercial, accounting, payment, and release states independently.
3. Check open holds, cancellation requests, overdue actions, and failed deliveries.
4. Assign the work and due date when ownership is unclear.
5. Perform the smallest valid next action and preserve the existing record.

If a record appears stale, refresh it before retrying. Never repair a failed workflow by creating a duplicate order, quote, accounting source record, Lab work order, shipment, or release.
