# Order operations

When starting a Customer Job, select the Customer department as well as the
Company. A single available department is shown automatically. The department
owns the Job and determines Customer access and the applicable service rule;
a Blocked department rule cannot be bypassed by a Ready organization default.

Order operations is the Commercial workspace for Customer laboratory orders, Partner PSeq kit fulfillment, and Partner data assembly. POMS owns operational state, assignments, tenant-safe communication, PSeq result release, and PSeq accounts receivable.

Use the far-left sidebar beneath the toolbar to move among **Order intake**, **Order staging**, **PSeq kits**, **Assembly**, **Attention**, **Result release**, **Finance**, and **Legacy integrations**, subject to your explicit roles. **Order intake** combines sales handoffs, commercial review, pricing, and quotes in one active queue. **Order staging** shows every Customer and its readiness blockers; a Commercial Operator may prepare internal PSeq work before the Customer administrator is active when the stage-eligibility minimum is met. Quote issue and Customer commitment require all non-billing readiness items and an active Customer administrator. Billing readiness may be completed after acceptance but is required before invoicing.

When the Portal API and frontend are both running in their local development environments, **Simulate HubSpot handoff** can create a durable pending request for either a sales-assisted order or a no-charge Trial Project. The simulator requires a unique HubSpot Deal identifier and clearly identifies the resulting request as simulated. It does not contact HubSpot, bypass review, create an executable sales-assisted order, or implement the Trial Project lifecycle. Review the resulting request in **Accounts**.

The sidebar header identifies Order operations and, on wider screens, provides the pin or unpin icon. Pointer users can preview an unpinned sidebar by approaching the left edge; keyboard and touch users open it from the edge tab. Choosing a section closes an unpinned sidebar.

## Enter and find orders

- **Order intake** covers Customer and Partner commercial requests through review, pricing, quote issuance, and Customer acceptance. Accepted work leaves the active intake queue and continues in its owning Lab Operations queue.
- **PSeq kits** covers Partner order review, substitutions, backorders, shipments, and fulfillment.
- **Assembly** covers Partner intake validation, quoting, processing, quality review, and output release.
- **Attention** owns invitation, readiness, staged-order, projection/scan, unreleased-result, overdue-invoice, unapplied-cash, and reconciliation-difference exceptions.
- **Result release** publishes a scientifically approved, complete PSeq package per sample without a payment gate.
- **Finance** owns PSeq invoices, receipts/import, explicit allocation, aging, and reconciliation.
- **Legacy integrations** covers retained accounting-connector and notification recovery outside the active native PSeq AR workflow.

Search Order intake by order, Job, Company, or request number. A PSeq Lab Service row identifies an optional proposed price so Commercial users can find pricing decisions without opening every record. On the order, **Review proposed price** compares the proposed USD price per specimen with the active catalog price, requested quantity, difference, and subtotal. **Approve price and issue quote** preserves the proposal unchanged; **Amend price and issue quote** requires an internal reason. Tax is system-calculated: include and freeze it when the billing and tax profile is complete and Finance-approved, or issue an explicitly pre-tax quote and calculate tax at invoicing. The issued quote, not the proposal, is the offer a Customer administrator may accept. The operational detail shows the current quote value, revision, and status. **Internal context** in the audit timeline explains Phaeno-only decisions; it is informational, not a Customer-facing warning. Assign an owner and due date when the workflow supports them. A queue assignment coordinates work; it does not grant authorization or change the workflow state.

## Keep state boundaries visible

Operational status, quote status, accounting-source readiness, payment, scientific readiness, file scanning, and publication are separate facts. Do not mark one complete merely because another succeeded. Tenant users receive a curated timeline, so record a tenant-safe reason separately from internal notes whenever an action can be seen outside Phaeno.

Order Ops ends at the Commercial boundary for a Customer laboratory order: it remains in **Order intake** while pricing and quote decisions are active. After acceptance and exact sample-roster finalization authorize that work, use **Lab Ops** for return-kit preparation, shipment-packet and tube comparison, specimen receipt and accession, protocol execution, materials, equipment, libraries, batches, sequencing sendouts, exceptions, and scientific approval. The separate **PSeq kits** and **Assembly** sections continue to own their non-laboratory operational workflows. Lab readiness never publishes a file by itself.

## Triage safely

1. Confirm the active organization and workflow before opening a record.
2. Review operational readiness, invitation delivery, scientific package, release, invoice, receipt, allocation, reconciliation, and notification states independently.
3. Check open holds, cancellation requests, overdue actions, and failed deliveries.
4. Assign the work and due date when ownership is unclear.
5. Perform the smallest valid next action and preserve the existing record.

POMS checks the latest Job immediately before issuing a quote. If the record changes after that check, POMS reloads it and preserves the entered quote lines; review those values, then issue the quote again. Never repair a failed workflow by creating a duplicate order, quote, invoice, receipt, result package, Lab work order, shipment, or release. Assign an attention item, preserve attempts, and record its resolution.

## Review queued notifications

Order notices check the current active organization, department, user, and administrator assignments when dispatched. The department notification address is included when configured; otherwise the organization notification default applies. An inactive or mismatched department cannot use either route. Review failed notices with no eligible recipients before retrying them; changing an assignment after a notice was queued changes who can receive it.
