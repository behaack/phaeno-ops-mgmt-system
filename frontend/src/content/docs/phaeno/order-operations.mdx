# Order operations

Order operations is the Commercial workspace for Customer and Partner laboratory orders, Partner PSeq Kit orders, and Partner data assembly requests. Use it for each order's pricing, commercial decisions, follow-up, Finance, and result publication. Reusable defaults and scientific offerings are maintained in **Order configuration**.

When starting a Customer Job, select its Company and Department. A single available Department is selected automatically. The Department owns the Job and determines Customer access and the applicable service rule; a Blocked Department rule cannot be bypassed by a Ready organization default.

Use the far-left sidebar beneath the toolbar to move among **Order intake**, **Trial projects**, **PSeq kits**, **Assembly**, **Attention**, **Result release**, **Finance**, and **Legacy integrations**, subject to your explicit roles. **Order intake** owns staff-assisted Customer order creation, Sales handoffs, commercial review, pricing and manual quotes. Eligible organizations can place a configured standard Lab Service bundle from their own Job; that accepted commitment is shown on the same order. **Trial projects** handles no-charge evaluations.

Choose **New Customer order** in Order intake. Select the Customer and Department, then review **Customer readiness**. Active Customers remain selectable even when their setup is incomplete. Requirements under **Before starting pricing** must be resolved first. Expand **Later requirements** for additional quote and invoice setup; those items do not block creating the order for pricing. Retry a failed readiness check without discarding your entries.

Enter the Job name, sample counts by biological source, shared storage requirements, and safety declaration. You may also record a proposed price and optional notes. **Start pricing** saves the order and opens its detail workspace. An active Customer administrator is required before quote issuance. Exact sample IDs and tube counts are entered after quote acceptance; sample-list finalization authorizes the laboratory and shipping workflow. Billing setup may be completed after acceptance, before invoicing. Order intake owns this per-order process; reusable setup is maintained in Order configuration and the Company workspace.

The sidebar header identifies Order operations and, on wider screens, provides the pin or unpin icon. Pointer users can preview an unpinned sidebar by approaching the left edge; keyboard and touch users open it from the edge tab. Choosing a section closes an unpinned sidebar.

## Enter and find orders

- **Order intake** covers Customer and Partner commercial requests through review, pricing, quote issuance, and Customer acceptance. Held orders remain in active intake. Use **On hold** to focus on blockers and **All orders and history** to find accepted, completed or cancelled work. Laboratory execution continues in Lab Ops.
- **PSeq kits** shows commercial status and opens the same order in Lab Ops for review, substitutions, backorders, shipments and fulfillment.
- **Assembly** shows commercial context and opens the same request in Lab Ops for processing, quality review and output release.
- **Attention** owns invitation, readiness, staged-order, projection/scan, unreleased-result, overdue-invoice, unapplied-cash, and reconciliation-difference exceptions.
- **Result release** publishes a scientifically approved, complete PSeq package per sample without a payment gate.
- **Finance** owns PSeq invoices, receipts/import, explicit allocation, aging, and reconciliation.
- **Legacy integrations** covers retained accounting-connector and notification recovery outside the active native PSeq AR workflow.

Search Order intake by order, Job, Company, or request number. Order search runs across all matching records, with 25 orders per page. Pending CRM handoffs appear on the first active-intake page. The view, search and page stay in the address so you can return to the same queue. **Clear filters** restores active intake. A PSeq Lab Service row identifies an optional proposed price so Commercial users can find pricing decisions without opening every record. On the order, **Review proposed price** compares the proposed USD price per specimen with the active catalog price, requested quantity, difference, and subtotal. **Approve price and issue quote** preserves the proposal unchanged; **Amend price and issue quote** requires an internal reason. Tax is system-calculated: include and freeze it when the billing and tax profile is complete and Finance-approved, or issue an explicitly pre-tax quote and calculate tax at invoicing. The issued quote, not the proposal, is the offer a Customer administrator may accept. The operational detail shows the current quote value, revision, and status. **Internal context** in the audit timeline explains Phaeno-only decisions; it is informational, not a Customer-facing warning. Assign an owner and due date when the workflow supports them. A queue assignment coordinates work; it does not grant authorization or change the workflow state.

## Review configured bundles

A standard Lab Service order includes specimen processing and data assembly in one accepted scope. Review its saved offering version, final price and **Turnaround and progress** on the order. Exact sample entry still follows commitment; roster finalization owns Lab and shipping authorization.

A PSeq Kit purchase includes one assembly case per whole kit unit. Its order shows unit shipment facts, frozen profiles, deadlines and case history. Partner input corrections stay with the same case; included Assembly does not create a second quote or sale. **Open Lab work** leads to the existing execution workspace.

## Keep state boundaries visible

Operational status, quote status, accounting-source readiness, payment, scientific readiness, file scanning, and publication are separate facts. Do not mark one complete merely because another succeeded. External users receive a limited timeline, so record a reason suitable for the affected Customer or Partner separately from internal notes whenever an action can be seen outside Phaeno.

Order Ops ends at the Commercial boundary for a Customer laboratory order: it remains in **Order intake** while pricing and quote decisions are active. After acceptance and exact sample-roster finalization authorize that work, use **Lab Ops** for return-kit preparation, shipment-packet and tube comparison, specimen receipt and accession, protocol execution, materials, equipment, libraries, batches, sequencing sendouts, exceptions, and scientific approval. The **PSeq kits** and **Assembly** sections show the commercial record and link to their owning Lab Ops execution screen; the same physical action is recorded once. Lab readiness never publishes a file by itself.

## Triage safely

1. Confirm the active organization and workflow before opening a record.
2. Review operational readiness, invitation delivery, scientific package, release, invoice, receipt, allocation, reconciliation, and notification states independently.
3. Check open holds, cancellation requests, overdue actions, and failed deliveries.
4. Assign the work and due date when ownership is unclear.
5. Perform the smallest valid next action and preserve the existing record.

POMS checks the latest Job immediately before issuing a quote. If the record changes after that check, POMS reloads it and preserves the entered quote lines; review those values, then issue the quote again. Never repair a failed workflow by creating a duplicate order, quote, invoice, receipt, result package, Lab work order, shipment, or release. Assign an attention item, preserve attempts, and record its resolution.

## Review queued notifications

Order notices check the current active organization, department, user, and administrator assignments when dispatched. The department notification address is included when configured; otherwise the organization notification default applies. An inactive or mismatched department cannot use either route. Review failed notices with no eligible recipients before retrying them; changing an assignment after a notice was queued changes who can receive it.

## Use Finance

Choose **Invoices and aging**, **Receipts**, **Customer billing**, **Import receipts**, or **Reconciliation**. A Customer filter carries into the record and back. Open the invoice, receipt, Customer, or batch number to review its details, then choose the relevant action. Editing happens in a dialog and errors retain the entered values. Invoice adjustments and receipt reversals retain a required reason.

The dashboard summarizes recorded commercial and laboratory work and links to the owning screens. If data is unavailable, refresh or use the workspace directly; an unavailable count is not zero work.
