# Data provisioning

Phaeno controls organization access and the lifecycle of Phaeno-owned source samples and curated package versions. Customer and Partner operational data is a separate access class and is never automatically reused for curated packages.

Use the far-left sidebar beneath the toolbar to move among **Source registry**, **Curated catalog**, **Organization grants**, and **Governance**. The sidebar header identifies Data provisioning and, on wider screens, provides the pin or unpin icon. Pointer users can preview an unpinned sidebar by approaching the left edge; keyboard and touch users open it from the edge tab. Choosing a section closes an unpinned sidebar.

## Understand the lifecycle

1. Register Phaeno-owned source material and its ownership and de-identification evidence.
2. Complete the editable source revision and mark it ready.
3. Snapshot one exact ready revision into a curated draft.
4. Validate and publish an immutable package version.
5. Mark the version eligible for the intended external audience when appropriate.
6. Explicitly grant one exact version to an organization or one of its Departments.
7. Preserve publication, grant, download, revocation, retirement, and governance history.

Each step is explicit. Source readiness does not publish a package. Publication does not grant access. Eligibility does not grant access. A later version does not upgrade an existing grant.

## Use the correct access

Only authorized Phaeno users register sources, publish versions, manage eligibility, create or upgrade grants, revoke access, or open and resolve governance incidents. When assigning a package, choose **All departments (organization-wide)** or one active Department. Prospect, Customer, and Partner users can view only exact active versions granted organization-wide or to their currently selected Department. Department administrators see download history for that same scope; organization administrators retain access across Departments and are the only external users who can submit an organization-wide governance attestation.

Organization membership and lifecycle are managed in **Accounts**. A Prospect-to-Customer or Prospect-to-Partner conversion preserves the organization identity, memberships, grants, pinned versions, and audit history. Conversion never creates, replaces, upgrades, or revokes a curated-data grant.

For a Customer using PSeq Lab Service, the account overview shows derived **Operational readiness**. Resolve account, administrator, entitlement, offering, order/sample/shipping/result-destination/instruction, and manual blockers before quote issuance or Customer commitment. Billing, terms, tax, and Finance approval remain visible but may be completed after acceptance; they must be complete before invoicing. An internal staged order needs only the active Customer, Ready PSeq service entitlement, active offering, and no manual block. Historical Portal readiness text is informational and does not authorize a transaction.

Invitation access and email delivery are separate. Review the delivery state, sends, error, and expiry before retrying. A hard bounce requires revoking the invitation and issuing a new one to the corrected address; never copy or bypass an invitation token.

## Protect the boundary

Operational Customer or Partner files do not enter the source pool automatically. Every curated package is derived from an approved Phaeno-owned source revision. Keep storage keys, internal evidence, another organization’s grant state, and governance investigation detail out of tenant-facing content.
