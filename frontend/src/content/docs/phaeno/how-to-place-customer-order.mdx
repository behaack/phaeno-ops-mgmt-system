# How to place a Customer order

Use this guide when Phaeno is entering a paid laboratory order on behalf of a
Customer. Work in the Phaeno organization with order-pricing access. For a no-charge
evaluation, use [Trial Projects](/docs/phaeno/trial-projects) under Order operations.

## Before you start

Have the Customer's Company and owning Department, a unique Job name, the agreed
service, Sample type, biological sources, sample counts, sequencing runs and handling
requirements. A proposed price is optional and will be reviewed before a quote is
issued. Customer setup may be incomplete while you save a Draft; pricing submission
requires the Customer and Department to pass the readiness checks shown on screen.

## 1. Identify the Customer and work

Open **Order ops → Order intake → New Order**. This opens the dedicated **New Customer order** page.

![Empty New Customer order form showing Customer, Department, Job name, Catalog service and Sample type.](/docs/phaeno/screenshots/customer-order-20261015.jpg "Current order-entry page, refreshed October 22, 2026; no Customer has been selected.")

1. **Customer:** search for and select the active Company. Review the readiness
   panel that appears after selection. Setup links let an authorized administrator
   complete missing setup without losing this form.
2. **Department:** choose the Department that owns the Job. A sole available
   Department is selected automatically; choose explicitly when there are several.
3. **Job name:** enter a unique, Customer-safe name for this work.
4. **Catalog service:** choose the service for the whole order. It is required
   before pricing submission.
5. **Sample type:** select the configured type that describes the material the
   Customer will submit. This also supplies the default storage requirements.

## 2. Enter scope and handling

- Under **Order scope**, leave **Use phases** off for one delivery scope. Turn it
  on only for agreed sequential delivery cohorts, then set the phase count.
- For each scope or phase, enter every biological source and its sample count.
  Use **Add source** for another source. Enter **Sequencing runs per sample**;
  sample counts and run counts are different quantities.
- If Sales discussed pricing, choose **Propose a price** and enter the proposed
  prices. Additional sequencing runs are priced separately from the first run
  included per sample. Review the displayed subtotal; a proposal is not a quote.
- Complete **Safety declaration**. Use **Insert “No known hazards”** only when
  that statement applies, and review the inserted text.
- Use the Sample type's storage requirements, or select **Use different storage
  requirements** and explain the exception. Add **Private notes** for Phaeno-only
  context and **Public notes** for instructions visible to the Customer on
  **Order review**. Both support basic text formatting and lists.
  Do not enter patient identifiers, individual sample IDs or tube barcodes here.

## 3. Save, then submit for pricing

Choose **Save draft** to retain incomplete work. Customer, Department and Job name
are required to save. A Draft does not submit the order for pricing, expose it to
the Customer or authorize laboratory work. Reopen it from intake and choose
**Actions → Edit draft** to continue.

When the scope is complete, review the summary and choose **Submit for pricing**.
The portal rechecks configuration and Customer readiness. If submission is blocked,
keep the saved Draft, follow the named setup or validation issue, then refresh
readiness and review before trying again. A price proposal does not bypass pricing
review or the independent quote controls.

## What happens next

The order enters pricing review. Follow its **Next step for Phaeno** to prepare
and issue the quote. The authorized Customer administrator accepts separately.
Acceptance, kit requests, sample entry and laboratory authorization are later
workflow steps; submitting for pricing does not complete them.

See [Order operations](/docs/phaeno/order-operations) for phase pricing and queue
triage, and [Customer lab authorization](/docs/phaeno/order-customer-lab-authorization)
for readiness, quote acceptance and additional scope.
