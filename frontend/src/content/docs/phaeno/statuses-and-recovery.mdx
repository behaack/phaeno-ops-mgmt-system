# Statuses and recovery

Recover the failed step from the record that owns it. Company access, order pricing, laboratory work, scientific release, billing, notifications, and retention have separate lifecycles. Repeating a successful earlier action can create duplicate work without fixing the failure.

## Find the owning workspace

| Work needing attention | Where to continue |
| --- | --- |
| Company access, invitation, or relationship request | **CRM → Companies → People** or the central **Requests** queue. Use **Approved / needs work** for approved requests awaiting completion. |
| Customer pricing, quote, hold, or cancellation | **Order operations → Order intake**, then the Job. **New Customer order** starts each order; it is not configuration. |
| Trial scope, approvals, acceptance, allowance, or closure | **Order operations → Trial projects**, then the Trial. |
| Partner reagent or Assembly commercial review | **Order operations → PSeq kits** or **Assembly**. **Open Lab work** continues fulfillment or execution. |
| Package, tube match, accession, execution, or scientific exception | The linked record in **Lab operations**. |
| Scientifically approved PSeq package awaiting publication | **Order operations → Result release**, then the package. |
| PSeq invoice, receipt, allocation, or reconciliation | The appropriate tab in **Order operations → Finance**. |
| Cross-workflow follow-up or failed notice | **Order operations → Attention**; follow the item to its record. |
| Curated-package grants, quarantine, or governance | **Data provisioning**, separate from the Customer Job's results. |

Check search, status, date, owner, and Department filters before creating another record. A failed load differs from an empty list. **Active intake** includes held work; use **On hold** to focus on it, or **All orders and history** for accepted and terminal records. Lists preserve filters when you return from a record.

## Recover Company readiness

Open the Company's **Departments & services** and select the affected Department in **Department readiness**. The same checks appear during **New Customer order**. Requirements are separated into before pricing, before quote issuance, and before invoicing. Follow the setup link beside the requirement; a historical readiness label does not satisfy it.

An active Customer, effective Ready service entitlement, valid offering, and no operational block allow pricing to begin. Quote issuance also requires operational configuration and an active Customer organization administrator. Billing and Finance approval may follow acceptance but must be complete before invoicing. See [Customer lab authorization](./order-customer-lab-authorization).

## Recover laboratory and release work

Packet and tube matching identifies authorized work; it does not record receipt. Continue to the linked Lab work, **Record receipt**, then **Confirm accession**. Resolve an unmatched or unsafe item through the receipt exception process.

Resolve blocked execution and scientific exceptions in Lab operations using the authorized role. Keep internal investigation detail separate from the Customer summary. If a milestone publication fails, retry the existing event where recovery is offered; do not recreate the authorization, approval, or Lab work order.

Review the complete, checksummed, malware-clean package and scientific approval in **Result release** before publication. Payment, invoice balance, and Customer credit do not gate PSeq results. Partner Assembly retains its separate credit and payment-hold rules. See [Scientific approval](./lab-scientific-approval) and [Data assembly review](./order-data-assembly).

Do not mark rejected files clean or change an order status to bypass a file gate. Correct the storage or scanning issue for failed or unavailable scans. Use the owning release action to withdraw a package that must no longer be delivered.

## Recover Finance and notifications

Use **Finance** for native PSeq invoices, receipts, allocations, corrections, imports, and reconciliation. An export does not record payment or mark an invoice posted elsewhere. Partner reagent shipments and Assembly outputs retain separate commercial source records for the manual accounting handoff.

Before retrying a receipt or import, check whether it succeeded. Correct changed import input and preview it again; only the operator who reviewed the preview can confirm it. A receipt is recorded from accepted evidence, not from a successful email or connector retry. See [Billing, payment, and release](./order-billing-payment-release).

Failed notifications do not reverse the underlying action. Correct eligible recipients or delivery issues and retry the existing notice where offered. For a hard-bounced invitation, use Company **People** to revoke it and issue a new invitation to the corrected Contact address. A queued recovery request is not proof of delivery or an available accounting connector.

## Respect retained history

Expired quotes require a current quote. Cancelled work, closed Trials, withdrawn files, completed Tasks, and expired downloads retain their history; follow the applicable new request, replacement, reissue, or correction workflow. Retention holds preserve or quarantine a release without extending its dates.

Escalate with the record number, status, safe error, time, and action attempted. Keep credentials, invitation tokens, patient identifiers, investigations, and scientific files out of ordinary support notes.
