# Partner data assembly

New Partner Assembly is included with a purchased PSeq Kit case. Order Ops owns the original Kit purchase and case administration. Historical standalone Assembly keeps its original Commercial quote, cancellation and accounting source record. Lab Ops owns input validation, processing milestones, quality review, and immutable output release. Inputs and outputs are manufacturing records; they are not source samples for Data Provisioning.

Find the Commercial order in **Order operations** > **Assembly**. Open **Lab operations** > **Data assembly** to validate the submitted input and perform the manufacturing workflow. For included cases, accepted intake proceeds to the processing queue without another quote or purchase. Historical standalone requests retain their quote and Partner-acceptance steps.

## Validate the submission

Confirm the active Partner, project reference, purchase-order requirement, included frozen assembly profile, or the historical request profile, required metadata, and uploaded input revision. Validate file type, size, completeness, malware-scan state, and every profile-specific requirement.

When the submission cannot pass input validation, Lab staff place the manufacturing record on hold and document the issue. Commercial staff use the linked order to request a targeted Partner correction or reject work that is unsafe, unsupported, or outside the offered profile. A correction creates a new input revision; it must not rewrite the revision that was reviewed previously.

## Historical standalone quotes

Prepare the per-job quote from the current assembly offering and preserve its profile, price, currency, and expiration snapshot. Issuing the POMS quote makes it available to the Partner immediately. Acceptance records the immutable Commercial commitment and queues the work.

## Process and review

Record processing milestones against the accepted input revision and selected profile. Internal implementation details and intermediate files remain internal. When processing is complete, perform the configured quality review and document any hold or required correction. Open **Review outputs** to inspect every uploaded output and its scan state, including files uploaded in an earlier session. POMS builds the output manifest from the uploaded files and pinned processing run; operators do not type a manifest or repeat the pipeline provenance.

## Approve an output release

An output release is immutable and identifies:

- the exact source input revision;
- the assembly profile and version;
- the output manifest and included files;
- checksums, scan state, and generation time; and
- the quality-review and approval facts.

Approve a new release rather than replacing a prior release. Included outputs use the original Kit shipment commercial source; they create no second invoice, commercial source or sale. Historical standalone output approval retains its original accounting-source step. Both apply the appropriate recorded balance or Partner assembly-credit gate. Credit-approved work can be released under Net 30; work without approved credit remains on payment hold because manual payment confirmation is not yet implemented.
