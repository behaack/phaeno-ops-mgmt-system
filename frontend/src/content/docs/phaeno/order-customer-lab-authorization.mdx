# Customer lab authorization

Customer laboratory work enters Order Ops as a Commercial request. Order Ops validates the Job-level specimen count, biological-source composition, storage, safety, and service profile before pricing. Individual samples do not exist yet.

## Initiate an order for a Customer

An authorized Phaeno order-pricing user can begin the pricing profile when the Customer is already known and does not need to create the request first.

1. Open **Order operations**, select **Order intake**, and choose **New Customer order**.
2. Wait for the eligible Customer list to load, search incrementally by Company name, then select the Customer from the matching results. **New Customer order** remains unavailable while the list is loading or could not be loaded. Names come from canonical active CRM Companies. A Company appears when its attached active Customer operational scope is not manually blocked and has a current **Ready** PSeq Lab Service entitlement plus the active canonical specimen offering. It does not need an online administrator at this stage.
3. Enter a unique **Job name**, every biological source and sample count, the shared storage requirements and safety declaration, and any Customer-safe Job notes. If Sales discussed a price, select **Propose a price**, enter the proposed USD price per specimen, and optionally add Customer-safe pricing context. A proposal is not a quote and does not authorize work. Do not enter individual sample IDs, tube barcodes, patient identifiers, or PHI.
4. Confirm that the pricing details contain no patient identifiers, PHI, or unnecessary personal data, then select **Start pricing**. POMS creates the Customer-owned Job with one immutable submitted request revision and opens it in **Quote in preparation**.
5. Review the committed sample count, source composition, storage, and safety facts on the operational detail. If a price was proposed, select **Review proposed price** and compare the proposal, active catalog price, difference, and calculated subtotal. Approve it unchanged or amend the unit price; an amendment requires an internal reason. Before issuing the quote, complete every non-billing readiness blocker, including activating a Customer administrator who can receive and approve it. Billing readiness may be completed after acceptance but is required before invoicing. The editor preselects the required **PSeq Lab Service**, explains that it is priced per specimen, and sets its read-only quantity from the Job's committed specimen count. The required line cannot be removed. Additional active catalog lines may be added with a per-unit price and a quantity chosen for that quote. Permanent accounting codes remain available in **Order configuration** but are not shown as quote-entry guidance.

If the designated item is missing, inactive, or not priced per specimen, quote issuance is paused until **Order configuration** is corrected. POMS validates the item identity, unit, and committed quantity again on the server.

POMS sends no Customer notice while a Phaeno-initiated Job remains **Quote in preparation**. Issuing or revising the quote rechecks ordering eligibility and the approver list, makes the quote available immediately, moves the Job to **Quote issued**, and notifies every active Customer administrator. The Customer can approve it; Phaeno cannot approve on the Customer's behalf. The accepting administrator becomes the Job's acting Customer contact for ordinary later notices. High-impact events, including cancellation by Phaeno and result release or payment hold, continue to notify all active Customer administrators.

## Review the request

Confirm that the Customer has an active laboratory-service entitlement and that the selected service is currently offered. Review the requested specimen count, each source group and count, shared handling and safety declarations, and Customer notes. Request a targeted correction when information is incomplete or unsafe rather than rewriting Customer data internally.

Keep internal review notes separate from the tenant-safe reason shown to the Customer. The initial workflow is not a PHI intake path; do not ask for or store patient-identifying information.

## Issue and accept the quote

The Customer or Sales price proposal is part of the submitted request revision, not a quote. POMS preserves its unit price, note, proposer, and time. The issued quote records whether Phaeno approved that proposal or amended it, along with the responsible Commercial reviewer and decision time. When dual control is enabled, a Phaeno proposer cannot review the same proposal.

The quote is a versioned, immutable Commercial offer for one job. Confirm the configured service, quantities, prices, currency, expected turnaround information, and expiration before issuance. Tax is calculated by POMS: when the complete billing and tax profile is Finance-approved, the quote includes and freezes tax; otherwise the quote is explicitly pre-tax and tax is calculated from the approved profile at invoicing. Saving the issued POMS quote makes it available for Customer acceptance.

Customer acceptance performs one Commercial operation that:

- records the accepted quote revision and frozen pricing profile; and
- opens post-acceptance sample-list preparation.

After the Customer enters or imports the exact compliant sample list, finalization atomically creates the immutable laboratory work authorization, linked Lab work order, shipment, and physical tube slots. Do not create a Lab work order manually if finalization fails. Resolve the reported roster or shipping-configuration issue and retry the durable operation.

Physical samples must not arrive before quote acceptance and exact roster finalization create the authorized shipment. Quarantine and escalate an unexpected package under the approved receiving and custody exception. Do not accession, process, bill, or attach it to executable Job work.

## Respect the Lab boundary

After acceptance, Order Ops continues to own Customer communication, Commercial holds, cancellation review, billing, files, and publication. Open the linked work from the order or from **Lab operations** > **Receipt & accession**. Lab Ops owns return-kit preparation, shipment identification, receipt, accession, physical lineage, controlled execution, exceptions, and scientific approval.

The Lab milestone **Ready for release** means the scientific gate passed. It does not upload, scan, attach, or publish a result. Order Ops must still apply the file, accounting-source, and credit release gates. Without approved credit, files remain held because manual payment confirmation is not implemented.
