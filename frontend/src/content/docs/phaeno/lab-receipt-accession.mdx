# Receipt and accession

Receipt and accession connect the Customer’s submitted specimen identity to the physical material handled in the laboratory. Record these steps in sequence and never create an unlinked laboratory specimen.

The first recorded shipment arrival moves an awaiting Job into **Lab operations → Lab work** with the milestone **Received**. Its commercial order moves to **In progress**, while the Customer/Partner Job list displays **Received**. Customer progress uses Received, Library Prep, Sequencing, Data Assembly, Quality Review and Results Available, with current counts and individual sample stages in After you send. Only recorded provider sequencing establishes Sequencing; sendout alone does not. Scientific approval remains distinct from released results. It remains in Lab work while you accession tubes, including when other containers are outstanding. A completed container leaves the accession queue; its tubes and freezer-box values remain in the Job's **Tubes** tab.

Verified tube receipt updates the sample's receipt progress. The commercial sample becomes **Accessioned** after all its expected tubes across active shipments are accessioned. Order updates can take a few seconds. Container receipt alone does not accept tubes. During tube accession, confirm the receipt checks: Accepted is the normal outcome, with no reason code required. A specimen becomes Accepted when at least one of its tubes is Accepted. For phased Jobs, each phase’s delivery clock starts only after every required sample for that phase has physically arrived; older Jobs retain their acceptance-based terms. This intake decision is separate from laboratory QC and scientific review.


Use **Lab operations → Transportation kit inventory** to assemble and review individual physical kits and their preparation history. Under **Receipt & accession → Kit requests**, configuration administrators review Customer requests, kits sent to Customers, and kits the Customer has confirmed received. Use the compact **Kit requests | Fulfilled requests** pill toggle above the list header to show one queue at a time. **Kit requests** is selected by default; **Fulfilled requests** shows the shipment-specific kit list and fulfillment dates. Shipment links select **Fulfilled requests** automatically. Search that queue by shipment, Job, Customer or kit number using its header search box. A filtered miss leaves the search available; use **Clear search** to restore the queue. Its status filter starts with active requests and offers **Kits sent** and **Kits received**. A request leaves the active list when all its kits are received; partial receipt leaves the remaining work visible. Saved kit links continue to open the matching workspace.

Use **Lab operations → Receipt & accession** for **Kit requests**, **Receive shipments**, and **Accession samples**. **Kit requests** opens by default; links that explicitly select receiving or accession still open that task. Under **Receive shipments**, use the compact **Receive a shipment | Expected shipments | Shipments received** pill toggle to show one view at a time. **Receive a shipment** opens by default and accepts the PH-P- insert scan. **Expected shipments** lists containers still to arrive with their carrier and tracking numbers. **Shipments received** shows all recorded container arrivals, most recent first, with the receipt time and individual tube accession counts, including completed containers. Switching between these receiving views keeps your scan draft and receipt feedback during the visit; changing views does not record arrival. Accession samples opens a received container for individual tube scanning and lists only containers still awaiting accession. Switching between the main task tabs retains the accession lookup draft and results.

Shipment queues show the PH-P- shipping-insert number in small text beneath the shipment number when an insert is available. This identifies the insert; record physical arrival only when the container arrives.

Search **Shipments received** from its header by shipment or PH-P number, Customer, Job, carrier or tracking number. Search applies after a short pause and starts at the first page. History shows 20 arrivals per page, newest first; use **Previous** and **Next** below the list. Your search and page stay available when switching receiving views, refreshing, or returning from shipment details. A search with no matches keeps the header available; use **Clear search** to restore the history.

**Fulfilled requests** also shows 20 records per page with **Previous** and **Next** below its list. Header search applies across all matching shipment-specific kits, including records beyond the current page, and starts at page one. Search, page and any selected-shipment scope stay available when returning to the list.

Under **Accession samples**, select a container from **Received containers awaiting accession**, or use the barcode field and **Open container** button in that card's header. The lookup stays available when the queue is empty. Other supported identifiers are explained below the field. Lookup opens the existing accession workspace; it does not record arrival or accession tubes.

## Fulfill Customer transportation-kit orders

Open **Lab operations → Receipt & accession → Kit requests** to review Customer transportation-kit orders. Accepting a Job does not queue kits. Customers and entitled Partners request kits when ready, confirming their Department delivery address. New kit requests notify Phaeno administrators for fulfillment. Open a request to review its Customer, Department, Job, phase, requested kit configurations and quantities, and the delivery address saved when the Customer confirmed. Transportation kits and outbound delivery are included with the accepted laboratory order; this action creates no additional quote, invoice or payment step.

Use the **Actions** menu on a request list item to **Prepare kits**, **Record kit shipment**, or **Cancel request** when eligible. These actions open dialogs in the list; the request identifier still opens its full details. Each action checks the latest request before allowing changes. Cancellation is available only before dispatch and requires confirmation. Preparation opens the kit detail after assembly so its tube roster can be completed. Use **Record kit shipment** from the list or the detail page to scan or enter each complete `KIT-` barcode, review the scanned kits, and enter the carrier, tracking number and dispatch time. The request shows ready stock counts by requested type instead of listing every physical kit. A scan verifies that the exact kit is ready and belongs to a requested type; remove a mistaken scan before saving. Shipment saving checks the physical kits again. If there is insufficient prepared stock, prepare and register more standard kits before returning to the request. Registration alone does not fulfill the request: it can still show **Pending** and zero dispatched kits until dispatch is recorded here. A partial dispatch leaves the remaining requested quantities outstanding. The kit order and saved delivery address remain linked to each dispatched kit.

Dispatch adds those kits to the Customer delivery location's inventory as **On the way**. The Customer acknowledges the containers that arrive, moving them to **Available**. Unassigned compatible received stock may supply an eligible phase at that Customer/Department/location. Once a kit is requested or allocated for a phase, it is dedicated to that phase and cannot mix samples from other phases. Cancelled or superseded phase requests need review before dispatch; retain existing dispatch and receipt history. Earlier requests without phase attribution remain visible for explicit physical-kit assignment. Customer acknowledgment is separate from the laboratory's later receipt of returned samples. Kits not yet acknowledged remain unavailable, and a partial receipt permits only the received portion.

Maintain delivery locations from the Customer's **Departments** workspace or the Department's delivery-location screen. Open a location and use **Actions → Edit location** in the page header. These locations belong to the Customer and Department, and are separate from global lab receiving destinations. Changing a saved location does not rewrite a confirmed kit order's delivery address. The Customer location workspace provides inventory and receipt access even if the originating Job has been cancelled.


The request shows what to do next. If no matching kits are ready, choose
**Prepare kits** to open kit inventory for the missing configurations. Prepare each physical
kit, register and verify its tube barcodes, complete its assembly run when applicable, then use **Back to kit request**. Once matching
kits are ready, **Record kit shipment** scans the kit barcodes and records the carrier,
tracking number and shipment time after handoff. A partially ready request can
ship its available kits while the remaining sizes are prepared. Preparation does
not reserve stock or record a shipment.

## Suppliers and products

Phaeno platform administrators manage **Suppliers** and **Products** under **More → Purchasing**. Choose **New supplier**, enter its name, and save. Open the supplier name to see its products. Choose **New product** and enter the **Product type**, **Product name**, a required **Product description**, and **Inventory unit** (such as each or mL). Choose a common inventory unit from **Units**, or type another unit when needed. This unit belongs to the product: new purchased material lots use it automatically, so staff enter only the amount received. Existing catalog products with no verified unit must be configured before another lot can be received. Enable **Can expire** when inventory for that product must carry an expiration date. This setting requires dates on new purchased material lots and prepared stock kits. It applies to every configured kit product, including additional supplies. Vendors can supply reagents, tubes, shipping containers, and other products from the same supplier record. Product numbers must be unique within each supplier, including inactive products. Changing Can expire leaves existing inventory dates and saved kit requirements unchanged; older records are not given inferred dates.

Each supplier and product row has an **Actions** menu. Choose **Edit** to correct details, or **Deactivate** and review the application confirmation to make the record inactive. **Show inactive** reveals those records; choose **Actions → Activate** to restore them. The supplier detail header offers the same actions. Inactivating an external supplier prevents selection of all its products for new kits; it does not alter prepared kits. Inactive product types also prevent their products from being selected for new kits. The tube **Lot** remains specific to each kit preparation and is not stored in the product catalog. **Phaeno** is the seeded internal producer and cannot be renamed or deactivated. Open it to define each Phaeno-made reagent or transportation kit as a separate product. For a transportation kit, enter its **SKU** and **Kit name**; the unit is **each**. The SKU is fixed after creation, while the Kit name can be corrected until a shipping specification is linked. Reagents retain a product name and the inventory unit appropriate to their yield. Deactivation blocks new manufacturing without deleting past runs or kits. Configure assembly procedures in **Lab settings → Workflows**, then start work in **Reagent manufacturing** or **Transportation kit inventory**.

For a purchased **Shipping Container** product, enter **Tube capacity**: the maximum number of individual tubes the outer container holds. Existing container products without a verified capacity show **Needs configuration** until an administrator edits them. Set the inventory unit of individual tube and outer-container products to **each**. A complete **Transportation kit** product is a different catalog type and cannot serve as the outer container component of a Phaeno-assembled kit.

## Manage product types

Open **More → Purchasing → Products** and select the **Product types** tab to manage the categories offered by your vendors. **Tube**, **Shipping Container**, **Reagent**, and **Transportation kit** are provided initially. Tube and Shipping Container are seeded, editable types; the Reagent and Transportation kit names and active status are fixed for Phaeno production. Transportation kit identifies a complete kit product; Shipping Container identifies a purchased outer-container product used as a component. Choose **New product type** and enter a unique name, description, and **Use in transportation kits**. For reagents and other supplies, choose **Other product**. A vendor can offer products of several types.

Open a type's name to review its description, status, kit use, and product count. Each type has an **Actions** menu in its list row and detail header. Choose **Edit** to change its details, or **Deactivate** to make it inactive after reviewing the confirmation. Use **Show inactive**, then **Actions → Activate**, to restore a type. Once products reference a type, its kit use cannot change; create a different type when that classification needs to differ. Existing products retain inactive type references for review and correction, but new products must choose an active type. Inactivating a type preserves kit history.

External reagent vendors use the same supplier directory used by **Purchasing → Purchased materials**. Catalog entries identify what was purchased but do not create material lots, receive stock, or record QC. Receive purchased lots in **Purchasing → Purchased materials**; make Phaeno reagents in **Reagent manufacturing**. Both lot types need a separate QC decision before use.

## Configure and assemble transportation kits

A 20-tube Phaeno kit needs one purchased Shipping Container product whose verified capacity is at least 20 and a tube-product quantity of 20. The kit specification records the usable tube count, which may be below the outer container's maximum. A Draft can be saved while a selected supplier product's inventory unit or capacity is being configured; activation waits for the complete, countable contents.

Create the purchased Tube and Shipping Container products under their external suppliers in **Purchasing**. Tube and outer-container stock used in kits must be counted in **each**; the container product records its maximum tube capacity. In **Lab settings → Workflows**, name a reusable Transportation kit assembly method, choose exactly one approved instruction-only **Assembly step**, and approve its version. In **Samples & shipping settings → Kit specifications**, enter the complete kit name and its stable kit SKU, select the purchased Shipping Container and named assembly method, and choose the Sample type for that revision. Required contents are one tube product with quantity equal to usable capacity, the selected outer container with quantity one, and any other required purchased products. A complete specification may be activated before method approval, but physical preparation waits for approval. Change contents or the Sample type in a new specification Draft; change the assembly step in a workflow version. Existing physical kits keep their exact pinned revisions and component evidence.

Open **Lab operations → Transportation kit inventory**. **In progress** lists saved unfinished assemblies separately, with **Resume assembly** for each kit. They are outside Inventory and cannot be dispatched. Inventory starts with completed kits **At Phaeno** that are ready for dispatch. Choose **Shipped kits** to see dispatched kits, including those later received, assigned or in use. Search completed kits by number, specification, SKU, location or Job. Open a kit to review its assembly, products, tubes, location, receipt, tracking and assignment. The originating request's Job remains distinct from the Job currently using the container.

Choose **Assemble transportation kit**, select an Active specification with an approved assembly method, and review its instructions and required contents. Enter requested lot and expiration information. **Print container barcode** saves the permanent kit identity and opens printing, then continues assembly in the same modal. **Save for later** saves the unfinished kit outside Inventory. The products, quantities, capacity and approved revisions stay pinned to this physical kit; future configuration changes do not rewrite it.

**Product expiration dates** lists each product marked Can expire in the kit contents. Record each labeled date. You may save an expired date as evidence of stock physically received, but expired components prevent assembly completion. Saved requirements and dates remain visible under **Details & history**.

The physical kit page has one **Actions** menu beside its name and status. **Preparation** shows assembly progress, required contents and the tube roster. **Details & history** shows products, expiration records, location, assignment and dispatch history. The permanent kit number remains beneath the title.

**Required contents** is a read-only summary. The assembly modal shows the saved instructions, remaining products, quantities and source lots. Use **Resume assembly** to reopen a saved kit. Quantities start with the remaining approved contents; use **0** for another component not yet packed. Scan each tube's permanent barcode as it is packed: its quantity is calculated from unique valid new scans plus registered tubes whose use is not yet recorded. Select an available source lot when a product has tracked lots; every tube in one kit must use the same source lot. Amber warnings identify products without tracked lots, whose quantities are recorded without deducting source-lot stock.

**Save for later** retains scanned tubes, recorded component use, source-lot deductions and label confirmation. Even fully packed contents remain outside Inventory until **Complete** succeeds. To complete in the same modal, pack the exact bill of materials, print and affix the container label, scan its **Attached container barcode**, and review the recorded tube count and IDs. Scan each tube once as it goes into the container; no second scan is required. The print request alone does not confirm a physical label printed; the affixed-label scan is also required. The recorded roster must contain the exact required number of unique tube IDs from the saved tube product. **Complete** atomically saves remaining consumption, tube IDs, label confirmation and one assembly-step record with the operator and completion time, then makes the kit available in Inventory. Completing confirms that all recorded tubes and required contents are packed in that container. No separate Complete assembly or Stop assembly actions are needed. If a check fails, none of that request's changes is saved; entries remain visible for review and retry. Previously saved consumption is retained and not deducted again. A kit that will not be finished can be withdrawn with a reason.

A physical kit uses one tube source lot throughout partial packing. Once tube use is recorded, the source-lot picker retains that lot. If earlier tubes were recorded without a source lot and tracked stock is introduced later, review the recorded use before packing more tubes.

The kit's **KIT-** number is its permanent container barcode. Print it from the assembly modal before completion; the detail Actions menu permits later reprints. **Actions → Correct tube ID** requires a reason and clears any previous optional rescan result. Use **Actions → Rescan packed tubes (optional)** when a packing mistake is suspected; this checks every physical tube against the saved roster and records the operator and time. Dispatch requires completed assembly and the exact unique tube roster; a second scan is optional. Return to **Kit requests** and use **Actions → Record kit shipment** on the request item to scan each physical kit barcode and record dispatch to its confirmed address. Only verified, ready kits of requested types are added to the shipment list. An unrecognized or unavailable barcode stays in the input with an error for correction. The Customer later assigns the received container to a Job.

**On the way** means dispatched but not yet acknowledged. **Available** means received and unassigned at the Customer location. **Assigned** means the Customer confirmed this container for a Job/shipment; **In use** means tube scanning has begun. Reset before the first tube scan releases an assignment to available inventory. After scanning, ordinary reset/reassignment remains locked. **Needs review** identifies incomplete location or receipt history that must be resolved before use. The original request, tracking and receipt remain distinct from the consuming Job.

If an older kit is already sent but its request still shows an outstanding quantity, the kit page offers **Update kit request** when the saved dispatch has one eligible matching request. Review the existing carrier, tracking number and dispatch time, then confirm **Update kit request**. This links the existing dispatch; it does not send another kit or confirm Customer receipt. A failed update keeps the confirmation available for review and retry. Existing Trial and Partner supply workflows remain unchanged. Revise the shipping specification and assembly workflow for future kits rather than changing an issued kit's capacity or contents.

## Delivery deadline from complete receipt

For a newly approved Job, the Customer has already accepted one delivery target in business days. Record each physical tube receipt separately. Once every required tube for every sample has been received, the Job receives its delivery due date using Monday–Friday business days and the Phaeno holiday calendar. A partially received shipment does not start the clock. If holiday coverage is missing, extend the calendar so the pending date can be calculated. Receipt and scientific acceptance remain separate records; acceptance is not blocked while the date is pending. Earlier Jobs keep their accepted timing terms. For an older Job without a deadline, an operator or supervisor can use **Actions → Set due date** with a customer-safe reason.

## Receive a shipment

Open **Lab operations → Receipt & accession → Receive shipments**. **Expected shipments** lists each expected container separately, including the Customer, Job, carrier, tracking number and expected tube count. Containers still being prepared remain visible with their current status; missing tracking is shown as not recorded.

When a container physically arrives, scan the **PH-P-** barcode under **Scan to receive this shipment** in the body of its shipping insert in **Shipping insert barcode**, then submit with Enter or **Receive shipment**. This action records the container's arrival time and staff audit event. The confirmation identifies the shipment and offers **Accession samples** at the right of its first row. Repeating the same scan shows the original receipt without creating another event. Invalid, replaced or cancelled inserts cannot receive a container. A failed response leaves receipt unconfirmed in the screen; scanning again safely checks or completes that receipt.

This receiving field accepts only the shipping insert's PH-P- code. The SHP… shipment reference, PH-S- shipment barcode, PH-O- Order barcode, PH-M- sample barcode, physical KIT- container barcode and individual tube barcodes do not acknowledge container arrival here. Receipt of a container does not accession its tubes or accept its samples for processing.

## Open a received container

Choose **Accession samples → Received packages**. **Received containers awaiting accession** shows containers that have arrived and still have tubes to accession. Open the shipment reference or scan its PH-P- insert. This lookup is read-only. Within this view, a **PH-S-** barcode opens the shipment's current confirmed insert, and **PH-O-** or **PH-M-** lookups let you choose a related manifest. The readable **SHP…** reference and physical **KIT-** barcode are not accepted by the lookup field.

The shipping insert opens one accession dialog. Its main list shows tubes still awaiting an intake decision, including pending placements. Saved accepted tubes move into the collapsed **Accessioned tubes (N)** section; open it to verify their sample identities, barcodes and recorded storage locations. Saved holds and rejections are available separately in **Recorded exceptions (N)**. The count above the list includes all recorded intake decisions. Once the shipment details have loaded, scan the **Freezer box barcode** and choose **Open box** or press Enter. Keep only that physical box open; its barcode remains visible as the destination. Opening a box does not assign any tubes.

Inspect each tube before placing it. If it has an exception, choose **Record exception** on its expected row instead of adding it to the acceptable box group. Select **Rejected** or **On hold** and a predefined reason. Confirm that the received tube or damaged remains match that shipment row. A broken tube whose barcode cannot be scanned can still be rejected against its expected identity. Missing tubes remain outstanding; do not mark them received merely because they appear on the insert.

A broken or destroyed rejected tube requires no freezer location and is unavailable for processing. POMS retains its expected-tube link, rejection reason, notes, actor and time. No remaining quantity is asserted for a non-stored rejection. If rejected material is actually retained, its real storage location may be recorded. An internally held tube must have a real retained-material location. Rejection itself does not record disposal.

For an acceptable tube, scan **Supplier tube barcode** once and press Enter or **Scan and place tube**. Wait for a successful identity check, match the displayed sample and tube identity, place it into the open box, then scan the next tube. This single scan identifies the expected tube and adds it to the pending box group. There is no separate identification pass or second tube scan. Duplicate, unexpected, previously decided, unavailable and conflicting-location tubes cannot enter the group.

The expected-tube table shows **Placement pending confirmation** and the pending box barcode until the group is saved. The footer shows **X with exceptions | Y to be accepted**: saved holds/rejections and the exact pending group count. Scanning alone does not save an intake decision or prove physical placement.

Choose **Review and finish box** to stop scanning and review the exact tube identities and count. Confirm that you inspected these tubes, recorded exceptions separately and physically placed every listed tube into the displayed box. **Accept and store N tubes** saves only that box group together. For the remaining undecided tubes, scan the next box and repeat; tubes already saved do not need another scan. Each tube retains its own recorded box. Routine acceptance needs no reason code. Check the box has space and meets the required storage conditions. Storage is recorded as entered; POMS does not validate a freezer registry, box capacity, storage conditions or positions inside it.

Opening an exception pauses box placement. If a pending tube needs an exception, reconcile its physical location before saving and record its actual location if retained. A successfully saved exception removes only that tube from the pending acceptance group. The remaining pending tubes retain their box assignment; rescan the same box before continuing. Cancelling an exception preserves the pending group.

**Pause box**, leaving the window, hiding the tab or losing the connection pauses placement. A group with pending tubes must resume by rescanning the same box. Earlier placement assignments stay with their original box, and a delayed scan from a paused context is not added. A different box can be opened after the current group is saved. Saved groups survive closing; discarded unsaved groups require you to reconcile any tubes already physically placed before continuing.

If any tube in a submitted group changed, the whole group is refused. A background change stops further placement; close and review the current intake records before continuing. Once a save is attempted, its box and tube list are locked. For an uncertain connection result, use **Retry same group** to resend the identical request. A completed group is returned without duplicate intake records. If you close after an uncertain result, check the recorded decisions before handling or assigning the tubes again. Group acceptance cannot relocate a previously stored tube.

POMS assigns or reuses the specimen's accession number and preserves each supplier barcode. A rejected received tube completes that tube's receipt handling without becoming acceptable material. The specimen becomes Accepted when at least one tube is Accepted. Complete receipt handling and scientific acceptance are separate facts.

Saved groups and exceptions survive closing. Unsaved box assignments prompt before being discarded; reconcile any physically placed tubes before reopening the workspace. When all expected tubes have decisions, choose **Done**. Historical tubes with no intake decision are not automatically accepted: inspect and scan them explicitly in this accession dialog, or ask a supervisor to document a correction from the tube details.

## Find accessioned samples

Choose **Accession samples → Accessioned samples** to review recorded samples, most recently received first. The compact toggle switches between this directory and the received-package queue; switching views does not record arrival or intake. Your package-lookup draft remains available when you switch back.

Search by customer sample ID, accession number, tube barcode, freezer-box location, Customer or Job. Use **All intake statuses** to filter by the sample's recorded intake status and **All sample use** to choose **Used** or **Not used**. Search and filter changes return to page one; **Previous** and **Next** move through 20 samples per page. **Clear filters** restores the full list. Search, intake, use and page remain in the URL and when returning from sample details.

Each row represents one sample. Open its customer sample ID to review its specimen record, then use **Back to accessioned samples** to return with your filters and page. Expand **N recorded tubes** to see each tube's barcode, decision and actual storage location. Rejected material without retained storage shows **Not stored**. The directory includes samples with an accession identity and at least one recorded submitted tube; it does not assert that every expected tube has arrived or been accessioned.

The status filter uses the sample's intake decision, separately from processing progress and individual tube decisions. A sample is Accepted when at least one tube is Accepted, even if another is held or rejected. A sample with only rejected tubes can remain Received while its tube details show the rejection. Accessioning does not mean preparation or sequencing has started.

**Sample use** shows **Used** once processing has started on a source tube or a biological material transfer has been recorded. A started attempt remains use even if later held or failed. Selecting a source, reserving a tray position or cancelling an unstarted attempt does not count as use. **Not used** means no use is recorded; intake acceptance and availability still determine whether material can be selected.

Expand the tubes to distinguish a used source from an unused reserve. Used does not mean all material is gone: a tube with recorded exhaustion also shows **Material exhausted**, and Failed or Disposed is shown separately. The sample's used-tube count refers to recorded tubes. Historical processing without a selected-source link shows **Historical source not recorded** on the used sample and **Use unknown** on tubes lacking their own use evidence. POMS does not guess which historical tube was used.

## Other specimen and label actions

The Lab work record retains individual receipt and accession actions for supported legacy intake. Its **Tubes** tab shows **Received tubes**, with clear intake and storage information. Open a tube barcode to see its receipt evidence, storage and lineage. POMS-generated container labels are available from the detail page’s **Actions** menu.

The label window renders the current container identity as a DataMatrix with readable text through the browser and installed printer driver. A newly allocated POMS tube shows **Label pending** and cannot be used as available material until its label is verified. Enter the reason, open the system print dialog, and inspect the physical label. Scan that label into **Scan printed tube barcode**; POMS records **Label printed** and makes the tube available only when the scan matches this container. If the label did not print correctly, enter the failure details and choose **Record failed attempt** before opening the print dialog again or closing the label window. The failure remains in history without falsely counting a usable label or making the tube available.

Every POMS-generated label reprint requires a reason and renders the same current container identity. A reprint or failed attempt adds a separate history event; it does not replace the barcode or erase an earlier print. Registered supplier tubes have no POMS print action. Verify readable text, barcode, accession, container, and adhesion using the approved bench procedure before processing continues.

## Scan and locate a container

Use **Library prep** > **Scan a container** to find the exact work order, accession, container status, location, parent barcode, and associated library. Place the cursor in the container-barcode field and scan either an adopted registered supplier barcode or a POMS-generated barcode. Code 39 start and stop characters and letter case are normalized; an altered checksum or incomplete barcode is rejected without opening another record. An unknown arriving tube must be identified through **Receipt and accession** with its shipment insert; a barcode shared by more than one manufacturer must be scanned within its shipment or job context. The field returns focus after the result so repeated scans remain efficient.

To move a stored tube, open its detail page and choose **Actions → Move to scanned location**. Scan the physical tube, scan the destination freezer box or location barcode, confirm both, and save. POMS records the previous and new location, actor, and time; the tube detail page shows its location history. A scan alone does not move the tube.

## Preserve physical lineage

Create child containers for aliquots, libraries, or other sample-derived materials. Select the immediate parent container and record the quantity and unit required by the approved procedure. POMS assigns each child container its own barcode and opens its label window after creation. The lineage must allow an operator to scan a derived container and trace it through its immediate parent to the submitted specimen. Phaeno reagent manufacturing has its own sample-independent run and lot records; it does not create a child specimen tube.

Do not reuse a barcode, move material by editing its parent, or delete a container to repair history. Record the supported correction or new derived container.

## Apply the intake decision

Acceptance belongs to the physical tube. A specimen is **Accepted** if one or more of its tubes is Accepted, even if another tube is held or rejected. With no accepted tubes, the specimen remains Received (awaiting an acceptable tube), or On hold if a tube is held. Do not use specimen intake to represent a processing hold; job holds and workflow exceptions remain separate.

Routine intake is performed during accessioning. On hold and Rejected require a predefined reason: identity/labeling mismatch, damaged container, leaking container, insufficient material, unacceptable receipt condition, missing information, or Other. Other requires an explanation. POMS retains each decision, stable reason code, notes, reviewer and time. These codes describe intake, not later processing failures.

An authorized Supervisor can open **Tubes → tube barcode → Actions → Correct intake decision** before the tube is used, including while its POMS label is pending. Every correction requires an explanation; held or rejected decisions still require their reason code. A non-stored record can be restored to Accepted or On hold only when material actually exists and a real storage location is provided. A newly allocated POMS tube still needs its first verified label print after a rejected intake decision is corrected. This action does not move stored material. A used source's original intake is locked: handle later problems through the specimen attempt hold or failure actions. Historical processing without a source link retains the earlier specimen-wide lock.

Customer-requested hold management is not available. Internal intake questions and attempt holds remain separate from customer requests. Customer communication uses the existing customer-safe exception workflow.


Shipping inserts, printable kit labels and retained packet copies show QR codes with readable identifiers. POMS-generated laboratory tube labels use DataMatrix and readable identifiers. Saved scan results show the identifier as text. Use a 2D scanner that supports both QR and DataMatrix where both workflows are performed. Existing manufacturer labels and older printed barcodes remain valid; do not relabel a tube or change its saved identity merely to match a display. A scan still requires the correct workflow and does not by itself confirm receipt or accession.


## Choose which tube to process

Open the sample's identifier in **Specimens** to see its tubes together. For a single purchased run, use one source tube and a reserve only after failure. When the sample has multiple purchased runs, one prepared library can supply several runs. Prepare another library only when that is the recorded choice; confirm remaining material before reusing a successful source tube. See **Libraries, batches, and sequencing** for the repeated-run workflow. Acceptance and physical availability determine which sources can be selected; receipt itself never starts work. **Tubes** precedes **Execution** in the job navigation.

Once a source attempt starts, that source's intake history is locked. Record an attempt hold or failure when processing cannot continue; unused reserve intake can be completed during accessioning or corrected by a supervisor. A specimen's intake can remain Accepted even when processing ultimately fails. See the protocol execution guide for source selection, explicit failure, reserve restart and confirmed material exhaustion.
