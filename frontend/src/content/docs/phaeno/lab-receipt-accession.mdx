# Receipt and accession

Receipt and accession connect the Customer’s submitted specimen identity to the physical material handled in the laboratory. Record these steps in sequence and never create an unlinked laboratory specimen.

The first recorded shipment arrival moves an awaiting Job into **Lab operations → Lab work** with the milestone **Received**. Its commercial order moves to **In progress**, while the Customer/Partner Job list displays **Received**. Customer progress uses Received, Library Prep, Sequencing, Data Assembly, Quality Review and Results Available, with current counts and individual sample stages in After you send. Only recorded provider sequencing establishes Sequencing; sendout alone does not. Scientific approval remains distinct from released results. It remains in Lab work while you accession tubes, including when other containers are outstanding. A completed container leaves the accession queue; its tubes and freezer-box values remain in the Job's **Tubes** tab.

Verified tube receipt updates the sample's receipt progress. The commercial sample becomes **Accessioned** after all its expected tubes across active shipments are accessioned. Order updates can take a few seconds. Container receipt alone does not accept tubes. During tube accession, confirm the receipt checks: Accepted is the normal outcome, with no reason code required. A specimen becomes Accepted when at least one of its tubes is Accepted; its acceptance-based turnaround target starts then. This intake decision is separate from laboratory QC and scientific review.


Use **Lab operations → Transportation kits** for **Inventory**, **Kit requests**, and **Kits sent**. Inventory holds individual physical kits and their preparation history. Kit requests lists Customer orders; Kits sent covers shipment-specific kits and fulfillment dates. These queues require configuration-administrator permission. The selected tab and filters stay in the page address, and older saved kit links open the corresponding new workspace.

Use **Lab operations → Receipt & accession** for **Receive shipments** and **Accession samples**. Receive shipments lists expected containers and tracking numbers and accepts the PH-P- insert scan. Accession samples opens a received container for individual tube scanning. Switching between these tabs retains the accession lookup draft and results.

## Fulfill Customer transportation-kit orders

Open **Lab operations → Transportation kits → Kit requests** to review Customer transportation-kit orders. New orders notify Phaeno administrators for fulfillment. Open a request to review its Customer, Department, Job, requested kit configurations and quantities, and the delivery address saved when the Customer confirmed. Transportation kits and outbound delivery are included with the accepted laboratory order; this action creates no additional quote, invoice or payment step.

Use **Record kit shipment** to choose matching, fully registered physical kits and enter the carrier, tracking number and dispatch time, then select **Record dispatch**. If there is insufficient prepared stock, prepare and register more standard kits before returning to the request. Registration alone does not fulfill the request: it can still show **Pending** and zero dispatched kits until dispatch is recorded here. A partial dispatch leaves the remaining requested quantities outstanding. The kit order and saved delivery address remain linked to each dispatched kit.

Dispatch adds those kits to the Customer delivery location's inventory as **On the way**. The Customer acknowledges the containers that arrive, moving them to **Available**. Unused available stock may supply any eligible Job at that Customer/Department/location; the originating Job is delivery history, not a restriction on use. Cancelling the originating Job does not consume delivered unused stock. Customer acknowledgment is separate from the laboratory's later receipt of returned samples. Kits not yet acknowledged remain unavailable, and a partial receipt permits only the received portion.

Maintain delivery locations from the Customer's **Departments** workspace or the Department's delivery-location screen. Open a location and use **Actions → Edit location** in the page header. These locations belong to the Customer and Department, and are separate from global lab receiving destinations. Changing a saved location does not rewrite a confirmed kit order's delivery address. The Customer location workspace provides inventory and receipt access even if the originating Job has been cancelled.


The request shows what to do next. If no matching kits are ready, choose
**Prepare kits** to open kit inventory for the missing configurations. Prepare each physical
kit, register and verify its tube barcodes, complete its assembly run when applicable, then use **Back to kit request**. Once matching
kits are ready, **Record kit shipment** selects the kits and records the carrier,
tracking number and shipment time after handoff. A partially ready request can
ship its available kits while the remaining sizes are prepared. Preparation does
not reserve stock or record a shipment.

## Suppliers and products

Phaeno platform administrators manage **Suppliers & products** in the Lab operations sidebar. Choose **New supplier**, enter its name, and save. Open the supplier name to see its products. Choose **New product** and enter the **Product type**, **Product name**, a required **Product description**, and **Inventory unit** (such as each or mL). Choose a common inventory unit from **Units**, or type another unit when needed. This unit belongs to the product: new purchased material lots use it automatically, so staff enter only the amount received. Existing catalog products with no verified unit must be configured before another lot can be received. Enable **Can expire** when inventory for that product must carry an expiration date. This setting requires dates on new purchased material lots and prepared stock kits. It applies to every configured kit product, including additional supplies. Vendors can supply reagents, tubes, shipping containers, and other products from the same supplier record. Product numbers must be unique within each supplier, including inactive products. Changing Can expire leaves existing inventory dates and saved kit requirements unchanged; older records are not given inferred dates.

Each supplier and product row has an **Actions** menu. Choose **Edit** to correct details, or **Deactivate** and review the application confirmation to make the record inactive. **Show inactive** reveals those records; choose **Actions → Activate** to restore them. The supplier detail header offers the same actions. Inactivating an external supplier prevents selection of all its products for new kits; it does not alter prepared kits. Inactive product types also prevent their products from being selected for new kits. The tube **Lot** remains specific to each kit preparation and is not stored in the product catalog. **Phaeno** is the seeded internal producer and cannot be renamed or deactivated. Open it to define each Phaeno-made reagent or transportation kit as a separate product. For a transportation kit, enter its **SKU** and **Kit name**; the unit is **each**. The SKU is fixed after creation, while the Kit name can be corrected until a shipping specification is linked. Reagents retain a product name and the inventory unit appropriate to their yield. Deactivation blocks new manufacturing without deleting past runs or kits. Configure assembly procedures in **Lab settings → Workflows**, then start work in **Reagent manufacturing** or **Transportation kits**.

## Manage product types

Open **Lab operations → Suppliers & products** and select the **Product types** tab to manage the categories offered by your vendors. **Tube**, **Shipping Container**, **Reagent**, and **Transportation kit** are provided initially. Transportation kit identifies the finished Phaeno product; Shipping Container identifies a purchased outer shipper component. Choose **New product type** and enter a unique name, description, and **Use in transportation kits**. For reagents and other supplies, choose **Other product**. A vendor can offer products of several types.

Open a type's name to review its description, status, kit use, and product count. Each type has an **Actions** menu in its list row and detail header. Choose **Edit** to change its details, or **Deactivate** to make it inactive after reviewing the confirmation. Use **Show inactive**, then **Actions → Activate**, to restore a type. Once products reference a type, its kit use cannot change; create a different type when that classification needs to differ. Existing products retain inactive type references for review and correction, but new products must choose an active type. Inactivating a type preserves kit history.

External reagent vendors use the same supplier directory as **Purchased Materials**. Catalog entries identify what was purchased but do not create material lots, receive stock, or record QC. Receive purchased lots in **Purchased Materials**; make Phaeno reagents in **Reagent manufacturing**. Both lot types need a separate QC decision before use.

## Configure and assemble transportation kits

Create a named **Transportation kit** product with a stable SKU under the internal **Phaeno** supplier. In **Lab settings → Workflows**, add its kit assembly workflow: choose approved instruction-only Lab steps and list every purchased component product and the whole-number quantity needed for one kit. Include one tube product, its exact tube count, and an outer shipper. Approve the workflow revision. In **Samples & shipping settings**, create the matching shipping specification, select the same finished product and approved workflow revision, and choose its one Sample type, then set compatible destinations, capacity, and packing instructions. A Sample type can have several dedicated kit products. A mismatched component list or tube count cannot be approved. A changed procedure or bill of materials requires a new approved revision; existing physical kits keep their pinned revisions. The product SKU is fixed from creation; once a shipping specification is linked, the Kit name is fixed as well. Define a new kit product for a different SKU or issued name.

Open **Lab operations → Transportation kits → Inventory**. The list starts with kits **At Phaeno** that are preparing or ready for dispatch. Choose **Shipped kits** in the Status filter to see dispatched kits, including those later received, assigned, in use, or needing review. **All statuses** and individual statuses are available when you need a broader or narrower view. Search by kit number, configuration, SKU, location or Job. Open the kit number to review its products, registered tubes, assembly, location, receipt, outbound tracking and any current assignment. Distinguish the originating request's Job from the Job currently using the container.

Choose **Prepare standard kit** and select an active approved kit configuration. The matched tube and outer shipper catalog products come from its approved bill of materials; additional required products appear in **Contents per kit**. Confirm the actual products and any requested lot and expiration information. The configuration supplies the usable tube capacity. Product and revision details are saved with each kit, so later catalog edits do not rewrite its history. Historical configurations without a linked finished product remain readable. A new kit does not yet belong to a Customer Job.

**Product expiration dates** lists each product marked Can expire in the complete kit contents and the actual tube and shipping-container selections. Enter each product's labeled date before preparing the kit. You may record an already-expired date for stock that has physically arrived; the saved kit details show **Expired** beside that date. Each physical kit retains the product requirement and date recorded at preparation. Historical kits without this evidence show that the requirements and dates were not recorded.

For a new linked kit, open its **Assembly run** and record each step and actual use of every required component. Source-lot quantities decrease when use is recorded, including when a run stops. Complete the run only when the step evidence and bill of materials are complete. A stopped run does not create finished stock.

Choose **Register tubes** and scan each permanent identifier from the actual tubes being packed. The tube product establishes the manufacturer's barcode namespace. A kit needs the full distinct roster; duplicate and excess scans are rejected. Before sealing, use **Verify physical tube roster** to scan the entire physical roster again. A corrected tube requires a reason and another full verification. The kit's own **KIT-** number is the container barcode; print and retain its readable label. Only a completed linked assembly with a verified physical tube roster is ready for dispatch. Return to **Kit requests**, open the request, and use **Record kit shipment** to select the kit and record dispatch to its confirmed address. The kit's **Record dispatch** action reaches the same request and stock. The Customer later assigns the received container to a Job.

**On the way** means dispatched but not yet acknowledged. **Available** means received and unassigned at the Customer location. **Assigned** means the Customer confirmed this container for a Job/shipment; **In use** means tube scanning has begun. Reset before the first tube scan releases an assignment to available inventory. After scanning, ordinary reset/reassignment remains locked. **Needs review** identifies incomplete location or receipt history that must be resolved before use. The original request, tracking and receipt remain distinct from the consuming Job.

If an older kit is already sent but its request still shows an outstanding quantity, the kit page offers **Update kit request** when the saved dispatch has one eligible matching request. Review the existing carrier, tracking number and dispatch time, then confirm **Update kit request**. This links the existing dispatch; it does not send another kit or confirm Customer receipt. A failed update keeps the confirmation available for review and retry. Existing Trial and Partner supply workflows remain unchanged. Revise the shipping specification and assembly workflow for future kits rather than changing an issued kit's capacity or contents.

## Delivery deadline before acceptance

Every job must have a delivery due date when its first sample is accepted. The saved standard turnaround calculates the date automatically. When no turnaround is recorded, an operator or supervisor opens the job and chooses **Actions → Set due date**, enters the delivery date/time and a customer-safe reason, then returns to acceptance. Acceptance cannot be saved until the date is set. Receipt and holding or rejecting a tube do not start the turnaround clock.

## Receive a shipment

Open **Lab operations → Receipt & accession → Receive shipments**. **Expected shipments** lists each expected container separately, including the Customer, Job, carrier, tracking number and expected tube count. Containers still being prepared remain visible with their current status; missing tracking is shown as not recorded.

When a container physically arrives, scan the **PH-P-** barcode under **Scan to receive this shipment** in the body of its shipping insert in **Shipping insert barcode**, then submit with Enter or **Receive shipment**. This action records the container's arrival time and staff audit event. The confirmation identifies the shipment and offers **Accession samples** at the right of its first row. Repeating the same scan shows the original receipt without creating another event. Invalid, replaced or cancelled inserts cannot receive a container. A failed response leaves receipt unconfirmed in the screen; scanning again safely checks or completes that receipt.

This receiving field accepts only the shipping insert's PH-P- code. The SHP… shipment reference, PH-S- shipment barcode, PH-O- Order barcode, PH-M- sample barcode, physical KIT- container barcode and individual tube barcodes do not acknowledge container arrival here. Receipt of a container does not accession its tubes or accept its samples for processing.

## Open a received container

Choose **Accession samples**. **Received containers awaiting accession** shows containers that have arrived and still have tubes to accession. Open the shipment reference or scan its PH-P- insert. This lookup is read-only. Within this tab, a **PH-S-** barcode opens the shipment's current confirmed insert, and **PH-O-** or **PH-M-** lookups let you choose a related manifest. The readable **SHP…** reference and physical **KIT-** barcode are not accepted by the lookup field.

The shipping insert opens the accession dialog with every expected tube and its saved intake decision. Once the shipment details have loaded, the **Supplier tube barcode** field receives keyboard focus unless you have already moved to another control. Inspect tubes before storage. Scan each available physical tube in **Supplier tube barcode** and press Enter or **Identify tube**. Identification selects the tube for this session; it does not save acceptance or a storage location.

Record the exceptions first. Choose **Record exception** on the expected tube's row. Select **Rejected** or **On hold** and a predefined reason. Confirm that the received tube or damaged remains match that shipment row. A broken tube whose barcode cannot be scanned can still be rejected against its expected identity. Missing tubes remain outstanding; do not mark them received merely because they appear on the insert.

A broken or destroyed rejected tube requires no freezer location and is unavailable for processing. POMS retains its expected-tube link, rejection reason, notes, actor and time. No remaining quantity is asserted for a non-stored rejection. If rejected material is actually retained, its real storage location may be recorded. An internally held tube must have a real retained-material location. Rejection itself does not record disposal.

The modal footer shows **X with exceptions | Y to be accepted**. Exceptions count tubes on hold or rejected in this shipment; the acceptance count includes only identified tubes with no saved decision. After exceptions are saved, choose **Accept (Y)**. Previously accepted, held, rejected and unidentified tubes are excluded. Review the listed identities, record each actual freezer-box barcode, and confirm inspection. **Accept N tubes** saves the entire batch together. Routine acceptance needs no reason code. Storage is recorded as entered; POMS does not validate a freezer registry or assign a position within the box.

If any selected tube changed, the whole batch is refused. Your entries stay visible; close the acceptance dialog and review the refreshed selection before trying again. For an uncertain connection result, retry the same request. A completed batch is returned without duplicate intake records. Bulk acceptance cannot relocate a previously stored tube.

POMS assigns or reuses the specimen's accession number and preserves each supplier barcode. A rejected received tube completes that tube's receipt handling without becoming acceptable material. The specimen becomes Accepted when at least one tube is Accepted. Complete receipt handling and scientific acceptance are separate facts.

**Clear selection** removes the unsaved identified selection without changing any saved intake decision. Saved exceptions survive closing. Unsaved identified selections and storage entries prompt before being discarded. When all expected tubes have decisions, choose **Done**. Historical tubes with no intake decision are not automatically accepted: identify and inspect them explicitly in this accession dialog, or ask a supervisor to document a correction from the tube details.

## Other specimen and label actions

The Lab work record retains individual receipt and accession actions for supported legacy intake. Its **Tubes** tab shows **Received tubes**, with clear intake and storage information. Open a tube barcode to see its receipt evidence, storage and lineage. POMS-generated container labels are available from the detail page’s **Actions** menu.

The label window renders the current container identity as a DataMatrix with readable text through the browser and installed printer driver. A newly allocated POMS tube shows **Label pending** and cannot be used as available material until its label is verified. Enter the reason, open the system print dialog, and inspect the physical label. Scan that label into **Scan printed tube barcode**; POMS records **Label printed** and makes the tube available only when the scan matches this container. If the label did not print correctly, enter the failure details and choose **Record failed attempt** before opening the print dialog again or closing the label window. The failure remains in history without falsely counting a usable label or making the tube available.

Every POMS-generated label reprint requires a reason and renders the same current container identity. A reprint or failed attempt adds a separate history event; it does not replace the barcode or erase an earlier print. Registered supplier tubes have no POMS print action. Verify readable text, barcode, accession, container, and adhesion using the approved bench procedure before processing continues.

## Scan and locate a container

Use **Library prep** > **Scan a container** to find the exact work order, accession, container status, location, parent barcode, and associated library. Place the cursor in the container-barcode field and scan either an adopted registered supplier barcode or a POMS-generated barcode. Code 39 start and stop characters and letter case are normalized; an altered checksum or incomplete barcode is rejected without opening another record. An unknown arriving tube must be identified through **Receipt and accession** with its shipment insert; a barcode shared by more than one manufacturer must be scanned within its shipment or job context. The field returns focus after the result so repeated scans remain efficient.

To move a stored tube, open its detail page and choose **Actions → Move to scanned location**. Scan the physical tube, scan the destination freezer box or location barcode, confirm both, and save. POMS records the previous and new location, actor, and time; the tube detail page shows its location history. A scan alone does not move the tube.

## Preserve physical lineage

Create child containers for aliquots, libraries, or other sample-derived materials. Select the immediate parent container and record the quantity and unit required by the approved procedure. POMS assigns each child container its own barcode and opens its label window after creation. The lineage must allow an operator to scan a derived container and trace it through its immediate parent to the submitted specimen. Phaeno reagent manufacturing has its own sample-independent run and lot records; it does not create a child specimen tube.

Do not reuse a barcode, move material by editing its parent, or delete a container to repair history. Record the supported correction or new derived container.

## Apply the intake decision

Acceptance belongs to the physical tube. A specimen is **Accepted** if one or more of its tubes is Accepted, even if another tube is held or rejected. With no accepted tubes, the specimen remains Received (awaiting an acceptable tube), or On hold if a tube is held. Do not use specimen intake to represent a processing hold; job holds and workflow exceptions remain separate.

Routine intake is performed during accessioning. On hold and Rejected require a predefined reason: identity/labeling mismatch, damaged container, leaking container, insufficient material, unacceptable receipt condition, missing information, or Other. Other requires an explanation. POMS retains each decision, stable reason code, notes, reviewer and time. These codes describe intake, not later processing failures.

An authorized Supervisor can open **Tubes → tube barcode → Actions → Correct intake decision** before the tube is used, including while its POMS label is pending. Every correction requires an explanation; held or rejected decisions still require their reason code. A non-stored record can be restored to Accepted or On hold only when material actually exists and a real storage location is provided. A newly allocated POMS tube still needs its first verified label print after a rejected intake decision is corrected. This action does not move stored material. A used source's original intake is locked: handle later problems through the specimen attempt hold or failure actions. Historical processing without a source link retains the earlier specimen-wide lock.

Customer-requested hold management is not available. Internal intake questions and attempt holds remain separate from customer requests. Customer communication uses the existing customer-safe exception workflow.


Shipping inserts, printable kit labels and retained packet copies show QR codes with readable identifiers. POMS-generated laboratory tube labels use DataMatrix and readable identifiers. Saved scan results show the identifier as text. Use a 2D scanner that supports both QR and DataMatrix where both workflows are performed. Existing manufacturer labels and older printed barcodes remain valid; do not relabel a tube or change its saved identity merely to match a display. A scan still requires the correct workflow and does not by itself confirm receipt or accession.


## Choose which tube to process

Open the sample's identifier in **Specimens** to see its tubes together. For a single purchased run, use one source tube and a reserve only after failure. When the sample has multiple purchased runs, one prepared library can supply several runs. Prepare another library only when that is the recorded choice; confirm remaining material before reusing a successful source tube. See **Libraries, batches, and sequencing** for the repeated-run workflow. Acceptance and physical availability determine which sources can be selected; receipt itself never starts work. **Tubes** precedes **Execution** in the job navigation.

Once a source attempt starts, that source's intake history is locked. Record an attempt hold or failure when processing cannot continue; unused reserve intake can be completed during accessioning or corrected by a supervisor. A specimen's intake can remain Accepted even when processing ultimately fails. See the protocol execution guide for source selection, explicit failure, reserve restart and confirmed material exhaustion.
