# Getting started

Portal gives a Prospect organization access to explicitly granted Phaeno-owned curated data and approved Trial Projects. Organization and assigned-Department administrators can accept frozen Trial scope and submit coded research RNA under the approved allowance and dates. Prospect access does not include paid laboratory requests, reagent orders, data-assembly requests, or other commercial ordering.

## Find current work

The dashboard and header bell show current work in your selected organization
and Department. The bell counts unfinished actions; opening an item does not
complete it. Available results and work waiting on an administrator or Phaeno
are separate from your own actions. Open the linked record to perform the
supported next step, or use **View all work** for the complete filtered list.
Unavailable counts remain unknown until they can be refreshed.

## Navigate the help center

Page-return links use a left arrow and name the destination. Use them to return
from a record to its list or originating workflow. If more than one return
destination is offered, choose the one that matches your task.

Open the user menu in the toolbar and choose **Documentation**. This entry is available on both wide and narrow screens. On smaller screens, the Documentation tab sits below the toolbar and organization details.

On narrow screens, the user menu opens a full-width tray from the right. Choose **Close** or press Escape to dismiss it and return to its toolbar button. Your workspace links appear directly in the tray. When configuration pages are available to your role, choose **Settings** to expand them. The section starts collapsed each time you open the menu; use Enter or Space to toggle it, Up and Down to move among visible links, and Escape to close the user menu.

Guide navigation opens beneath the toolbar from the far-left edge of the screen. Its header identifies Documentation and, on wider screens, provides the pin or unpin icon. Pointer users can preview an unpinned sidebar by approaching the left edge; keyboard and touch users open it from the tab. Choosing a guide closes an unpinned sidebar and displays the article. On narrow screens, click or tap outside the sidebar to close it; its tab and Escape also close it.

## Confirm the current organization

The header shows the organization active in your current session. That organization controls every record and action you can see, and information never carries between organizations.

If your account has more than one active organization membership, open the user menu and select one of your own organizations from the list beside the organization icon. The same select list is available in the narrow-screen menu tray. Switching returns to Dashboard and selects an available Department in that organization; its permissions, grants, Trials and guides apply. Save your work first, or cancel an unsaved-page warning to remain in the current organization. If an expected organization is missing, ask its administrator to review your access.

## Know your access level

Active Prospect members can review and download the exact curated package versions granted organization-wide or to their selected Department. Organization administrators can also review organization download history and may be asked to respond to a package-governance notice.

Prospect organization administrators use **User management** for durable invitations, Member or Organization administrator roles, and membership deactivation. Other members do not receive those controls.

## Find your work

- **Dashboard** separates **Needs attention**, **Waiting on others**, and **Available results and updates** for your selected Department. Trial acceptance and authorized sample submission appear when their prerequisites are met. Follow an item's link to act, or use **View all work** for the complete paged list. Use **First page** if a saved page is beyond the last page. **Retry work** recovers unavailable counts and list reads.
- **Trial Projects** opens approved scope, sample submission, laboratory progress, released results, and retention receipts. Read [Trial Projects](./trial-projects) before accepting a scope.
- **Data Library** contains curated package versions explicitly granted to the Prospect.
- **Samples & shipping** contains authorized Trial Project return kits, tube matching, and shipping packets when Phaeno has prepared a shipment.
- For organization administrators, **User management** manages organization invitations, roles, and memberships.
- **Documentation** in the user menu opens these Prospect guides.

Prospect organizations do not receive order or service workspaces. Phaeno alone can change the organization relationship after business approval.

## Get help safely

When contacting Phaeno, provide the organization name, package name and version, and a description of the step you were performing. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, invitation links, authentication tokens, or downloaded data in a support message.

## Search and browse guides

Use **Search documentation** at the top of Documentation to find a guide or a matching section. Results update after a short pause; use at least two characters. Try “Trial acceptance” or “download grace”.

Choose **Filters** to narrow results by Topic, Workflow, or Guide type. **Clear all** removes those selections. **Browse by topic** opens guides for a topic without requiring search words. Workflow links and **Related guides** at the end of an article provide other ways to find relevant instructions.

Select a result title to open its matching section. Your browser’s Back action restores the search and filters. Search uses the guides for your current organization; it does not include the public Website or operational records. A guide’s instructions do not change your account permissions.

If no guides match, try fewer words or clear the filters. If search is temporarily unavailable, use **Try again** or **Browse all guides**. A documentation update may require **Refresh page**; guide browsing remains available while the update finishes.
