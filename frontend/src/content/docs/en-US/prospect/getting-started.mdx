# Getting started

Portal gives a Prospect organization access to explicitly granted Phaeno-owned curated data and approved Trial Projects. Trial organization administrators can accept frozen scope and submit coded research RNA under the approved allowance and dates. Prospect access does not include laboratory requests, reagent orders, data-assembly requests, or other commercial ordering.

## Navigate the help center

Guide navigation opens beneath the toolbar from the far-left edge of the screen. Its header identifies Documentation and, on wider screens, provides the pin or unpin icon. Pointer users can preview an unpinned sidebar by approaching the left edge; keyboard and touch users open it from the tab. Choosing a guide closes an unpinned sidebar and displays the article.

## Confirm the current organization

The user menu shows the organization active in your current session. That organization controls every record and action you can see, and information never carries between organizations.

Self-service switching between multiple external organization memberships is not currently available. If the wrong organization is active, or your account belongs to more than one external organization, contact Phaeno before continuing.

## Know your access level

Active Prospect members can review and download the exact curated package versions granted to their organization. Organization administrators can also review organization download history and may be asked to respond to a package-governance notice.

Prospect organization administrators use **User management** for durable invitations, Member or Organization administrator roles, and membership deactivation. Other members do not receive those controls.

## Find your work

- **Dashboard** shows live organization-scoped counts and starting points for assigned data, authorized Trial Project shipments, and administrator user access.
- **Trial Projects** opens approved scope, sample submission, laboratory progress, released results, and retention receipts. Read [Trial Projects](./trial-projects) before accepting a scope.
- **Data Library** contains curated package versions explicitly granted to the Prospect.
- **Samples & shipping** contains authorized Trial Project return kits, tube matching, and shipping packets when Phaeno has prepared a shipment.
- For organization administrators, **User management** manages organization invitations, roles, and memberships.
- **Docs** opens these Prospect guides.

Prospect organizations do not receive order or service workspaces. Phaeno alone can change the organization relationship after business approval.

## Get help safely

When contacting Phaeno, provide the organization name, package name and version, and a description of the step you were performing. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, invitation links, authentication tokens, or downloaded data in a support message.
