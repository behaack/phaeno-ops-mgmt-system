# Prepare and ship Trial Project samples

Open the approved Trial and its **Shipping containers** section. Phaeno creates and approves the Trial scope. A Prospect organization administrator accepts it and submits coded samples before shipping is authorized. Organization and Department administrators may prepare the resulting shipments; these shipping permissions do not grant permission to accept Trial scope or submit its original samples. See [Trial Projects](./trial-projects).

## Choose compatible containers

Open **Choose containers** for tubes awaiting allocation. Review the recommended sizes by common name, SKU and usable tube capacity. The recommendation prefers the fewest containers and then the least spare capacity; it does not verify or reserve stock.

Open **Adjust containers** to review **Containers to use**. The recommendation starts as one row per container, with a compatible size selector, SKU/capacity and tube count. **Add container** chooses a size for the capacity still needed and assigns the remaining tubes up to its capacity. A row's **Remove** action removes only that container; changing or removing it preserves the other rows' tube counts.

Size choices include smaller containers and the smallest size that covers the remaining capacity needed, excluding choices that make another selected container unnecessary. With sizes 5/10/20, three remaining tubes offer only a 5. For 30 tubes, 20+10 or six 5s are valid; two 20s are not offered. With 10+5 already selected, Add chooses another 10 and then a 5. **Add container** is disabled once selected capacity covers all tubes. **Use recommendation** replaces the current rows and tube counts. Stock requirements apply automatically; no manual stock quantities are entered here. The preview shows tubes, containers, capacity, spare slots and unallocated tubes.

**Confirm containers** creates one shipment for each container with allocated tubes. A partly filled container is valid and empty containers do not become shipments. If capacity is insufficient, **Prepare available containers** prepares the allocated tubes while the remaining tubes stay visible. The accepted Trial sample count and no-charge scope do not change. Tubes from one sample may occupy different containers.

Adjust each row's tube count as needed. Counts must be whole numbers, fit each container and together equal the tubes allocated in the preview. The **Summary** stays visible while updating; confirmation becomes available when the current allocation has been checked.

## Scan permanent tube barcodes

Use **Shipping container** at the top of the shipment page to switch between this Trial's containers or return to remaining tubes awaiting containers. Each option shows the container identity, tube count and status. Switching with an unsaved scan asks whether to discard it; switching is blocked while saving.

Before scanning starts, an organization or Department administrator can use **Reset container configuration** beside the **Shipping container** selector. The confirmation covers **all containers and tubes for the Trial's shipment plan**, not just the shipment you opened. The submitted sample list stays intact, and the previous configurations remain in the records and audit history for support. Retired configurations are not shown in your **Shipping containers** panel. Cancelling the confirmation changes nothing.

**Reset container configuration** is also unavailable while a barcode entry is unsaved or saving. Discard an unsaved entry before revising the plan; saving the first scan locks the plan as described below.

The action is locked once any container in that plan has a scan, linked physical kit, shipping insert, dispatch or receipt. Clearing a scan does not restore it. If work starts while your confirmation is open, the change is rejected; refresh and review the current state. A successful change returns you to container selection while preserving approved Trial scope, tube identities and physical kit inventory.

Your organization must retain the connection between each coded sample ID and its own sample record. Do not enter patient names, medical-record numbers, dates of birth or other direct identifiers. Phaeno retains the permanent-tube-to-sample crosswalk used for shipping and intake.

Select **Open shipment** for a prepared container and use **Scan tubes into this shipment**. Scan the physical tube for the displayed sample and tube number, then press Enter or **Save scan**. A successful match shows the saved number and barcode graphic, updates progress and advances to the next tube. Saved matches survive leaving the page. **Previous tubes** and **Next tubes** review long lists without expanding the whole page.

Use tubes registered in a fulfilled kit for this Trial. The first successful scan associates the physical kit with this container; subsequent tubes must come from that kit. Unknown, ineligible, duplicate and already-used barcodes cannot advance scanning. A failed scan retains the barcode and current sample for correction and retry. Do not cover, alter or remove a permanent manufacturer barcode.

Use **Change tube** or **Correct tube** to fix an assignment before dispatch and explain the correction. History is retained. When a shipping insert has already been confirmed, the correction voids it and issues a corrected revision. Destroy unused copies of the old shipping insert.

## Confirm, print and dispatch each shipment

When the shipment header has more than one action, open **Actions** to find them, including **Print shipping insert**, **Download tube list (CSV)** and **Record shipment** when permitted. If only one header action is available, it appears directly. Your permissions and the shipment's stage determine which actions appear. Use **Print shipping insert** again if you need another copy of the current revision.

Review every declared tube, then select **Review and confirm shipping insert**. Confirmation distinguishes samples from tubes and freezes that shipment's destination, packing instructions and crosswalk. Spare slots do not create expected returns.

Print one receiving sheet for each physical container and place it inside that container. It shows the Customer, Job/Trial and shipment references, sample and tube totals, essential handling notes and two widely separated scan targets: the large **Scan to receive this shipment** PH-P- insert barcode and, when assigned, the physical container barcode. Follow the full preparation and packing instructions in the Portal before shipping. **Full packing instructions and sample / tube list** on the insert page retains the confirmed manifest, every sample and tube barcode, and split-sample references; these details do not print with the receiving sheet. Use **Download tube list (CSV)** to retain the complete crosswalk.

**Print shipping insert** checks the current revision, then opens the browser print dialog while keeping your shipment page open. Cancelling printing leaves you on the same shipment with your saved tube matches intact. If the insert cannot be checked, review the message on the shipment page, reconnect if needed and select **Try again**. Failed confirmation keeps the dialog entries for retry. Retain the shipping insert or use **Download tube list (CSV)** for your records. The shipping insert does not purchase postage or replace the carrier label.

After handing each package to the carrier, record its carrier, tracking number and shipment time. Receipt progress aggregates tubes across shipments. In the Trial's **Shipping containers** panel, expand **Sample receipt progress** to review each sample. The first received package does not mark all tubes of a split sample received, and a barcode lookup is not proof that physical material arrived.

Use **Back to Trial** for approved scope and current progress or **Shipping container** to move between its containers. Contact Phaeno with the Trial and shipment references if no compatible container exists, a tube cannot be matched confidently, or existing assignments need repacking. Do not guess a tube identity or change approved scope through shipping.


Portal-generated identifiers now render as QR codes, with the exact identifier
printed underneath. Shipping inserts, container labels, kit records and displayed
sample/tube identifiers use the same format. Use a QR-capable 2D scanner. Existing
manufacturer labels and older printed barcodes remain valid; do not relabel a
tube or change its saved identity merely to match the new display. A scan still
requires the correct workflow and does not by itself confirm receipt or accession.
