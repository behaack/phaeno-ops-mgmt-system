# Prepare and ship Trial Project samples

Open the Trial and its **Return kit and shipment** section to find related shipments. The shared **Samples & shipping** list also shows authorized shipments across your Department. The shipment shows the Phaeno return kit, expected samples, destination, and current packet status. Phaeno creates and approves scope in **Trial Projects**. A Prospect organization administrator accepts it and submits coded samples there before this shared shipment is authorized. See [Trial Projects](./trial-projects).

## Keep your records connected to the tubes

Each Phaeno-supplied tube has a permanent manufacturer barcode. Your organization or department administrator scans or enters that barcode beside the correct Customer sample identifier before anything is shipped. Use a stable, non-PHI identifier that your organization can connect to its own sample record. Do not enter a patient name, medical-record number, date of birth, or other direct identifier.

Phaeno preserves the tube-to-sample crosswalk in the Portal and in the shipment packet. Download the CSV or print the packet for your records. Your organization remains responsible for knowing what its Customer sample identifier means; Phaeno is responsible for retaining the identifier-to-tube association used for shipping and intake.

If the wrong tube was assigned, an organization or department administrator can scan the replacement tube and record why the assignment changed. The earlier association remains in history. If a packet was already confirmed, saving the correction before shipment voids that packet and issues a corrected revision; destroy any unused copy of the old packet and print the replacement. Do not cover, alter, or remove the manufacturer barcode.

## Confirm and ship

Assign exactly one registered tube from the displayed return kit to every expected tube slot. A sample with multiple tubes has one slot per tube, shown as **Tube 1 of N**. Confirm the crosswalk only after checking every row. The Portal then freezes the destination, instructions, manifest, packet barcode, and tube-to-sample crosswalk for that packet revision.

Print the full packet. Follow the preparation and packing instructions, place the manifest inside the package, and use the ship-to page with the carrier label. The Phaeno packet barcode does not buy postage and is not a tube barcode.

Confirmation shows both the number of samples and the number of tubes. **View packet** checks the current revision before offering **Print packet**; reconnect or use **Try again** if it cannot be checked. A correction or replacement refreshes the packet. If saving fails, the message appears in the open dialog and your entries stay available for review.

After handing the package to the carrier, record the carrier, tracking number, and shipment time in **Samples & shipping**. If a tube cannot be matched confidently or its barcode will not scan, stop and contact Phaeno instead of guessing.

Use **Back to Trial** from the shipment to return to its approved scope and sample progress, or **All shipments** for your authorized shipment list. Shipping administration may be performed by the Department administrator; accepting Trial scope and submitting original samples remain organization-administrator actions.
