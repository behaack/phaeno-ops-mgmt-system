# Organization access and transition

A Prospect organization may receive explicitly granted curated data and participate in an approved no-charge Trial. It cannot place paid commercial orders. Its users, memberships, grants, downloads, and audit history remain scoped to that organization.

## Manage organization access

Organization administrators manage access from **User management**. Enter the person’s first name, last name, and exact sign-in email, then choose **Member** or **Organization administrator**. Invited people appear in the same list with a **Pending invitation** status. Open a card’s actions menu to resend or revoke an invitation, edit an active member’s organization role, or deactivate another member’s organization membership. An administrator cannot deactivate their own membership from User management.

Never copy or request an invitation token. A deactivated membership is restored only through a fresh invitation and acceptance. Contact Phaeno when the entire user account is disabled or when an invitation or membership action cannot be completed.

## Understand a relationship change

Only Phaeno can convert a Prospect organization to Customer or Partner, and only after the business relationship is approved. Conversion preserves the same organization, users, Department memberships, package grants, download history, approved Trial history, and audit history. Existing Trial results remain in their original Department under their existing retention dates. Conversion does not reopen a Trial or authorize additional Trial samples.

Conversion does not automatically:

- create or authorize an order;
- grant additional package versions;
- configure prices, credit, offerings, or analyses;
- accept commercial terms on the organization’s behalf; or
- change a user’s role.

After conversion, Phaeno completes the applicable service and commercial setup before order actions become available. Until then, continue to use only the capabilities visible in the portal.

## Protect organization boundaries

Do not use another organization’s invitation, package link, downloaded file, or commercial reference. If the current organization name is wrong, stop before opening data and contact Phaeno.
