# Your Trial Project

A Trial Project is a no-charge, closed-ended PSeq evaluation of extracted RNA. Phaeno defines and approves its scope with you. It does not create a laboratory order, quote, invoice, or payment obligation.

**For Research Use Only. Not for use in diagnostic procedures.** Do not enter protected health information (PHI), patient identifiers, medical record numbers, dates of birth, or direct personal identifiers. Use coded research references and keep any identity mapping outside the Portal.

## Review and accept the scope

Open **Trial projects** and select the Trial number. Confirm the selected organization and department. Active members can view approved Trials in their department; only an organization administrator can accept scope or submit samples. Department-administrator rights alone do not permit those actions.

The workspace shows the objective, original sample allowance, submission opening and closing times, approved PSeq analyses, deliverables, acceptance criteria, material handling, and terms. Dates are displayed in your local time zone. Commercial and Scientific Operations approvals must come from two different authorized Phaeno people.

Select **Review and accept scope**, read the complete approved scope in the dialog, and affirm the RUO/no-PHI terms. Acceptance applies to that scope revision. A later amendment requires new Phaeno approvals and your organization's acceptance again. Previous approved revisions remain available in the workspace.

## Submit extracted RNA

An organization administrator selects **Submit sample** while the approved submission window is open. Enter a unique coded sample reference, biological source, number of tubes, quantity and unit, storage requirements, safety declaration, and any additional inputs required by the approved PSeq analyses. Select the available extracted-RNA type and shipping destination. Quantity must meet that sample type's configured limits. Confirm RUO and no PHI for each submission.

The original sample allowance is specific to this Trial. There is no automatic renewal or universal sample cap. If submission is unavailable, the workspace explains whether approval, acceptance, a hold, the time window, closure, or the allowance is responsible.

Submitting creates the authorized laboratory work and a shipment awaiting Phaeno's return-kit preparation. Continue in [Prepare and ship samples](/docs/prospect/sample-shipping). Follow the approved packing and receiving instructions; submitting a record does not mean the material has been received.

If Phaeno authorizes a replacement, choose the failed original sample in **Approved replacement for**. Each authorization allows one replacement and preserves the original history. A failed sample never grants an unapproved extra slot.

## Follow progress and download results

The workspace shows sample progress, customer-safe updates, and any schedule estimate. Trial estimates are not guaranteed turnaround times. A hold stops scientific progress and delivery while Phaeno resolves the issue.

Partial results may become available before the entire Trial finishes. Their release does not start the complete-package retention period. When Phaeno releases all approved deliverables for the submitted samples, the Trial becomes **Completed** and the complete package receives its frozen retention policy. If the approved work cannot be completed, Phaeno records **Closed incomplete** with a reason.

Use **Download package** for the release archive, including its RUO manifest, or download an individual file. After complete release, use the complete package instead of an earlier partial archive. **Retention and receipt** shows the package's manifest, frozen deadlines, download state, and retained lineage.

The initial file policy is 30 exact 24-hour days from complete release, with a warning five days before deletion if files remain undownloaded and a five-day grace period when required. Phaeno may configure organization overrides; the receipt shows your package's actual policy. A successful download by any authorized organization member satisfies that file for the organization. Failed or partial transfers do not count. Access may close earlier if the release is withdrawn or held. Deletion removes bytes; history and receipts remain and do not promise restoration.

## Material, closure, and next steps

Residual RNA normally remains for 30 days after terminal Trial closure, using the exact duration frozen in your scope. Phaeno records actual destruction, return, or exhaustion separately. Return is available only under arrangements approved before submission, including destination, handling, and shipping responsibility. Trial material is not reused for another purpose through this workflow.

Trial completion does not convert your organization automatically. If Phaeno later converts it to Customer or Partner, approved Trial history remains under the same organization and department. Conversion does not extend download deadlines or permit new submissions to the old Trial. Further work requires a separate approved workflow.

If a record changes while you are entering information, select **Reload current Trial; keep my entries**, review the updated state, and retry. For a changed scope, close the acceptance dialog and reopen the current approved scope before accepting. Contact Phaeno with the Trial number and the step that failed; do not send sample identity mappings or confidential scientific files in a support message.
