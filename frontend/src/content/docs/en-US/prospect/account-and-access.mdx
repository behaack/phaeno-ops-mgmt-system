# Account and access

Your Portal access is created through an invitation to a specific Prospect organization. The current organization, your active membership, and your role determine which data and administrative information is available.

## Accept an invitation

1. Open the newest invitation link sent for your Prospect organization.
2. Sign in with the same email address that received the invitation.
3. Review the organization, your name, and the offered role.
4. Accept or decline the invitation.

The invitation page shows the email address currently signed in. If it is not the invited email, choose **Sign out and use invited email**, then sign in with the correct account. The invitation remains available in that browser while you switch accounts, and the identity service returns you to the invitation page after sign-in or account creation.

Invitation links are private. A link can expire, be revoked, or be replaced by a newer invitation. If it is unavailable, ask your organization administrator or Phaeno to send a new invitation.

The portal does not collect or reset your sign-in password. Use the account recovery offered by the identity service on the sign-in page.

## Protect your account

After your first sign-in step, connect an authenticator app and confirm the code it displays. Save the one-time backup codes shown during setup somewhere private and separate from the device running the authenticator. Never send an authenticator or backup code to Phaeno or your organization administrator.

If you lose the authenticator but still have a backup code, use that code to sign in, then contact Phaeno to reset the authenticator enrollment. If you lose both the authenticator and every backup code, contact Phaeno directly. Phaeno will verify your identity and organization before resetting MFA; all active sessions are revoked and you must connect a new authenticator before Portal access resumes. Email can start the support request but does not itself bypass MFA.

## Confirm the current organization

The user menu shows the organization active in your current session. Every package, download, and administrative view is scoped to it.

Self-service switching between multiple external organization memberships is not currently available. If the wrong organization is shown, stop and contact Phaeno before opening or downloading data.

## Work within a department

The header identifies your current organization and department. If you have access
to more than one department, open the user menu and choose **Department** before
opening or creating work. With a single available department, it is selected for
you. Changing departments closes the previous workspace; finish or save your work
before switching.

Members see only their assigned departments. Organization administrators can
select any active department in their organization, but the selected department
still determines the records displayed. A department administrator's operational
authority applies only to that department; it is not organization-wide access.
Organization-wide data grants may be available in each department.

If a department is unavailable or an expected Prospect record is missing, ask
your organization administrator or Phaeno to review the assignment. Do not change
URLs to try to access another department. Organization and department administrators can open **Departments** from the
user menu to manage the departments within their authority.

## Manage department access and settings

Organization administrators manage all departments, organization defaults, the
default department, and department lifecycle. Department administrators manage
settings and member access only in their assigned departments. A person can be
an administrator in Research and a member in Operations; granting one department
role does not grant organization-wide administrator rights.

In **Departments**, use a department's actions menu to **Edit settings** or
**Manage members**. To add an existing organization member, enter their exact
email, choose **Find member**, and review the access change before confirming.
Only an organization administrator can invite someone new through **User
management** or change an organization role. Organization administrators already
have access to every department and cannot be restricted by a department admin.
An active member must retain at least one active department assignment.

Organization administrators use **Edit organization defaults** to set the
purchase-order rule, billing and notification email, and shipping and result
instructions. A department override takes precedence; a blank override inherits
the organization value, then the existing system or commercial fallback. Saved
shipping and accepted-quote snapshots retain their recorded values. Result
instructions do not change the storage destination or grant an ordering service.

Invitations require reviewed department access for members. Select each
department and grant its administrator role only when needed. Organization
administrators receive access to every department, including future additions.
If settings changed while you were editing, review your preserved entries against
the latest version before saving again. Closing an unsaved form asks whether to
discard the changes.

## Understand roles

- **Member** can review and download package versions explicitly granted to the current Prospect organization.
- **Organization administrator** can also review organization download history and respond to applicable package-governance instructions.

Prospect organization administrators manage membership only for their current organization. In **User management**, active members and invited people appear in one list. A **Pending invitation** status means account registration is incomplete. Use the card’s actions menu to resend or revoke that invitation, edit an active member’s organization role, or deactivate another member’s membership. An administrator cannot deactivate their own membership from User management. Restoring a deactivated membership requires a fresh invitation and acceptance. Contact Phaeno for a globally disabled account or an action the organization administrator cannot complete.

## Resolve common access states

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Access unavailable after sign-in | Choose **Continue invitation** if it is shown, then review and accept the saved invitation. Otherwise, sign out and reopen the newest invitation link. |
| Invitation link unavailable | Ask an administrator to confirm that the invitation is still pending and resend it. Use only the newest link. |
| Email does not match | Sign out and use the email address that received the invitation, or request a corrected invitation. |
| No active memberships | Ask your Prospect administrator to coordinate a fresh invitation with Phaeno. |
| Wrong organization shown | Stop before opening data and contact Phaeno to verify the active membership. |
| Organization unavailable | Ask Phaeno whether the organization is inactive. |
| Account disabled | Contact Phaeno. A Prospect administrator cannot override a globally disabled account. |
| Authenticator lost, backup code available | Use one backup code to sign in, then contact Phaeno for an MFA reset. |
| Authenticator and backup codes lost | Contact Phaeno for identity verification and an MFA reset. Do not send old codes. |

Never send a password, sign-in code, invitation token, or browser storage value in a support message.
