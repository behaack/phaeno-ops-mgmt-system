# Data Library, billing, and organization access

Partner access is divided among curated Phaeno data, operational Partner work, commercial records, and organization membership.

## Use curated data

The Data Library contains exact package versions that Phaeno explicitly granted across your Partner organization or to your selected department. Active members can review the package summary, quality information, manifest, and checksums, then download individual files or a complete archive.

Packages are read-only and remain separate from reagent and data-assembly records. A later package version does not replace an existing grant automatically.

Phaeno may revoke or quarantine access. Those actions block new portal viewing and downloads but cannot recall files already downloaded. Partner administrators receive safe follow-up guidance when action on prior downloads is required.

## Understand commercial documents

POMS preserves immutable quotes and commercial source records. Phaeno Finance prepares journal entries and invoices through a manual accounting handoff outside the Partner workspace. The portal does not collect payment-card information or provide hosted payment links.

Reagent pricing is negotiated per Partner and snapshotted when an order is placed. Data-assembly pricing is per job through an immutable quote. Assembly credit status is a separate audited setting that determines Net 30 or paid-before-download release.

## Manage the Partner team

The authorization model permits Partner organization administrators to invite people by email and assign member or administrator access only within their organization. Invitations are organization-specific and must be accepted by the invited email account.

The **User management** screen saves invitations, eligible role changes, and membership deactivation to the Portal account system. Membership deactivation preserves historical actions, and a fresh invitation is required for reactivation. Phaeno administrators control global account and organization status.

Organization and department administrators can review curated download history for the selected department. This includes downloads from packages shared across the organization. Organization administrators can switch departments to review each department's activity. Department administrators see only activity within their assigned departments.

Older download records without a recorded department remain available only to organization administrators, for organization-wide grants or grants assigned to the selected department. Changing a person's department assignments does not move or erase their earlier download records.

Operational downloads and order exports remain scoped to the selected Partner and department, with the applicable release gate for downloads.

Safety and withdrawal instructions about previously supplied data continue to go to current organization administrators during an organization suspension. These notices do not restore Portal access. Contact Phaeno if you cannot record a required response in the portal.
