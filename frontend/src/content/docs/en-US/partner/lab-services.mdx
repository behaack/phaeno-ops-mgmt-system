# Use Partner Lab services

PSeq Lab Service permission applies to the service family. Choose an available offering within that family; each offering has its own price, supported samples and scientific scope. Inactive offerings are unavailable for new work. Additional-sample quotes retain the service accepted for the Job.

## Additional samples after acceptance

Phaeno can issue a separate **Change quote** for additional samples on an active accepted Job. An organization or assigned-Department administrator reviews the added source counts, incremental charge, tax status and expiry, then accepts with any required purchase order number or declines the addition. The original agreement and existing work remain in effect. Expired or superseded proposals cannot be accepted.

After acceptance, prepare the added phases independently when the Job uses paired preparation. Earlier Jobs retain individual sample-list entry and finalization. Previously authorized samples stay locked; only new samples receive additional laboratory and shipping authorization. Laboratory review may block unsafe additions without saving partial work. Completion includes accepted additions in the Job's invoice; declining the proposal does not cancel the Job.

When Phaeno records the first container's arrival, the Job shows **Received**. Individual sample receipt follows verification of actual tubes. A sample shows **Accessioned** after all its expected tubes across shipments have been accessioned. Partial receipt remains visible while other tubes are outstanding. Receipt does not mean scientific acceptance or analysis has begun.

The Job list and header show recorded laboratory progress: **Received → Library Prep → Sequencing → Data Assembly → Quality Review → Results Available**. **Progress** shows each cohort’s current sample stages; expand the phase to review individual samples. Counts describe current work, not completion checkmarks. Partial release does not complete the whole Job. The **Order status** filter still finds commercial stages such as In Progress, On Hold or Completed.

**Sequencing** requires recorded sequencing activity; shipping to the sequencing provider alone is insufficient. **Quality Review** includes scientific review and approved results awaiting release. **Results Available** requires an actual release. Holds, cancellation requests, and completed or cancelled orders retain their visible status.

Use Lab services within the selected Department to describe and price a Job before entering its exact sample list. Organization and Department administrators may prepare a Job. An organization administrator or an administrator of the selected Department may place a configured standard order or accept an applicable manual quote.

## Follow the Job workspace

For a Job with one phase, **Shipping (1 sample)** or **Shipping (N samples)** identifies the work to send. **Progress** shows status, timing, samples, shipments and holds directly; there is no Phase 1 row to expand. Before tubes and containers are recorded, physical receipt reads **Awaiting sample shipment**. TAT starts after all required samples are received. The ordered phase list and phase instructions below apply to Jobs with multiple phases.

The current task stays above four supporting tabs. Accepted Jobs open in **Progress**; unaccepted requests open in **Order and billing** for pricing review. For multi-phase Jobs, **Progress** lists every cohort, including phases already sent and phases still planned. Expand a phase to review its samples, shipment links, physical receipt, laboratory stages, turnaround and specimen holds. A phase's shipment reference opens its saved shipment, where permitted instructions and reprinting remain available.

**Files and results** contains released packages and downloads. For multi-phase Jobs, use **Phase** to show one cohort or **All phases**. **Order and billing** contains the accepted scope, quote and permitted invoices. **History** combines order decisions and timing changes, newest first, with submitted request snapshots. Changing supporting tabs retains the current shipping task and saved work.

Sample preparation and shipment controls appear immediately beneath the current task when its preparation or Send step needs them. Request and Receive do not show an unrelated preparation form. Active sample holds and customer requests remain prominent above the work areas; **Review holds** opens the affected phase. Expand its **Specimen holds** to review status and permitted actions. A hold request blocks new work and release but does not establish that an ongoing procedure has stopped.

## Request and prepare kits by phase

Before acceptance, **Review and Accept Order** keeps the focus on scope, pricing
and the quote's **Actions** menu. Shipping steps and next-step guidance are hidden
until the order is accepted.

After **Confirm price and order** succeeds, the confirmation closes and **Progress** opens automatically. Use the shipping task's next action when eligible, including **Request transportation kits**; acceptance itself does not request kits.

Accept or place the Job once, confirming price and Sample type. This does not order transportation kits. When ready, choose **Request transportation kits** in **Next step** and confirm the Department delivery address. The request covers the current phase only. Kit capacity is based on its physical samples, not sequencing runs. Each phase uses distinct kits; received compatible unused stock is allocated once and only the shortage is requested. Kits and outbound delivery remain included in the agreed price.

If previously received kits cover every sample in the phase, the first two steps show **Existing kits allocated** and **Received · existing stock**. **Next step** explains that no new delivery is needed and opens sample preparation. A new kit delivery stays at **Receive kits** until every requested kit is physically received and confirmed. Under **Receive kits**, the status shows **Request received**, **Sent**, or **Received**; partial dispatch and receipt show **Partially sent** or **Partially received**.

**Shipping: Phase name (X samples)** identifies the current phase and its sample count, automatically following phase order. **Next step** names the action and offers its button: request kits, record physical receipt, prepare samples or review shipments. After samples are sent, **View progress** follows receipt and laboratory work. After every required shipment from the current phase is recorded as sent, the page opens the next phase at **Request transportation kits**. Request its kits whenever you are ready; you do not need to wait for the preceding phase’s results. **Progress** keeps physical receipt, laboratory progress and available result counts visible for every cohort. Laboratory processing still follows the agreed phase order. Approved cancelled phases are skipped.

Under **Send and record shipments**, one button follows the selected shipment: **Review and confirm shipment contents**, then **Print shipping insert**, then **Record shipment** after you confirm the insert is printed and packed. Packing instructions open inside **Confirm printed and packed** after printing; keep them open while packing or collapse them when finished. You can also use **Actions → View shipping instructions**. Repeat for each physical kit.

While Phaeno prepares the kits, **Next step** shows **Wait for Phaeno to send kits**, with no receipt action. **View kit order** beside the shipping heading opens the saved address, quantities and fulfillment status. Once Phaeno has sent a kit awaiting receipt, **Record kit receipt** becomes available in **Next step** to an organization or assigned-Department administrator. Confirm each arrived physical kit by scanning its barcode only after arrival. If all sent kits in a partial delivery have been received, wait for Phaeno to send the remaining kits before recording more receipts. **Cancel** returns without recording receipt. **Cancel kit request** closes only an unshipped request; correct it by requesting again. Dispatched history stays intact. After receipt, **View kit order** remains beside the phase heading, in **Actions** when several secondary actions are available. Other phase orders and earlier whole-Job requests remain available as history.

Save one non-PHI Sample ID with a physical tube barcode from a received kit assigned to that phase. Match its accepted biological sources, sample quantity and sequencing-run allocation, then choose **Confirm phase pairs**. This locks and authorizes that phase and creates its return shipments; later phases can wait until their samples are ready. Review the matching shipping inserts and record carrier handoff. TAT begins when Phaeno physically receives every required sample for that phase.

For earlier Jobs, the Job has two areas. **Ordering and shipping** shows six horizontal icons: **Pricing review** (or **Confirm pricing** when a quote is ready), **Sample identification**, **Container supply**, **Assign containers**, **Match samples to tubes**, and **Send samples**. Checkmarks show completed work, the current step is highlighted, and future steps are muted. On a narrow screen, move along the step strip to review the remaining steps. Once transportation kits are ordered and compatible containers are still needed, **Your next step** reads **Wait for containers to arrive**. **View kit delivery** opens the shipping area; the message distinguishes Phaeno preparing the containers from containers in transit. Confirm receipt only after the kits physically arrive. **Assign shipping containers** appears when compatible received containers are available at the selected container location; previously received stock can be used without waiting for a new delivery. **Container location** appears with container selection: one active location is shown in a read-only text box, while multiple locations offer a selector. The selector remains available when the selected location has no stock so you can check another location. The kit-order modal separately uses **Kit receiving location** for the address where new kits should arrive.

Hover over an icon, focus it with the keyboard, or tap it to read its detailed explanation and status, including who must act when work remains. Press Escape, move keyboard focus away or tap outside to close the information. Icons provide information; selecting one does not open work or change the Job. This also applies to completed and future steps. **Your next step** below the icons explains the required task, any prerequisite, and whether it is with you, your administrator, Phaeno or the carrier. Use its action button for the required work on the same Job. The steps reflect saved records; they are not checkboxes to mark yourself. Waiting for pricing or delivery is not a task you must complete personally.

**Send** includes reviewing and confirming the shipping insert, printing its current revision, following the packing instructions, putting the insert inside the correct container, handing the package to the carrier and recording the shipment. Repeat for each required container. Confirming an insert records its revision; opening a print dialog does not prove that it was printed or packed. Send is complete only when every required active shipment has been recorded as sent.

The Job's **Actions** menu gathers eligible commands under **This order** and **Selected shipment**, with the selected shipment reference shown when shipping commands apply. One available command may appear directly. Permissions and the current stage determine which commands are available; Members retain review and permitted downloads without administrator actions. Cancellation remains available under its existing rules.

Use **Progress** for current shipping, physical receipt and laboratory progress. For multi-phase Jobs, expand a phase for its recorded shipments and tracking. Other phases can still be prepared while a sent phase is processing. Any request for your attention remains above the work areas.

## Review an existing Job

If Phaeno has prepared a Job after discussing the scope with your organization, it appears in **Lab services** as **Quote in preparation** while Phaeno prices it. You do not need to submit that Job for pricing.

Open the Job and review its services, quantities, sample count, biological-source composition, storage requirements, safety declaration, and notes. When Phaeno issues the quote and it becomes **Quote issued**, an organization or department administrator can use **Actions** beside **Order details and billing** to accept it, propose changes, or decline it. Phaeno cannot accept it for your organization.

For a manually quoted Job, individual sample entry remains unavailable until your organization accepts the quote.

Lab Service records belong to your Partner organization and selected Department. Do not enter your downstream customer’s identity. Partner Lab results follow the Lab Service workflow described here. Contact Phaeno for billing records. Included Kit assembly cases are managed from their purchased PSeq Kit order.

## Submit a lab service request

**Request lab service** is available only while your organization has current ordering authorization and PSeq Lab Service is offered. If new Jobs are unavailable, the page explains whether Phaeno must enable ordering authorization or the service is temporarily unavailable. Existing accepted Jobs remain available and follow their normal hold or cancellation workflow.

1. Open **Lab services** and select **Request lab service**.
2. In **Submit lab service request**, enter a unique **Job name** and each **Biological source** with its sample count. The Portal calculates the Job's total sample count from those source groups. Enter the **Storage requirements** and **Safety declaration** shared by every sample, and any optional **Job notes**. Do not enter a patient name or identifier.
3. Select **Submit request**. The portal assigns an immutable eight-character **Job number** using unambiguous uppercase letters and numbers.
4. The request opens on its detail page at **Pricing review**, with **Waiting for pricing** as its next step. Phaeno prepares pricing for you to accept or decline. Submission does not authorize work; individual sample entry remains unavailable until you accept.

Members can review work in their assigned Department. Organization and Department administrators may create, edit, submit and accept applicable manual quotes in that Department. Organization and assigned-Department administrators may also place configured standard orders. Department authority does not grant access to other Departments.

If an older draft still contains sample rows from the former workflow, the Job identifies them as legacy rows. Clear each row’s details before submitting for pricing; enter the current sample roster again only after accepting the price.

## Existing configured standard drafts

For an existing unsubmitted draft with a configured standard offering, in **Standard PSeq Lab Service**, choose an available **Offering**. Review its allowed material types and biological sources, included scientific outputs, single delivery target in business days, sample count, purchased sample-sequencing runs, and price per sample. Standard pricing includes one library preparation, one sequencing run and data assembly per sample; it does not require a separate assembly purchase. Extra runs use the existing prepared library while material remains available and are quoted separately through pricing review.

Choose **Review standard order**. The review shows the current subtotal, approved tax, and final total. An organization or assigned-Department administrator supplies a PO when required, confirms the scope and prohibited-data statement, and selects **Place standard order**. The offering, scope, price and approved tax are retained with the Job. Detailed samples are entered afterward.

If a readiness or billing requirement is incomplete, the displayed blockers explain why placement is unavailable. Phaeno must resolve the relevant service, shipping, billing or tax setup. A standard order cannot be committed with an unknown final total. If the offering, scientific definitions, price or organization settings change while reviewing, select **Refresh terms and review again** and affirm the new terms before placing.

Describe any required sources, outputs or timing in the lab service request and its **Job notes**. There is no separate custom-work inquiry to submit for this Job.

## Submit for review

New requests are submitted for pricing when you select **Submit request**. Older unsubmitted drafts can use **Actions → Submit for pricing**. Do not include patient identifiers, PHI, or unnecessary personal data. No individual sample list is required or permitted at this stage. Submission preserves a pricing-profile revision. Phaeno can prepare pricing, request specific corrections, or reject work that cannot be performed.

While **Pricing review** is pending, use **Actions → Edit request** to change the scope and select **Submit changes**, or **Actions → Withdraw request** to stop the request. Each submitted change preserves the earlier revision and returns the latest scope to Phaeno for pricing. When pricing is issued, the first step changes to **Confirm pricing**; review the quote and accept or decline it. Direct scope edits are unavailable once pricing is issued.

When corrections are requested, create and submit a new revision. The prior revision remains in the job history; it is not silently overwritten.

## Review and accept a quote

**Order scope** shows the single **Service** for the order and its **Service quantity** in samples across phases. Additional sequencing runs are charges for that service. Each phase's TAT starts when Phaeno physically receives every required sample for that phase. Business days exclude Phaeno holidays.

For a quote with several phases, **Order details and billing** keeps each phase's scope and pricing in one block. Review its samples, biological sources, **TAT**, total sequencing runs, quantity × unit price and **Phase price**. When the saved scope and prices support it, **Runs per sample** distinguishes the one included run from additional runs, and pricing explains their quantity. For example, 15 samples with 3 total runs per sample include 1 run and add 2 runs per sample: 15 × 2 = 30 additional runs. On a narrow screen, pricing follows scope within the same phase. The quote's subtotal, tax status and total appear after the phases. The **Progress** tab tracks operational progress after the order is confirmed.

From **Actions** beside **Order details and billing**, select **Download quote PDF** below the divider to save a Phaeno-branded copy of the displayed quote for your Partner organization. It includes the Job, quote revision and dates, service and sample quantity, and any billing details and payment terms saved with that quote. Each phase keeps its recorded samples, sequencing runs, biological sources, TAT and prices in one block. When supported by the saved allocation and prices, it explains the included run, additional runs per sample and their quoted quantity. The quote total appears beside payment terms. Longer quotes continue with repeated headings and page numbers. Members can download quotes for their assigned Department; downloading does not accept a quote. If the download fails, select **Download quote PDF** again to retry. If the quote has incomplete saved scope or terms and the PDF remains unavailable, contact Phaeno with the Job number.

Laboratory services are priced per job. Phaeno issues an itemized, immutable Portal quote for the accepted scope. When your approved billing and tax information is available, the quote includes calculated tax and freezes those commercial terms. Otherwise the quote is labeled **Pre-tax total**, and applicable tax is calculated when the invoice is issued. A zero tax amount is valid for an exempt, non-taxable, or zero-rate determination. The quote is available after all non-billing readiness items are complete and a Partner administrator is active; billing must be complete before invoicing.

Before accepting, confirm the committed sample count, biological-source composition, standard service scope, price, currency, expiration date, and whether the displayed amount includes tax or is pre-tax. A Partner organization administrator or an administrator of the selected Department can accept the current unexpired quote.

If the scope changes after acceptance, Phaeno issues a separate change quote. Added work does not begin until the applicable change is accepted.

Use **Actions → Accept quote**, **Propose changes**, or **Decline quote** beside **Order details and billing**. **Download quote PDF** appears below the divider in the same menu. When downloading is the only available action, it appears directly. Declining a quote closes the entire request; the confirmation explains this before you proceed. Before a quote is available, use **Withdraw request** to close your request. After acceptance, use **Request cancellation** for Phaeno review.

**Propose changes** opens one multiline **Proposed changes** field. Explain the pricing, service, phase or timing changes you would like reviewed, using up to 2,000 characters, then select **Send proposal**. The Job stays open and returns to Phaeno for pricing review. Acceptance is paused until Phaeno issues a revised quote. Your explanation and the exact quote revision remain in history; sending a proposal does not approve your suggested terms.

When declining, choose a reason: **Our needs changed**, **Cost is too high**, **Selected another vendor**, **Prefer not to say**, or **Other**. Only **Other** requires an explanation in the multiline **Please explain** field. The selected reason is recorded with the request closure.

If accepting, declining, withdrawing, or requesting cancellation fails, review the message in the open dialog. Your entries remain available to correct and retry. Closing the dialog does not confirm the action. You are asked before discarding changed entries. While saving, the dialog stays open and its entries and actions are unavailable until the save finishes.

## Expired quotes and extension requests

An unaccepted Partner Lab Service quote shows **Expired** and its expiration date once its deadline passes. **Accept quote** appears beside **Order details and billing** for organization and department administrators; it is disabled with an explanation when acceptance is unavailable. Members can review and download the quote, and should ask an administrator to accept it.

For the current expired quote, an organization or department administrator can select **Request quote extension**, optionally explain why more time is needed, and submit the request. **Extension requested** confirms that Phaeno has the request; another request for the same quote is unnecessary. If saving fails, the dialog keeps the entered reason so you can retry.

If Phaeno issues a new revision, review its price, terms and new expiration date before accepting. An extension request does not accept a quote or automatically extend its deadline. The original revision remains in the history, and its PDF stays available. An accepted quote remains **Accepted** when its former expiration date passes.

## Ship and track samples

After placing the standard order or accepting the manual quote, open **Sample identification** in **Samples and shipping**. A blank row is provided for each remaining accepted sample, grouped under its biological source. Enter a unique **Sample ID** in each row; **Tubes** starts at 1 and can be changed if that sample includes reserves. Select **Save sample IDs** for the group. You can save part of a group and return to the remaining blank rows. The counts show saved samples; blank rows are not saved records. If a save is interrupted, confirmed saves remain and unconfirmed entries stay available for review and retry.

Use **Actions** at the right of the **Samples and shipping** heading for list options. For CSV entry, select **Actions → Download CSV template** and **Actions → Import sample list**. Import is available only while there are no saved samples or unsaved entries; discard unsaved entries and clear saved sample details first to start again with a CSV. Once all required sample IDs and tube counts are saved, the menu is replaced by **Review and finalize list**, and CSV options are hidden. The CSV accepts `customer_sample_id`, `biological_source`, `tube_count`, and optionally `sequencing_runs`; it does not accept barcodes. If `sequencing_runs` is omitted, each sample has one run. Previewing a CSV does not change the Job. Confirming a valid preview saves the complete list as one operation.

Samples are grouped by **Biological source**. Each heading shows saved and accepted sample counts, including sources with no saved entries yet. These counts measure samples, not tubes. All expected rows are visible during identification. Once the list is complete, saved samples are shown 10 per page with **Previous samples**, **Next samples**, and a range such as **Samples 1–10 of 18**. A group spanning pages repeats its heading and full count. Each saved sample’s **Actions** menu shows its sample ID in the header. Use **Actions → Edit sample** or **Actions → Clear sample details**. The **Clear sample details?** modal offers **Cancel** and **Clear sample details**. Confirming clears the saved ID and tube count while preserving the accepted source and required sample count. A blank required row returns with one tube as its default; complete it again before finalizing. Use Edit sample for simple corrections. Finalized samples retain their existing editing restrictions. The routine **Expected** lab status is hidden while preparing the list; other statuses remain visible.

Sample IDs must be unique within the Job, and tube counts must be positive whole numbers. Do not enter patient names or direct identifiers. The biological source is already supplied by the group. To correct a saved sample, use **Actions → Edit sample** and choose another accepted source with room only when it accurately describes the sample. Save or discard unsaved IDs before editing saved samples, importing, or finalizing. Leaving with unsaved entries requires confirmation.

If an existing group exceeds its accepted count, such as **2 of 1**, it shows a red warning. Its samples remain available to review and correct. Edit a sample's source only when another accepted source accurately describes it and has room, or clear an extra entry’s details with confirmation. Samples with a source outside the accepted list are also shown for correction. The list cannot be finalized until its total and every source count match the accepted scope.

Do not ship physical samples before the order is commercially committed and the exact roster is finalized. An early or unmatched package is quarantined and escalated by Phaeno; it is not accessioned or attached to active Job work.

The final list must contain exactly the accepted number of unique non-PHI sample IDs, match every accepted source-group count, and give each sample a positive whole-number tube count. A green check beside the overall counter means the total and source counts are complete; it does not finalize the list.

After saving every required sample ID and matching the accepted source counts, select **Review and finalize list** at the right of the **Samples and shipping** heading or in **Your next step**. Both buttons open the same review. Clearing sample details returns the card to its incomplete-list controls and hides the finalization shortcut until the required details are saved again. The review follows the same biological-source grouping as the sample area. Both views sort sample IDs in natural numeric order within each group, such as TEST-2 before TEST-10. The review shows source counts and the overall sample, allocated run and tube totals. Review every sample and affirm the no-PHI confirmation before selecting **Finalize sample list**. Finalization authorizes the specimen-specific Lab work and lets Phaeno prepare the registered return kit.

Use **Prepare containers** from the Job's **Actions**, or use the container preparation area in **Samples and shipping**, for the tubes awaiting allocation. Review compatible sizes by common name, SKU and usable tube capacity. The recommendation prefers the fewest containers and then the least spare capacity; it does not verify or reserve stock. An organization or Department administrator can open **Assign containers** to review **Containers to use**, starting with one row per recommended container. Each row has a compatible size selector, SKU/capacity and tube count.

**Add container** chooses a size for the capacity still needed and assigns the remaining tubes up to its capacity. A row's **Remove** action removes only that container; changing or removing it preserves the other rows' tube counts. Choices include smaller containers and the smallest size that covers the remaining need, excluding choices that make another selected container unnecessary. With sizes 5/10/20, three remaining tubes offer only a 5. For 30 tubes, 20+10 or six 5s are valid; two 20s are not offered. With 10+5 already selected, Add chooses another 10 and then a 5.

**Add container** is disabled once selected capacity covers all tubes. **Use recommendation** replaces the current rows and tube counts. Stock requirements apply automatically; there are no manual stock quantities to enter here. Another permitted plan needs no separate approval.

Review tubes, containers, usable capacity, spare slots and unallocated tubes. **Confirm assignment** creates a shipment for each container with allocated tubes. Empty containers are not created. A partly filled container is valid; spare slots are not missing sample tubes. If capacity is insufficient, **Confirm partial assignment** leaves the exact outstanding tube count visible while preparing allocated tubes. Tubes from a single sample may be split across containers, without changing accepted counts or price.

Adjust each row's tube count as needed. Counts must be whole numbers, fit each container and together equal the tubes allocated in the preview. The **Summary** stays visible while updating; confirmation becomes available when the current allocation has been checked.

Use **Shipping container** in the Job's **Samples and shipping** area to select this Partner Job's container or remaining tubes awaiting containers. With multiple choices, select deliberately before using a shipment command; one available choice is selected directly. Its reference stays visible in the work area and the **Selected shipment** action group. Switching container or returning to **Samples** asks before discarding an unsaved scan and is unavailable while saving. **Container, kit and receipt details** expands the supporting information without narrowing the scanner.

Before scanning starts, an organization or Department administrator can use **Reset container configuration** in **Container, kit and receipt details**. Review the confirmation: it returns **all containers and tubes for the Partner Job** to selection, not just the shipment you opened. The finalized sample list stays intact; previous configurations remain in the records and audit history for support. Retired configurations stay out of normal container choices; an older direct link identifies them as history. Cancelling changes nothing. **Reset container configuration** is unavailable while a barcode entry is unsaved or saving; discard an unsaved entry before revising the plan. Any saved scan, linked physical kit, shipping insert, dispatch or receipt anywhere in that Job's shipment plan locks the action. Clearing a scan does not restore it. If work starts while confirmation is open, the change is rejected; refresh and review the current state. A successful change preserves the accepted quote, kit orders and physical inventory.

Use **Match tubes** in **Samples and shipping** or the Job's **Actions** to match registered tubes in the selected container. One scan field opens inside the active tube row in the expanded sample. Container identity and overall progress stay above the list. For the displayed sample and tube number, scan its permanent barcode, enter the actual material amount and unit in that tube, then press Enter or **Save scan**. Each match is saved separately before the next sample and tube are revealed. A successful save updates the barcode and progress, expands the next unmatched tube’s sample, scrolls its row into view and focuses its inline scan field. Failed, unknown, ineligible, duplicate and already-used scans retain the entered barcode and current target for review and retry. The first saved match locks container reset.

Record **Material amount in this tube** and **Material unit** for every physical tube, such as 20 µL. Enter the amount actually being sent, separately from tube capacity or the sample's tube count. Do not assume an equal share of a sample total. Amounts must be positive, with up to six decimal places; new tube entries start blank. The frozen shipment crosswalk and CSV retain this customer declaration, its user and time, distinctly from a laboratory measurement. Earlier records with no declaration show **Unknown**.

Select **Record material amount** for a matched tube that is missing its declaration. Before dispatch, **Edit tube** or **Correct tube** updates its barcode or amount. Correcting existing information requires a reason and retains history. A correction after insert confirmation issues a new insert revision to print and pack. Every tube needs a declared amount and unit before confirming a new insert or recording dispatch.

Expand a sample to review its individual tubes, saved barcodes and container identities. The list keeps 10 samples per page; there is no separate Scan tubes view. While matching, saving reveals the next unmatched tube's sample and page. Reviewing another page or expanding a different sample does not change the active scan target or entered barcode. **Return to active tube** restores its sample and inline scan field, including the unfinished barcode. No barcode field is shown while the active sample is collapsed or on another page. The scan progress applies to the selected container; each sample's matched count covers all its active containers. **Done scanning** appears at the right of the **Samples and shipping** heading only after every tube in the selected container has a saved match. Leaving or changing containers preserves the existing unsaved-scan warning. Paging and dismissal are unavailable while saving. After the last match, the field closes and the same list remains open for review.

Use tubes from a fulfilled kit registered for this Partner Job. The first successful scan associates that physical kit with the shipping container; its other tubes must come from the same kit. Do not cover or alter permanent tube barcodes. **Edit tube** or **Correct tube** requires a correction reason and preserves history. A correction to a confirmed shipping insert before dispatch voids the old revision and issues a corrected one; destroy unused old copies.

The Job's **Actions** menu groups shipping commands under **Selected shipment** and applicable pricing, withdrawal and cancellation commands under **This order**. The selected shipment reference identifies the container affected. If only one command is available, it appears directly. Your permissions and the complete shipment's stage determine availability. **Review and confirm shipment contents** and **Record shipment** use the direct next-step button; **View shipping instructions**, **Download tube list (CSV)** and reprinting remain in **Actions**. Completing one visible page does not complete a whole container. Authorized readers retain print and download access.

Print one receiving sheet for each physical container and place it inside that container. It shows the Customer, Job/Trial and shipment references, sample and tube totals, essential handling notes and two widely separated scan targets: the large **Scan to receive this shipment** PH-P- insert barcode and, when assigned, the physical container barcode. Follow the full preparation and packing instructions in the Portal before shipping. Follow the approved temperature-control method, amount and packing steps for the actual container. Instructions may call for regular ice, dry ice, cold packs, another method or no cooling. Do not substitute methods or multiply the amount by the number of samples. Contact Phaeno if the instructions are missing or conflict. **Full packing instructions and sample / tube list** on the insert page retains the confirmed manifest, every sample and tube barcode, and split-sample references; these details do not print with the receiving sheet. Use **Download tube list (CSV)** to retain the complete crosswalk.

Phaeno's later Sample type or Shipping procedure changes do not silently replace the instructions selected for a placed Job. An explicit Phaeno safety hold prevents a new shipping insert until Phaeno resolves it; an insert already issued keeps its saved instructions. Contact Phaeno if the Job shows a hold or its instructions need review.

Under **Send**, the next-step button first opens **Review and confirm shipment contents**, then becomes **Print shipping insert**. **Actions → View shipping instructions** shows the saved destination, shared steps, sample requirements and actual container's packing instructions, including the cooling method and amount. Printing checks the current revision and keeps the selected container and saved matches intact. After the print dialog returns, confirm that the current insert was printed and placed inside its container only when you have done both. Cancelling printing, dismissing the confirmation or a print error does not acknowledge the work. If the insert cannot be checked, reconnect if needed and choose **Try again**.

After your printing-and-packing confirmation, the next-step button becomes **Record shipment**. Your confirmation is remembered in this browser tab for this shipment's current insert. A corrected insert must be printed and confirmed again. You must still record the shipment after carrier handoff. With several shipments, deliberately select the container you are working on. A sole available container remains selected directly. Reopen **Actions → View shipping instructions** at any time. Reprint through **Actions** for another copy of the current revision; reprinting does not issue a new revision. Retain the shipping insert or use **Download tube list (CSV)** for your organization's sample records.

After handoff to the carrier, use **Record shipment** for each container's carrier, tracking number and shipment time. If you close the form after entering details, confirm whether to discard them; the form stays open while a save is in progress. **Progress** shows the recorded shipments and their receipt progress while other containers may still need preparation. A sample stays partly received until all its expected tubes are recorded; container arrival and individual tube accession are separate laboratory steps. Work stays on the Job. Existing standalone shipment bookmarks remain available, and retained Trial shipping keeps its existing entry point.

After finalization, each sample's **Matched** count shows saved tube matches across its active shipments. Expand the sample to review its tubes and their containers. Once a shipment is sent, tubes are received, or laboratory progress is recorded, the row also shows relevant **Receipt** and laboratory details. Receipt means actual tubes recorded as received, such as **1 of 4 tubes received**; matching barcodes does not establish receipt. These counts cover the whole sample even when its tubes are split across containers. Accession details and review reasons remain visible when present. **Checking...** means information is loading; **Not available** means it cannot currently be shown, not that the count is zero.

Use **Progress** for current shipping, physical receipt and laboratory progress. For multi-phase Jobs, expand a phase for its recorded shipments and tracking. Other phases can still be prepared while a sent phase is processing. Any request for your attention remains above the work areas.

If Phaeno needs replacement material or clarification, the job shows a Partner-safe action and the current schedule health. Internal scientific investigation details are not shown.

**Ready for release** means Phaeno's scientific review is complete and the final deliverable package is complete, checksummed, and malware-clean. It does not mean a file is downloadable until an authorized Result Release Manager publishes that sample. Invoice balance and credit status never gate PSeq result release.

## Understand turnaround

For newly approved Jobs, the quote or standard-order review shows one delivery target before approval. The business-day clock starts after Phaeno physically receives every required tube for every sample, including across separate shipments. Business days are Monday–Friday, excluding Phaeno holidays. At that point the due date is set. Earlier approved Jobs keep the turnaround terms shown when they were placed.

In **Progress**, review complete receipt, TAT and delivery due date (expand the relevant phase for a multi-phase Job), including the original due date when changed. **Job milestones** retains first receipt, scientific acceptance, laboratory completion and full Portal-delivery time. One-phase Jobs also show original and current processing/delivery targets there. Timing changes and customer-safe reasons appear in **History**. Receipt, scientific acceptance and release remain distinct.

Phaeno may update the expected completion with a recorded reason. The original target remains visible. A later expected date queues a notice; an earlier date updates the Portal. Read the customer-safe explanation and current dates on the Job rather than treating an earlier message as the current schedule.

## Cancel work

For an eligible Job, choose the red **Request cancellation** item in its header's **Actions** menu. For an eligible phase, expand it in **Progress** and use that phase's **Actions → Request cancellation**. Cancellation stays inside **Actions** even when it is the only available action. Review the affected scope and enter the required reason before submitting; closing the dialog leaves the Job and phase unchanged.

A draft can be discarded before submission. Before Phaeno accepts work, an administrator can cancel eligible scope. After acceptance, submit a cancellation request. Phaeno records whether the request is approved, partially approved, or declined. Any correction to an issued PSeq invoice is retained as an append-only credit, debit, or write-off adjustment rather than editing the invoice.

## Find and resume work

Search and filter by status, dates, or **Submitted by me**. **Previous** and **Next** show the complete matching result set. From a Job, select **Back to lab services** to return with the filters and page retained. **Clear all** removes them. An empty filtered result is distinct from a failed load; use **Retry records** when loading fails.

## What appears at each stage

While reviewing an order or quote, the **Order and billing** tab opens by default, with **Order details and billing** visible.
Its heading row gathers **Accept quote** and **Decline quote** under **Actions** when your permissions
and the current order allow those actions. Review the total sample count and the
biological-source counts in **Order scope**, with pricing and expiry in **Quote and billing**.
Accepting still opens the confirmation dialog and any required purchase-order
entry; selecting the first button alone does not place the order. Expired quotes
cannot be accepted, and Members retain their existing review-only permissions.

Preparation and shipment controls appear beneath the current task when needed. Saved submission instructions appear with preparation. Previously saved samples and shipment records remain available in **Progress**; **Order and billing** remains accessible through its tab.

After a shipment is sent, expand its phase in **Progress** and open the shipment reference. Use its permitted **Reprint shipping insert** action. Reprinting opens the current insert without issuing a new revision or recording another shipment or receipt.


Shipping inserts, printable kit labels and retained packet copies show QR codes with readable identifiers. Phaeno-printed laboratory tube labels use DataMatrix and readable identifiers. Saved scan results show the identifier as text. Use a 2D scanner that supports both QR and DataMatrix where both workflows are performed. Existing manufacturer labels and older printed barcodes remain valid; do not relabel a tube or change its saved identity merely to match a display. A scan still requires the correct workflow and does not by itself confirm receipt or accession.


## Multiple tubes for one sample

Standard pricing is per **sample** and includes one library preparation, one sequencing run and data assembly. Additional runs are priced separately and use the prepared library while material remains available. Twenty samples sequenced once require twenty standard services. One sample sequenced twenty times requires one standard service plus nineteen additional runs. Both allocate twenty **sample-sequencing runs** for laboratory execution. In the Job profile, leave **Sample-sequencing runs** blank for one run per sample, or enter the total requested runs. A machine batch is not the pricing unit.

After accepting pricing, enter each sample once. When pricing includes one run per sample, **Runs** shows a fixed **1** during sample entry, and **Sample-sequencing runs** is fixed at **1** in **Edit sample**. **Tubes** remains editable: you may send reserve tubes so material is available in case of failure, without buying extra runs. For Jobs explicitly priced with additional runs, allocate those purchased runs across samples; the total must equal the purchased run count before **Review and finalize list** becomes available. The CSV template includes **sequencing_runs** and validates the same purchased total; an older three-column CSV defaults each sample to one run. Existing accepted prices and previously recorded scope are preserved.

Phaeno reviews each received tube independently and selects one suitable source. A reserve is used only after the preceding processing attempt is explicitly closed as failed. If no material remains for further permitted analysis, the sample can have a failed processing outcome even though a tube passed receipt checks. Follow the Job's customer-facing progress and any request for additional information.

With one container, the tube count is assigned automatically up to its capacity and shown in a read-only text box. Any remaining tubes stay unallocated. With multiple containers, edit the tube counts to split the allocation. Returning to one container restores its automatic allocation.

Samples are ordered by ID alphabetically and digit by digit, rather than by numeric value. In the Job, biological-source groups stay together. Tube matching follows that sample order, with each sample’s tubes in tube-number order. Saving a match advances to the next unmatched tube.

Sample rows show matching progress as **1 of 1 tube matched** or **2 of 2 tubes matched**. This includes the tube total, so the row does not repeat a separate quantity while matching status is shown. Long sample IDs wrap; they are not shortened or changed.

### Delivery due date

The delivery due date is separate from the current expected completion. Phaeno may adjust it with a recorded customer-safe reason; the original delivery target remains visible, and the change queues a notice. A forecast change alone does not move the delivery due date. Results are delivered when data for every in-scope sample has been made available through the Portal; you do not have to download or acknowledge them. Partial results and laboratory completion do not by themselves complete delivery. Standard turnaround remains an operating target unless your agreement explicitly provides a guarantee.

## Requesting a specimen pause or resumption

An organization administrator or administrator of the assigned Department can open a Lab Job, select **Progress**, expand the affected phase, and open **Specimen holds**. Choose **Request pause** for the affected sample and enter a reason. Other samples are unaffected.

The request immediately blocks new preparation, new sequencing handoffs, new analysis and result release for that sample. **Pause requested** does not mean an ongoing physical procedure has stopped. Phaeno must confirm a safe pause, including any work already with an external provider. If that procedure cannot pause, its facts can still be recorded; new work and release remain blocked.

Use **Request resumption** when ready. Work remains blocked until Phaeno approves resumption. Other authorization, material, quality and workflow requirements still apply, and approval does not automatically start work. Read **Hold history** for the recorded reasons and decisions.

A hold does not automatically change charges, deadlines or retention dates, and results already delivered remain available. Contact Phaeno directly for an urgent request or a separate commercial or retention adjustment.

## Follow phased Jobs

One Job may have several ordered phases, each with its own samples, agreed turnaround expectation and billing portion. After confirmation, the **Progress** tab shows each cohort, receipt counts, current sample stages and delivery due date. **Mixed** means samples are at different stages; **Received** remains the customer-facing receipt status. Held and review counts accompany progress. Results complete a phase only when all required outputs for its samples and purchased runs are available through the Portal. The next phase can then begin processing.

A phase's TAT starts when Phaeno physically receives the last required tube across that phase's samples and containers. Receiving a future cohort early starts its clock even while it waits for the preceding phase. A container arrival alone is not complete tube receipt.

An administrator can select the red **Actions → Request cancellation** item for an eligible phase before the first required tube is received. Phaeno reviews the request; a request is not approval. Receipt closes eligibility. Cancellation retains the recorded scope and any invoices for separate financial review.

Approved cancelled cohorts are excluded from outstanding preparation counts. Prepare the remaining phases; existing cancelled sample and tube records remain in the history. Phaeno must establish a phase's biological-source and run allocation before approving cancellation if those allocations have not been recorded.

Phaeno may propose rephasing an open Job. Review the previous plan, proposed counts, TAT, billing portions and named sample moves, then use **Actions → Accept rephasing** or **Decline rephasing**. Only unsent future samples can move, including while earlier work runs. Sending any required tube fixes that sample's assignment; received and processing samples remain fixed. Acceptance rechecks current facts and retains original commitments and issued invoices. Closed Jobs cannot be rephased.

Invoices follow the agreed billing schedule and can cover upfront or completed work, or portions of several phases. Phase completion does not automatically issue an invoice. Payment does not gate PSeq result release.
