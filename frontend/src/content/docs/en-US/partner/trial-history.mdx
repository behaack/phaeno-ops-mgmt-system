# Trial history after Partner conversion

If your organization previously had a Prospect Trial Project, **Trial projects** preserves the approved scope, samples, customer-safe progress, releases, and history in the original department. Active authorized members may view that history under the same organization identity.

**For Research Use Only. Not for use in diagnostic procedures.** Keep PHI and sample identity mappings outside the Portal. A Trial release archive includes its approved scope/file manifest.

Partner conversion does not reopen a Trial or authorize new Trial samples. Partner reagent orders and data-assembly requests use their own workflows and permissions. Trial results are not subject to Partner pricing or payment gates.

Open **Retention and receipt** for the original release policy and download history. Conversion does not extend file retention or grace dates. Download available files before the displayed deadline. Withdrawn, held, or deleted files remain unavailable; retained history does not restore deleted bytes. Contact Phaeno with the Trial number if a reissue is needed.
