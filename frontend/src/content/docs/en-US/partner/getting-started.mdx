# Getting started

Portal gives Partner organizations a tenant-isolated workspace for reagent orders, data-assembly requests, released outputs, curated data, and organization access.

## Navigate the help center

Guide navigation opens beneath the toolbar from the far-left edge of the screen. Its header identifies Documentation and, on wider screens, provides the pin or unpin icon. Pointer users can preview an unpinned sidebar by approaching the left edge; keyboard and touch users open it from the tab. Choosing a guide closes an unpinned sidebar and displays the article.

## Confirm the current Partner organization

The user menu shows the organization active in your current session. Prices, offerings, requests, files, and permissions always come from that organization. A Partner cannot see another Partner’s negotiated prices or work.

Self-service switching between multiple external organization memberships is not currently available. If the wrong organization is active, or your account belongs to more than one external organization, contact Phaeno before continuing.

## Know your access level

Partner organization administrators can create and place reagent orders, create and submit data-assembly requests, accept applicable commercial changes, decide substitutions, request cancellations, and manage members. Other active Partner members have read, progress-tracking, and eligible output-download access.

## Find your work

- **Dashboard** shows live organization-scoped counts and recent status for reagent orders, data-assembly requests, and assigned data, with a User management starting point for administrators.
- **Reagent orders** contains the organization’s available products, drafts, placed orders, substitutions, and shipments.
- **Data assembly** contains input revisions, quotes, processing status, and released outputs.
- **Data Library** contains Phaeno-curated packages explicitly assigned to the Partner.
- For organization administrators, **User management** manages durable invitations, Member or Organization administrator roles, and membership deactivation.
- **Docs** opens the Partner guides.

## Protect submitted data

The initial workflows are not designed to collect protected health information. Do not place patient identifiers, credentials, or unnecessary confidential information in project names, notes, file names, manifests, or uploads.

Use a Partner-controlled project reference and follow the metadata and file requirements shown by the selected assembly profile.

## Get help

Provide the portal order, request, shipment, or project reference when contacting Phaeno. Never include passwords, authentication tokens, or sensitive file contents in a support message.

## Earlier Trial Projects

If your organization converted from Prospect, **Trial Projects** retains the approved Trial history and available results in their original department. Conversion preserves existing download deadlines. See [Trial history](./trial-history).

## Search and browse guides

Use **Search documentation** at the top of Docs to find a guide or a matching section. Results update after a short pause; use at least two characters. Try “reagent orders” or “data assembly”.

Choose **Filters** to narrow results by Topic, Workflow, or Guide type. **Clear filters** removes those selections. **Browse by topic** opens guides for a topic without requiring search words. Workflow links and **Related guides** at the end of an article provide other ways to find relevant instructions.

Select a result title to open its matching section. Your browser’s Back action restores the search and filters. Search uses the guides for your current organization; it does not include the public Website or operational records. A guide’s instructions do not change your account permissions.

If no guides match, try fewer words or clear the filters. If search is temporarily unavailable, use **Try again** or **Browse all guides**. A documentation update may require **Refresh page**; guide browsing remains available while the update finishes.
