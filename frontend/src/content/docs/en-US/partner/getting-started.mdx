# Getting started

Portal gives Partner organizations a workspace for your Partner organization for PSeq Kit orders, Lab Service Jobs, included assembly cases, released outputs, curated data, and organization access.

## Find current work

The dashboard and header bell show current work in your selected organization
and Department. The bell counts unfinished actions; opening an item does not
complete it. Available results and work waiting on an administrator or Phaeno
are separate from your own actions. Open the linked record to perform the
supported next step, or use **View all work** for the complete filtered list.
Unavailable counts remain unknown until they can be refreshed.

Current additional-scope Lab quotes and proposed Kit substitutions appear as
administrator decisions. Other members see those decisions as waiting. Later
Lab phases retain their shipping attention after earlier work begins.
**Needs review** marks elevated work without a deadline; **Due soon** refers to
a recorded due date.

For a Change quote, use its **Actions** control to download the quote or open
acceptance/decline review. Unsaved decision details ask for explicit discard on
Cancel, Escape or navigation. A pending save must finish before you leave.

## Navigate the help center

Page-return links use a left arrow and name the destination. Use them to return
from a record to its list or originating workflow. If more than one return
destination is offered, choose the one that matches your task.

Open the user menu in the toolbar and choose **Documentation**. This entry is available on both wide and narrow screens. On smaller screens, the Documentation tab sits below the toolbar and organization details.

On narrow screens, the user menu opens a full-width tray from the right. Choose **Close** or press Escape to dismiss it and return to its toolbar button. Your workspace links appear directly in the tray. When configuration pages are available to your role, choose **Settings** to expand them. The section starts collapsed each time you open the menu; use Enter or Space to toggle it, Up and Down to move among visible links, and Escape to close the user menu.

Guide navigation opens beneath the toolbar from the far-left edge of the screen. Its header identifies Documentation and, on wider screens, provides the pin or unpin icon. Pointer users can preview an unpinned sidebar by approaching the left edge; keyboard and touch users open it from the tab. Choosing a guide closes an unpinned sidebar and displays the article. On narrow screens, click or tap outside the sidebar to close it; its tab and Escape also close it.

## Confirm the current Partner organization

The header shows the organization active in your current session. Prices, offerings, requests, files, and permissions always come from that organization. A Partner cannot see another Partner’s negotiated prices or work.

If your account has more than one active organization membership, open the user menu and select one of your own organizations from the list beside the organization icon. The same select list is available in the narrow-screen menu tray. Switching returns to Dashboard and selects an available Department in that organization; its permissions, prices, records and guides apply. Save your work first, or cancel an unsaved-page warning to remain in the current organization. If an expected organization is missing, ask its administrator to review your access.

## Know your access level

Partner organization administrators and department administrators may prepare Kit and Lab drafts, submit included assembly inputs, accept applicable manual quotes or changes, decide substitutions, and request cancellations within their operational Department. Organization and assigned-Department administrators may place configured Lab Service and PSeq Kit purchases or request custom work for that Department. Organization administrators also manage organization-wide invitations and roles. Other active Partner members have read, progress-tracking, and eligible output-download access.

## Find your work

- **Dashboard** separates **Needs attention**, **Waiting on others**, and **Available results and updates** for your selected Department. Follow an item's link to its owning record, or use **View all work** for the complete paged list. Use **First page** if a saved page is beyond the last page. **Retry work** recovers unavailable counts and list reads.
- **PSeq Kit orders** contains the organization’s available products, drafts, placed orders, substitutions, and shipments.
- **Assembly cases** contains included input revisions, processing status and outputs, plus historical standalone requests.
- **Lab services** contains Lab Jobs, configured offering reviews, samples, shipping and released PSeq results. See [Partner Lab services](./lab-services).
- **Data Library** contains Phaeno-curated packages explicitly assigned to the Partner.
- For organization administrators, **User management** manages durable invitations, Member or Organization administrator roles, and membership deactivation.
- **Documentation** in the user menu opens the Partner guides.

## Protect submitted data

The initial workflows are not designed to collect protected health information. Do not place patient identifiers, credentials, or unnecessary confidential information in project names, notes, file names, manifests, or uploads.

Use a Partner-controlled project reference and follow the metadata and file requirements shown by the selected assembly profile.

## Get help

Provide the portal order, request, shipment, or project reference when contacting Phaeno. Never include passwords, authentication tokens, or sensitive file contents in a support message.

## Earlier Trial Projects

If your organization converted from Prospect, **Trial Projects** retains the approved Trial history and available results in their original department. Conversion preserves existing download deadlines. See [Trial history](./trial-history).

## Search and browse guides

Use **Search documentation** at the top of Documentation to find a guide or a matching section. Results update after a short pause; use at least two characters. Try “reagent orders” or “data assembly”.

Choose **Filters** to narrow results by Topic, Workflow, or Guide type. **Clear all** removes those selections. **Browse by topic** opens guides for a topic without requiring search words. Workflow links and **Related guides** at the end of an article provide other ways to find relevant instructions.

Select a result title to open its matching section. Your browser’s Back action restores the search and filters. Search uses the guides for your current organization; it does not include the public Website or operational records. A guide’s instructions do not change your account permissions.

If no guides match, try fewer words or clear the filters. If search is temporarily unavailable, use **Try again** or **Browse all guides**. A documentation update may require **Refresh page**; guide browsing remains available while the update finishes.
