# Included assembly inputs and outputs

Each purchased PSeq Kit unit includes one assembly case. Organization and Department administrators prepare its files and metadata within the selected Department. The case belongs to your Partner organization; Portal does not collect the downstream customer's identity.

## Prepare an included case

1. Open **PSeq Kit orders**, select the order, and find **Included assembly cases**.
2. After the kit is shipped, select **Prepare inputs** on the intended case. Check its kit label, lot and submission deadline.
3. Enter the Partner project or reference and required metadata.
4. Review the read-only included profile and outputs. These are frozen from purchase and cannot be replaced with another catalog profile.
5. Choose allowed input files within the displayed per-file and total limits, and confirm the prohibited-data statement.
6. Select **Save draft** or **Submit for intake validation**.

Files must complete managed upload and clean-scan checks before submission. Do not include patient identifiers, PHI or unnecessary personal data. **Assembly cases** is a list of included cases and retained historical requests; new standalone assembly purchases are not created from that list.

## Submit and correct

Submission freezes an immutable input revision. Phaeno validates the input and may request field- or file-specific changes or reject it with a Partner-visible reason. Valid included work proceeds to the processing queue without a second quote or purchase.

If changes are requested, open **Edit request**. **Input files** shows every saved input and its scan result. Use **Remove input** to exclude an incorrect or failed-scan file from the editable revision, then select its replacement. Removing a current input preserves earlier submitted revisions. Review the profile's file types and per-file/total limits before uploading.

Save the draft or submit the corrected revision. A failure after draft creation retains the same request. Successfully uploaded files remain listed; retry resumes the remaining work. **Open saved draft** returns to the saved record when final submission has not succeeded. Earlier revisions remain preserved for traceability.

## Historical standalone requests

Earlier standalone assembly requests retain their original per-job quote, PO, approval and commercial history. Their existing quote controls remain available when applicable. Those historical terms do not introduce another quote or sale for a purchased Kit case.

## Track and download outputs

The request shows intake review, queued work, processing, output review, output availability, holds, and completion. An approved output release contains an immutable manifest and one or more checksummed files tied to the source input revision and assembly profile.

For included cases, scientifically ready outputs use the original Kit shipment commercial source and the Partner assembly-credit or recorded-payment decision. No second invoice, commercial source or sale is created. Credit-approved Partners may download under their approved credit terms. Partners whose original purchase has neither approved assembly credit nor sufficient recorded payment can see that outputs are ready but cannot download them; contact Phaeno about the original Kit payment or credit decision.

Use **Download package** to retrieve every file in a release as one ZIP, or download a file individually. Only a complete server response counts. Partial, cancelled, failed, and timed-out transfers remain undownloaded, and an active transfer is shown as in progress until it reaches a terminal outcome. One completed download by any currently authorized Partner member satisfies that file for the organization; the Partner view does not expose the member's identity.

Each newly released output shows the retention schedule frozen when that release became available. **Standard deletion** is the normal deletion time. **Conditional grace through** applies only when at least one file in the release remains undownloaded at the standard deadline; when that happens, the whole release receives the displayed grace period. Dates and times include the browser's local time-zone label. An earlier output created before retention snapshots were introduced does not show a schedule; contact Phaeno before relying on a deletion date for that release.

A corrected output is a new release. Partner users download the release and deliver it through their own customer process; Portal does not collect the Partner’s end-customer identity or deliver directly to that customer.

Use the available withdrawal or cancellation-request action on the request. Phaeno records unused-case cancellation from the Kit order; completed work and the original purchase history remain preserved.


Where retention enforcement is enabled, file and ZIP requests close at the same
frozen deadline. **Grace period active** keeps an incomplete release available
through its stated final date; later completion does not shorten grace. A transfer
admitted before cutoff may finish within its original time limit. File withdrawal,
a failed scan, or removal of your access can stop an active ZIP. A failed ZIP counts
none of its files from that attempt, while earlier successful downloads remain.

Closed download controls do not indicate physical deletion. The page refreshes
availability after an unsuccessful attempt. If timing cannot be verified, contact
Phaeno; do not infer new dates for earlier releases without a schedule. These rules
do not bypass payment holds or scientific release approval.

Where scheduled retention notices are enabled for Assembly releases, active
Organization admins receive one download reminder for an incomplete release and
one grace notice if grace applies. A delayed reminder is skipped if every file has
completed or the standard deadline has passed. There are no daily reminders.
Notices identify the release and deadline and link to the authenticated assembly
request; open it for current availability. Delivery problems do not extend the
dates. Contact Phaeno if a notice needs support. Notices follow your Partner
organization's admins and do not go to its end customers.

## Keep an output receipt

Open **View retained package receipt** beside an output's retention schedule and select
**Print / save PDF**. The retained receipt lists every file, size, checksum,
original deadline, recorded completion and deletion facts, and reissue links.
It holds metadata, not downloadable copies. Times show the display time zone and
UTC. Assembly outputs are identified as project-level files; the receipt does
not infer sample mappings from the scientific manifest.

Organization admins may review the organization's downloader history. Other
members see authorized release facts within their Department. A quarantine stops
downloads; a preservation hold does not extend the deadline. Contact Phaeno for
support or an authorized reissue after deletion. Reissue requires a newly
prepared, approved package and preserves the earlier receipt and dates.

## Find and resume requests

If accepting a quote, withdrawing, or requesting cancellation fails, review the message in the open dialog. Your entries remain available to correct and retry. Closing the dialog does not confirm the action. You are asked before discarding changed entries. While saving, the dialog stays open and its entries and actions are unavailable until the save finishes. Successful changes refresh the request and its status in the list.

The list provides search, status/date filters, **Submitted by me**, total counts, and **Previous**/**Next**. Opening a request and returning keeps the filtered page. **Clear all** removes filters. Failed loads offer retry instead of reporting that no requests exist. Closing a form with unsaved entries asks whether to discard them.
