# Statuses and troubleshooting

A laboratory Job, each sample, its quote, invoice, and released result package have separate statuses. One sample can have released results while others are still processing. PSeq result access is independent of payment and Customer credit.

## Laboratory Job statuses

| Status | Meaning and next action |
| --- | --- |
| Draft request | An organization or department administrator can continue editing or withdraw the request. |
| Submitted for quote | Phaeno is reviewing the submitted revision. |
| Changes requested | Open the Job, correct the identified items, and submit the revised pricing profile. |
| Quote in preparation | Phaeno is preparing pricing. No quote acceptance is available yet. |
| Quote issued | An administrator can accept the current, unexpired quote for the selected Department. |
| Placed, awaiting samples | Enter or import and finalize the exact sample list, then follow the Job's shipping instructions. |
| In progress | Review individual samples for progress. |
| Results available | Review **Files and results** for the packages released to you. |
| On hold | Read the reason and complete the requested action or contact Phaeno. |
| Cancellation requested | Phaeno is reviewing the request; work is not automatically cancelled. |
| Completed | Work is complete. Results remain subject to the retention dates on each release. |
| Cancelled or declined | Review the history and any linked commercial adjustment. |

## Sample and result progress

**Expected**, **Received**, and **Accessioned** track intake. **Lab analysis** and **Data processing** track scientific work. Sample progress alone does not make a file downloadable. The complete output package must pass its file checks and scientific review, then a Result Release Manager publishes it.

Use the Job's **Files and results** section to review the sample, package version, files, and download availability. A withdrawn or quarantined package is unavailable even if processing finished. **Downloads closed** means the retention access window ended; it does not prove that stored files were deleted.

## Quotes and invoices

- **Issued** means the current quote is available for review.
- **Superseded** means a newer quote replaced it.
- **Expired** means its validity period ended; request a current quote.
- **Accepted** records agreement to the price and scope and opens sample-list entry. Finalizing the exact compliant roster authorizes laboratory work and creates the shared shipment.

The Job's **Quote and billing** section shows issued invoices, due dates, balances, and **Download invoice PDF**. Contact Phaeno about billing or a recorded payment; the Portal does not collect card payments. An unpaid invoice does not hold back scientifically released PSeq results. See [Results and billing](./results-and-billing).

## Recover a missing record or failed action

1. Confirm the organization and selected Department in the header.
2. On **Lab services**, clear search, status, date, or **Submitted by me** filters before assuming a Job is missing. A loading failure has its own retry action and differs from no matching Jobs.
3. Open the Job and inspect the affected sample and **Files and results**. Results may still be processing, under scientific review, awaiting publication, withdrawn, or outside their retention window.
4. For sample-list errors, correct the named row or source-group count. After changing a CSV, preview it again before replacing the draft list. A failed finalization does not authorize shipment; correct the problem and retry from the same Job.
5. If the Job changed during an edit, review the refreshed record and preserved entries. A pricing submission clears its privacy confirmation so you can review and confirm the updated Job before resubmitting.

For a failed download, retry once while the package remains available. If it fails again, contact Phaeno with the Job number, coded sample reference, file or package version, visible status, and time. Do not send credentials, patient identifiers, or scientific file contents in support messages.
