# Getting started

Portal keeps your organization’s laboratory requests, sample progress, released results, commercial documents, curated data, and user access in one place.

## Find your next action

The dashboard shows **My work**, actions needing attention, work waiting on your
administrator or Phaeno, and available results/updates. Select a work item to open
its current record. Only actions allowed in your selected organization and
Department appear as your tasks; administrator decisions are shown as waiting
context when you cannot perform them.

The header bell is available from every workspace. Its badge counts unfinished
actions, and its preview shows the first five priority items. **View all work**
opens the full list with area, priority and paging controls. Reading a notification
does not complete work or record a successful download. Counts refresh after
saved actions and periodically while the page is visible. Unavailable counts are
shown as unknown, with retry guidance.

For each Job's current phase, shipping attention follows physical facts.
Sample preparation requires received, assembled and usable kits. A kit allocation
or request marked received does not substitute for physical stock evidence.
Review kit supply when stock is unusable; outstanding dispatch and cancellation
decisions appear as waiting. Usable stock can allow preparation while Phaeno
completes an outstanding partial request. Members see administrator steps as
waiting rather than actions they can perform.

## Navigate the help center

Open the user menu in the toolbar and choose **Documentation**. This entry is available on both wide and narrow screens. On smaller screens, the Documentation tab sits below the toolbar and organization details.

On narrow screens, the user menu opens a full-width tray from the right. Choose **Close** or press Escape to dismiss it and return to its toolbar button. Your workspace links appear directly in the tray. When configuration pages are available to your role, choose **Settings** to expand them. The section starts collapsed each time you open the menu; use Enter or Space to toggle it, Up and Down to move among visible links, and Escape to close the user menu.

Guide navigation opens beneath the toolbar from the far-left edge of the screen. Its header identifies Documentation and, on wider screens, provides the pin or unpin icon. Pointer users can preview an unpinned sidebar by approaching the left edge; keyboard and touch users open it from the tab. Choosing a guide closes an unpinned sidebar and displays the article. On narrow screens, click or tap outside the sidebar to close it; its tab and Escape also close it.

## Confirm the current organization

The header shows the organization active in your current session. That organization controls which records and actions you can see. Customer information never carries across organizations.

If your account has more than one active organization membership, open the user menu and select one of your own organizations from the list beside the organization icon. The same select list is available in the narrow-screen menu tray. Switching returns to Dashboard and selects an available Department in that organization; its permissions, records and guides apply. Save your work first, or cancel an unsaved-page warning to remain in the current organization. If an expected organization is missing, ask its administrator to review your access.

## Know your access level

Customer organization administrators and department administrators can create and submit laboratory requests, accept quotes, and request cancellations within their operational Department. Organization administrators manage organization-wide invitations and roles. Other active members can review their organization’s jobs, follow progress, and download results that have been released.

If an expected action is missing, first confirm the organization shown in the header. Your organization administrator can then check your membership.

## Find your work

The dashboard separates **Needs attention**, **Waiting on others**, and
**Available results and updates** in your selected organization and Department.
The header bell counts unfinished actions that you can perform. Opening an item
does not complete it; perform the next step in its linked record.

Select a category to show its work list. **Work area** and **Work priority**
narrow that list; the counts at the top retain your overall current scope.
**View all work** opens the complete paged list. If a saved page is beyond the
last page, use **First page** or choose an earlier page; an empty page does not
mean there are no outstanding actions.

Available updates include released results and assigned curated data. A released
package leaves its new-result count after every file has a completed download;
partial or failed downloads do not complete a file. Counts stay unknown when a
read fails. **Retry work** refreshes both the summary and current list, including
when a filtered list is available but the summary failed.

- **Dashboard** shows the next supported actions, who is responsible, and links to the owning records. Work awaiting your administrator or Phaeno appears separately from your own actions. The list refreshes while open and when you return.
- **Lab services** contains each job from request and pricing through sample preparation, shipping, laboratory progress, and results.
- **Data Library**, available through navigation, contains Phaeno-curated packages assigned to your organization or Department. Each lab Job owns its **Files and results** section.
- For organization administrators, **Customer settings → People and access** manages durable invitations, Member or Organization administrator roles, and membership deactivation.
- **Documentation** in the user menu opens these guides.

## Keep submissions safe

Forms with unsaved changes ask you to keep editing or discard the draft when you
close them, navigate away, or switch organizations or Departments. A save in
progress must finish before you leave. Once a form closes after a successful
save, it does not block your next navigation.

The initial laboratory request workflow is not a protected-health-information intake workflow. Do not enter patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, or other direct identifiers in request fields, notes, file names, or uploads.

Use your organization’s own reference values and follow the instructions attached to the selected analysis.

## Get help

When contacting Phaeno, provide the portal request or sample reference and a description of the step you were performing. Do not send credentials, authentication tokens, or sensitive sample content in a support message.

## Earlier Trial Projects

If your organization converted from Prospect, **Trial Projects** retains the approved Trial history and available results in their original department. Conversion preserves existing download deadlines. See [Trial history](./trial-history).

## Search and browse guides

Use **Search documentation** at the top of Documentation to find a guide or a matching section. Results update after a short pause; use at least two characters. Try “sample shipping” or “results and billing”.

Choose **Filters** to narrow results by Topic, Workflow, or Guide type. **Clear filters** removes those selections. **Browse by topic** opens guides for a topic without requiring search words. Workflow links and **Related guides** at the end of an article provide other ways to find relevant instructions.

Select a result title to open its matching section. Your browser’s Back action restores the search and filters. Search uses the guides for your current organization; it does not include the public Website or operational records. A guide’s instructions do not change your account permissions.

If no guides match, try fewer words or clear the filters. If search is temporarily unavailable, use **Try again** or **Browse all guides**. A documentation update may require **Refresh page**; guide browsing remains available while the update finishes.
