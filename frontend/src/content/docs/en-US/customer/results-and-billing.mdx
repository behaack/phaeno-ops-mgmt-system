# Results and billing

Scientific readiness and Customer release are separate. The portal shows whether each sample is still being produced, scientifically approved, withdrawn, or available to download.

## Follow sample progress

Open a laboratory job to see each sample’s current state. Receipt, accessioning, laboratory work, data processing, result readiness, and completion are recorded independently so one delayed sample does not hide progress on the others.

## Understand the release rule

A PSeq result may be released after its final output package is complete, checksummed, malware-clean, and scientifically approved. An authorized Phaeno Result Release Manager publishes the selected sample; other samples can remain in progress. Payment balance, payment terms, tax state, and Customer credit do not gate release.

## Download released results

1. Open the laboratory job and select the sample.
2. Confirm that the result package is marked available rather than still under review or withdrawn.
3. Review the release details and download the available files.

Each release is immutable and linked to its scientific approval, package version, checksums, and quality facts. A corrected result is delivered as a new version; it does not replace the audit history of an earlier release. Download and retention events are audited. If a file passes its retention cutoff, contact Phaeno for an authorized reissue.

Downloads require an active membership in the organization that owns the job and are audited.

## Use POMS invoices

At job completion, POMS issues one numbered invoice from the accepted quote. The invoice freezes its billing, tax, currency, and payment terms; its due date is the completion date plus those terms. The immutable invoice PDF is available to your organization. POMS does not collect payment-card information or provide online payment processing in this release.

Receipts may be partially or fully applied to the invoice. Overpayments remain visible as unapplied cash until Finance explicitly allocates or resolves them. Corrections appear as retained credits, debits, or write-offs; an issued invoice is never silently rewritten.
