# Results and billing

Scientific readiness and commercial release are separate. The portal always shows whether a result is still being produced, ready but commercially held, or available to download.

## Follow sample progress

Open a laboratory job to see each sample’s current state. Receipt, accessioning, laboratory work, data processing, result readiness, and completion are recorded independently so one delayed sample does not hide progress on the others.

## Understand the release rule

- **Approved Customer credit:** Net 30 terms apply. Phaeno may release a ready sample’s files after the required commercial source record is created, even while other samples remain in progress.
- **No approved Customer credit:** the portal may show that results are ready, but files remain on payment hold. The current portal does not provide payment confirmation or an operator override; contact Phaeno before relying on a delivery date.

Credit status is maintained by authorized Phaeno users and is not changed from the Customer workspace.

## Download released results

1. Open the laboratory job and select the sample.
2. Confirm that the result is marked available rather than payment-required or still under commercial review.
3. Review the release details and download the available files.

Use **Download package** to retrieve every file in one ZIP, or download an individual file. The portal records a download only after the complete response finishes. A transfer that is partial, cancelled, failed, or timed out remains **Not downloaded** and does not remove the release's conditional grace protection. While a transfer is active, the page shows **Download in progress** and refreshes the authoritative organization-level state after completion. One completed download by any authorized member satisfies that file for the organization.

Each release is immutable and linked to its scientific and quality facts. A corrected result is delivered as a new release; it does not replace the audit history of an earlier release.

For a newly released result, the job's **Data & results** tab shows the retention schedule frozen at release. **Standard deletion** is the normal deletion time. **Conditional grace through** is used only when at least one file in that release remains undownloaded at the standard deadline; in that case the whole release receives the stated grace period. The displayed date and time include your browser's local time-zone label. Earlier releases created before retention snapshots were introduced do not show a schedule; contact Phaeno before relying on a deletion date for one of those releases.

Downloads require an active membership in the organization that owns the job and are audited. The Customer view shows whether the organization completed each file, but it does not expose which member downloaded it.

## Understand billing records

POMS keeps the immutable quote and the commercial source record associated with completed work. Phaeno Finance uses a manual accounting report outside the Customer workspace to prepare journal entries and invoices. Payment-card information is not collected by the portal.

QuickBooks Online integration is currently deferred. The Customer workspace does not display a hosted payment page or accept payment. If a ready result remains on payment hold, contact Phaeno and provide the job number.
