# Results and billing

Scientific readiness and Commercial release are separate. PSeq result release
is never gated by invoice balance, payment, or credit.

## Follow sample progress

Open a laboratory job to see each sample’s current state. Receipt, accessioning, laboratory work, data processing, result readiness, and completion are recorded independently so one delayed sample does not hide progress on the others.

## Understand the PSeq release rule

- A provider-registered final-output package is checked for complete SHA-256
  checksums and clean malware scans.
- A Scientific Reviewer pins and approves that exact package and version.
- A Result Release Manager decides when the eligible package becomes visible to
  the Customer. This may happen per sample while other samples remain in work.
- A correction is a new package and release version. Withdrawal preserves the
  prior package and audit history.

An invoice may be open, partially paid, paid, adjusted, or overdue without
changing scientific approval or result-release eligibility.

## Review governed result status

Open **Order-to-cash** to review the immutable package version, pipeline version,
sample scope, artifact count, and current approval/release state. The current
additive slice does not expose governed result bytes or send a result-available
email. Phaeno must use an approved delivery path until those sensitive-data
controls are separately activated.

Retention warning, cutoff, and grace events are recorded as evidence. The
current lifecycle raises owned attention when deletion becomes due; it does not
delete result bytes automatically.

## Understand PSeq invoices

POMS freezes the approved billing contact/address, payment terms, tax decision,
and rate or exemption evidence into the issued PSeq quote. Job completion issues
a numbered USD invoice and immutable PDF from the accepted quote. Open
**Order-to-cash** to review the invoice status, total, balance, issue/due dates,
and download the PDF.

Phaeno Finance records receipts and allocations in POMS. QuickBooks Online,
online ACH/card collection, and a hosted payment page are deferred. Never send
payment-card or bank-login credentials through Portal notes or support messages.
