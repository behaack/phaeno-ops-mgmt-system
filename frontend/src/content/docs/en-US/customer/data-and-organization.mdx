# Data Library and organization access

The Data Library provides a consistent place to review released lab-job data and Phaeno-curated data packages. The two sources remain distinct and retain their own release and access rules.

## Open data for a lab job

From a job under **Lab services**, open **Data & results**, then select **Open in Data Library**. The Data Library opens in that job’s context and shows only released result packages and files for the job. If nothing has been released, return to the job to review its processing, scientific-readiness, payment, or release status.

Select **View organization Data Library** to leave the job context and return to the curated packages assigned to your organization.

## Use the Data Library

The organization Data Library shows active curated-package grants shared across your organization or assigned to your selected department. These packages are separate from laboratory job inputs and results. Each grant points to an exact, immutable package version. A later package publication does not silently change the version your organization received.

You can review the package summary, scientific context, quality information, file manifest, and checksums. Active organization members can download individual files or a complete archive. The archive includes the manifest and every file in that version.

Curated packages are read-only. Customer users cannot edit, republish, or grant them to another organization.

## Understand access changes

Phaeno may grant, upgrade, revoke, quarantine, or retire a package. Revocation or quarantine blocks new viewing and downloads but cannot recall copies already downloaded. If a prior download needs action, the portal gives organization administrators Customer-safe guidance.

Organization and department administrators can review curated download history for the selected department. This includes downloads from packages shared across the organization. Organization administrators can switch departments to review each department's activity. Department administrators see only activity within their assigned departments.

Older download records without a recorded department remain available only to organization administrators, for organization-wide grants or grants assigned to the selected department. Changing a person's department assignments does not move or erase their earlier download records.

## Manage organization members

The authorization model permits Customer organization administrators to invite members by email, choose member or administrator access, change eligible roles, and deactivate other users’ memberships only within their organization. An administrator cannot deactivate their own membership from User management. Invitations must be accepted by the invited email account.

The **User management** screen saves invitations, eligible role changes, and membership deactivation to the Portal account system. Deactivation ends portal access without deleting the person or audit history, and restoring an inactive membership requires a fresh invitation. Phaeno administrators handle global account disablement and organization status.

Every user should have their own account. Do not share sign-in credentials or use a shared mailbox as a substitute for an accountable user.

Safety and withdrawal instructions about previously supplied data continue to go to current organization administrators during an organization suspension. These notices do not restore Portal access. Contact Phaeno if you cannot record a required response in the portal.
