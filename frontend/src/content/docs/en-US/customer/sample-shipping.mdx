# Prepare and ship samples

For a normal Lab Service Job, sample entry begins only after the price is accepted and the compliant sample list is finalized. Promotional orders follow their approved allowance. Open the owning Job under **Lab services**, then open **Samples & shipping** to see the return kit, expected tube slots, destination, and packet status.

## Keep your records connected to the tubes

Each Phaeno-supplied tube has a permanent manufacturer barcode. Your organization administrator scans or enters that barcode beside the correct Customer sample and tube slot before anything is shipped. A sample may own more than one slot, shown as **Tube 1 of N**. Use a stable, non-PHI identifier that your organization can connect to its own sample record. Do not enter a patient name, medical-record number, date of birth, or other direct identifier.

Phaeno preserves the tube-to-sample crosswalk in the Portal and in the shipment packet. Download the CSV or print the packet for your records. Your organization remains responsible for knowing what its Customer sample identifier means; Phaeno is responsible for retaining the identifier-to-tube association used for shipping and intake.

If the wrong tube was assigned, an organization administrator can scan the replacement tube and record why the assignment changed. The earlier association remains in history. If a packet was already confirmed, saving the correction before shipment voids that packet and issues a corrected revision; destroy any unused copy of the old packet and print the replacement. Do not cover, alter, or remove the manufacturer barcode.

## Confirm and ship

Assign exactly one registered tube from the displayed return kit to every expected tube slot. Confirm the crosswalk only after checking every row. POMS then freezes the destination, instructions, manifest, packet barcode, and tube-to-sample crosswalk for that packet revision.

Print the full packet. Follow the preparation and packing instructions, place the manifest inside the package, and use the ship-to page with the carrier label. The Phaeno packet barcode does not buy postage and is not a tube barcode.

After handing the package to the carrier, record the carrier, tracking number, and shipment time on **Samples & shipping** in the job. If a tube cannot be matched confidently or its barcode will not scan, stop and contact Phaeno instead of guessing.
