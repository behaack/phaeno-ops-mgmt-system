# Prepare and ship samples

For a normal Lab Service Job, sample entry begins after the price is accepted. Shipping and laboratory authorization begin after the exact sample list is finalized. Open the owning Job under **Lab services**, then use **Samples and shipping** within **Ordering and shipping**. Container preparation, tube matching, shipping inserts and dispatch stay on this Job. An organization administrator or an administrator of the selected Department performs these actions. Members can review their Department's shipments and use permitted print and download actions.

The six horizontal icons above the work area are **Confirm order**, **Samples**, **Kits**, **Containers**, **Match tubes**, and **Send**. Checkmarks show completed work, the current step is highlighted, and future steps are muted. Hover over an icon, focus it with the keyboard, or tap it to read its detailed explanation and status, including who must act when work remains. Press Escape, move keyboard focus away or tap outside to close the information. Icons provide information without opening work, including completed and future steps. **Your next step** below them explains the required task and who must act; use its action button for the required work. **After you send** separately tracks dispatched containers, receipt, laboratory progress and results. Partial sends can appear there while preparation continues for other containers.

**Send** includes shipping insert review and confirmation, printing the current revision, packing it inside the correct container, handing the package to the carrier and recording dispatch. It completes only when every required active shipment is recorded as sent. Confirming or printing an insert alone does not complete Send.

## Order and receive transportation kits

Transportation kits belong to your Customer/Department delivery location. Received, unused containers can be used for an eligible Job at that location, including containers originally requested while preparing a different Job. The originating Job remains part of the delivery history; cancelling it does not consume unused containers.

Use **Prepare containers** from the Job's **Actions**, or use the preparation area for tubes awaiting containers, and choose the location you will ship from. Review available containers and kits still on the way. If more are needed, use **Order transportation kits**, review the recommended sizes and delivery address, and select **Confirm kit order**. You can adjust compatible sizes before confirming. Transportation kits and outbound delivery are included with the accepted laboratory order at no additional charge. Existing compatible received stock can satisfy the need without another order.

Use **Add a delivery location** or **Manage delivery locations** when an address needs setup or correction. Delivery locations belong to your organization and Department. Administrators can maintain them from the Department workspace. The confirmed order retains the address shown at confirmation even if the saved location changes later. From these location pages, **Return to Lab Job** restores the Job and selected container without automatically placing another kit order.

**Kits ordered** means Phaeno has received the order for fulfillment. Reopening the page or repeating the same confirmation does not place a second order. An order can be cancelled before any of its kits are dispatched. Once dispatched, the kit number, carrier and tracking appear as **On the way**. This is provisional inventory and is not yet available for preparing samples.

When the kits arrive, select **Confirm kits received** and acknowledge only those you have actually received. Each acknowledged container becomes **Available** at its delivery location. If delivery is partial, prepare only with the received containers while the remaining kits stay outstanding. Once receipt is complete, the delivery area shows **Kits received** without asking you to confirm arrival again. An organization or Department administrator records receipt; ordinary members can review the status.

Select **View shipping and receiving location** to open the selected location and review its **Transportation kits** inventory, tracking and receipt actions. Use this location view when the Job that originally requested the kits has been cancelled. Inventory distinguishes containers **On the way**, **Available**, **Assigned** to a Job, and **In use**. Refresh to see changes recorded by another user.

Preparation requires registered, received containers at the selected location. A container at another Customer, Department or location, an unreceived container, or one already assigned or in use cannot be claimed. Contact Phaeno if physical stock and its recorded location or receipt do not agree; entering a quantity does not register stock or transfer it between locations.

## Configure the supplied containers

**Choose shipping containers** recommends compatible available stock at the selected location, using the fewest containers and then the least spare capacity. Each size shows its common name, SKU number and usable tube capacity. A catalog size alone does not establish that a physical container is available.

Open **Adjust containers** and scan the permanent barcode on each physical container you intend to use. Review its size, SKU, capacity and tube allocation. Each container occupies one row, with its barcode shown beside its allocation. Barcode entry and preview do not reserve stock until you confirm. A row's **Remove** action removes that selection from the draft.

Use compatible sizes you actually have available. For example, 30 tubes can use a 20 and a 10, or six 5s. Smart choices avoid redundant containers; spare slots are allowed when needed for the available sizes. Three remaining tubes should use an available 5 rather than adding an available 10 or 20. The Portal checks recorded stock automatically, without manual availability quantities.

The preview shows tubes, containers, usable capacity, spare slots and unallocated tubes. **Confirm containers** reserves the exact scanned containers and creates a shipment for each nonempty container. If another user has claimed a selected container, the save fails with an explanation and preserves your draft for correction. Partial preparation leaves the remaining tubes awaiting containers. Spare slots are empty capacity, not missing samples.

A sample's tubes may be split among shipments. Container choices do not change accepted sample counts or pricing. Assigned tubes and confirmed shipping inserts are not silently rearranged if configuration changes. Contact Phaeno when existing assignments or a dispatched shipment need reconciliation.

Adjust each row's tube count as needed. Counts must be whole numbers, fit each container and together equal the tubes allocated in the preview. The **Summary** stays visible while updating; confirmation becomes available when the current allocation has been checked.

## Scan and retain tube identities

Use **Shipping container** in the Job's **Samples and shipping** area to choose a container or remaining tubes awaiting containers. With several choices, select deliberately before using a shipment command; a single available choice is selected directly. The selected shipment reference remains visible. Switching container or returning to **Samples** asks before discarding an unsaved scan and is unavailable while saving. Open **Container, kit and receipt details** for supporting information without narrowing the scan area.

Before scanning starts, an organization or Department administrator can use **Reset container configuration** in **Container, kit and receipt details**. Review the confirmation: it returns **all containers and tubes for the Job** to container selection, not just the shipment you opened. Your finalized sample list stays intact, and the previous configurations remain in the records and audit history for support. Retired configurations stay out of normal container choices; an older direct link identifies them as history. Cancelling the confirmation changes nothing.

**Reset container configuration** is also unavailable while a barcode entry is unsaved or saving. Discard an unsaved entry before revising the plan; saving the first scan locks the plan as described below.

Before any tube is scanned, a successful reset releases the selected containers back to available inventory at their locations and returns the Job's tubes to preparation. Previous configurations remain in history. The action is locked once any container for the Job has a saved tube scan, bound tube roster, shipping insert, sample-return dispatch or sample receipt. Clearing a scan does not restore it. If another user starts work while your confirmation is open, refresh and review the current state. The finalized sample list, quote, original kit deliveries and their receipts remain intact.

Use a stable **Customer sample ID** that your organization can connect to its own sample record. Do not enter patient names, medical-record numbers, dates of birth or other direct identifiers.

Use **Match tubes** in **Samples and shipping** or the Job's **Actions** to match registered tubes in the selected container. One scan field opens above the same grouped sample list. For the displayed sample and tube number, scan its permanent barcode and press Enter or **Save scan**. Each match is saved separately before the next sample and tube are revealed. A successful save updates the barcode and progress and returns focus to the scan field. Failed, unknown, ineligible, duplicate and already-used scans retain the entered barcode and current target for review and retry. The first saved match locks container reset.

Expand a sample to review its individual tubes, saved barcodes and container identities. The list keeps 10 samples per page; there is no separate Scan tubes view. While matching, saving reveals the next unmatched tube's sample and page. Reviewing another page or expanding a different sample does not change the active scan target or entered barcode. **Return to active tube** restores its sample and scan field. The scan progress applies to the selected container; each sample's matched count covers all its active containers. **Done scanning** asks before discarding an unfinished entry, and leaving or changing containers preserves the existing unsaved-scan warning. Paging and dismissal are unavailable while saving. After the last match, the field closes and the same list remains open for review.

Use registered tubes belonging to the physical container assigned during preparation. The first successful tube scan changes that container from assigned to in use and locks reset; it does not choose a different container implicitly. A tube from another container, an unknown or duplicate barcode, or an already-used tube cannot advance the step. A failed scan keeps the entered value and current sample available for correction and retry. Do not cover, alter or remove permanent container or tube barcodes.

If the wrong tube was assigned, use **Change tube** or **Correct tube** and record why the assignment changed. The earlier association remains in history. A correction after shipping insert confirmation, but before dispatch, voids that shipping insert and issues a corrected revision. Destroy unused copies of the old shipping insert and print the replacement.

## Confirm each shipment and print its shipping insert

Use the Job's **Actions** menu for **Selected shipment** commands, including **Review and confirm shipping insert**, **Print shipping insert**, **Download tube list (CSV)** and **Record shipment** when permitted. The shipment reference identifies the container affected; **This order** holds applicable order commands. If only one command is available, it appears directly. Your permissions and the complete shipment's stage determine availability. Completing one visible tube page does not complete the container. Authorized readers retain print and download access. Use **Print shipping insert** again for another copy of the current revision.

Within **Send**, review every physical tube in the container, then select **Review and confirm shipping insert**. Confirmation shows separate sample and tube counts and freezes the destination, instructions, manifest and crosswalk for that revision. A container can be partly filled when all its declared tubes are matched; spare capacity does not require extra tubes.

Print one receiving sheet for each physical container and place it inside that container. It shows the Customer, Job/Trial and shipment references, sample and tube totals, essential handling notes and two widely separated scan targets: the large **Scan to receive this shipment** PH-P- insert barcode and, when assigned, the physical container barcode. Follow the full preparation and packing instructions in the Portal before shipping. **Full packing instructions and sample / tube list** on the insert page retains the confirmed manifest, every sample and tube barcode, and split-sample references; these details do not print with the receiving sheet. Use **Download tube list (CSV)** to retain the complete crosswalk. Laboratory staff acknowledge container arrival in Receive shipments; the tubes are then accessioned individually. Customer barcode lookup alone does not record laboratory receipt.

When **Send** is current and the selected shipment's insert is ready, **Your next step** offers **Print shipping insert** directly. It checks the current revision, then opens the browser print dialog while keeping the Job page, selected container and saved tube matches intact. After the print dialog returns, explicitly confirm that the current insert was successfully printed and placed inside its correct container only when you have done both. Opening, closing or cancelling printing never makes that confirmation for you. If you cancelled printing, dismiss the follow-up and print again when ready. Print or revision-check errors also leave the work unacknowledged.

After your explicit printing-and-packing confirmation, **Your next step** changes to **Record shipment**. Your confirmation is remembered in this browser tab for this shipment's current insert. A corrected insert must be printed and confirmed again. You must still record the shipment after carrier handoff. **Send** stays unfinished until every required dispatch is recorded.

With several shipments, deliberately select the current container before printing or recording it; each has its own insert and confirmation. A sole available container remains selected directly. Reprint the current insert through **Actions** for another copy; this does not issue a new revision. If the insert cannot be checked, review the message on the Job, reconnect if needed and select **Try again**. If insert confirmation fails, the message appears in the open dialog and your entries remain available. Retain the shipping insert or use **Download tube list (CSV)** so your organization can connect its own identifiers to the permanent tubes. The shipping insert does not buy postage or replace the carrier label.

## Record dispatch and follow receipt

After handing each package to the carrier, use **Record shipment** for its carrier, tracking number and shipment time. Repeat for each physical shipment. **After you send** shows recorded dispatches, tracking and actual receipt; **Ordering and shipping** remains active for containers still being prepared. All required active shipments must have recorded dispatch before the customer shipping steps are complete. A later clarification or replacement request can still require your attention.

For each finalized sample, use the **Matched** and **Receipt** counts in **Samples**. Saved matches cover that sample's active shipments, while receipt is the actual recorded tube count. **Checking…** or **Not available** must not be read as zero. Per-container and Job-wide totals remain explicitly scoped in the scan and receipt details. A sample split across containers remains partly received until all its expected tubes have been recorded; receiving the first package does not mark the entire sample received.

Use **Samples** to return to the grouped Job overview or **Shipping container** to choose another container without leaving the Job. Existing standalone shipment bookmarks remain available, and Trial shipping keeps its existing entry point. If a tube cannot be matched confidently, a printed barcode cannot be read, or there is no compatible container, contact Phaeno with the Job and shipment references instead of guessing.

After a shipment is sent, select its **Shipping container** in **Samples and shipping**, then choose **Reprint shipping insert**. When several containers are available and none is selected, **Actions → Select container to reprint insert** takes you to the container selector. Reprinting opens the browser print dialog for the current insert without issuing a new revision or recording another shipment or receipt.


Portal-generated identifiers now render as QR codes, with the exact identifier
printed underneath. Shipping inserts, container labels, kit records and displayed
sample/tube identifiers use the same format. Use a QR-capable 2D scanner. Existing
manufacturer labels and older printed barcodes remain valid; do not relabel a
tube or change its saved identity merely to match the new display. A scan still
requires the correct workflow and does not by itself confirm receipt or accession.
