# Prepare and ship samples

For a normal Lab Service Job, sample entry begins after the price is accepted. Shipping and laboratory authorization begin after the exact sample list is finalized. Open the owning Job under **Lab services**, then use **Samples and shipping** → **Shipping containers**. An organization administrator or an administrator of the selected Department prepares containers, matches tubes, confirms packets and records dispatch. Members can review the shipments available to their Department.

## Order and receive transportation kits

Transportation kits belong to your Customer/Department delivery location. Received, unused containers can be used for an eligible Job at that location, including containers originally requested while preparing a different Job. The originating Job remains part of the delivery history; cancelling it does not consume unused containers.

Open **Choose containers** for tubes awaiting containers and choose the location you will ship from. Review available containers and kits still on the way. If more are needed, use **Order transportation kits**, review the recommended sizes and delivery address, and select **Confirm kit order**. You can adjust compatible sizes before confirming. Transportation kits and outbound delivery are included with the accepted laboratory order at no additional charge.

Use **Add a delivery location** or **Manage delivery locations** when an address needs setup or correction. Delivery locations belong to your organization and Department. Administrators can maintain them from the Department workspace. The confirmed order retains the address shown at confirmation even if the saved location changes later.

**Kits ordered** means Phaeno has received the order for fulfillment. Reopening the page or repeating the same confirmation does not place a second order. An order can be cancelled before any of its kits are dispatched. Once dispatched, the kit number, carrier and tracking appear as **On the way**. This is provisional inventory and is not yet available for preparing samples.

When the kits arrive, select **Confirm kits received** and acknowledge only those you have actually received. Each acknowledged container becomes **Available** at its delivery location. If delivery is partial, prepare only with the received containers while the remaining kits stay outstanding. Once receipt is complete, the delivery area shows **Kits received** without asking you to confirm arrival again. An organization or Department administrator records receipt; ordinary members can review the status.

The delivery-location detail also provides **Transportation kits** inventory, tracking and receipt actions. Use this location view when the Job that originally requested the kits has been cancelled. Inventory distinguishes containers **On the way**, **Available**, **Assigned** to a Job, and **In use**. Refresh to see changes recorded by another user.

Preparation requires registered, received containers at the selected location. A container at another Customer, Department or location, an unreceived container, or one already assigned or in use cannot be claimed. Contact Phaeno if physical stock and its recorded location or receipt do not agree; entering a quantity does not register stock or transfer it between locations.

## Configure the supplied containers

**Choose shipping containers** recommends compatible available stock at the selected location, using the fewest containers and then the least spare capacity. Each size shows its common name, SKU number and usable tube capacity. A catalog size alone does not establish that a physical container is available.

Open **Adjust containers** and scan the permanent barcode on each physical container you intend to use. Review its size, SKU, capacity and tube allocation. Each container occupies one row, with its barcode shown beside its allocation. Barcode entry and preview do not reserve stock until you confirm. A row's **Remove** action removes that selection from the draft.

Use compatible sizes you actually have available. For example, 30 tubes can use a 20 and a 10, or six 5s. Smart choices avoid redundant containers; spare slots are allowed when needed for the available sizes. Three remaining tubes should use an available 5 rather than adding an available 10 or 20. The Portal checks recorded stock automatically, without manual availability quantities.

The preview shows tubes, containers, usable capacity, spare slots and unallocated tubes. **Confirm containers** reserves the exact scanned containers and creates a shipment for each nonempty container. If another user has claimed a selected container, the save fails with an explanation and preserves your draft for correction. Partial preparation leaves the remaining tubes awaiting containers. Spare slots are empty capacity, not missing samples.

A sample's tubes may be split among shipments. Container choices do not change accepted sample counts or pricing. Assigned tubes and confirmed packets are not silently rearranged if configuration changes. Contact Phaeno when existing assignments or a dispatched shipment need reconciliation.

Adjust each row's tube count as needed. Counts must be whole numbers, fit each container and together equal the tubes allocated in the preview. The **Summary** stays visible while updating; confirmation becomes available when the current allocation has been checked.

## Scan and retain tube identities

Use **Shipping container** at the top of the shipment page to switch between this Job's containers or return to remaining tubes awaiting containers. Each option shows the container identity, tube count and status. If a scan is unsaved, switching asks whether to discard it; switching is blocked while the scan saves.

Before scanning starts, an organization or Department administrator can use **Reset container configuration** beside the **Shipping container** selector. Review the confirmation: it returns **all containers and tubes for the Job** to container selection, not just the shipment you opened. Your finalized sample list stays intact, and the previous containers remain in history. Cancelling the confirmation changes nothing.

**Reset container configuration** is also unavailable while a barcode entry is unsaved or saving. Discard an unsaved entry before revising the plan; saving the first scan locks the plan as described below.

Before any tube is scanned, a successful reset releases the selected containers back to available inventory at their locations and returns the Job's tubes to preparation. Previous configurations remain in history. The action is locked once any container for the Job has a saved tube scan, bound tube roster, packet, sample-return dispatch or sample receipt. Clearing a scan does not restore it. If another user starts work while your confirmation is open, refresh and review the current state. The finalized sample list, quote, original kit deliveries and their receipts remain intact.

Use a stable **Customer sample ID** that your organization can connect to its own sample record. Do not enter patient names, medical-record numbers, dates of birth or other direct identifiers.

Open a prepared shipment and work through **Scan tubes into this shipment**. For the displayed sample and tube number, scan its permanent manufacturer barcode and press Enter or **Save scan**. A successful match displays the exact saved number and barcode graphic, updates progress, and moves focus to the next tube. Each match is saved separately, so returning later resumes at the next unmatched tube. Long lists have **Previous tubes** and **Next tubes** controls.

Use registered tubes belonging to the physical container assigned during preparation. The first successful tube scan changes that container from assigned to in use and locks reset; it does not choose a different container implicitly. A tube from another container, an unknown or duplicate barcode, or an already-used tube cannot advance the step. A failed scan keeps the entered value and current sample available for correction and retry. Do not cover, alter or remove permanent container or tube barcodes.

If the wrong tube was assigned, use **Change tube** or **Correct tube** and record why the assignment changed. The earlier association remains in history. A correction after packet confirmation, but before dispatch, voids that packet and issues a corrected revision. Destroy unused copies of the old packet and print the replacement.

## Confirm each shipment and print its packet

Review every physical tube in the container, then select **Review and confirm packet**. Confirmation shows separate sample and tube counts and freezes the destination, instructions, manifest and crosswalk for that revision. A container can be partly filled when all its declared tubes are matched; spare capacity does not require extra tubes.

Print the full packet and follow its preparation and packing instructions. Put that container's manifest inside the package. It includes the frozen container name, SKU, capacity and physical container barcode, distinct order, shipment and sample barcodes, and every permanent tube barcode for its contents. A split sample shows how many of its total tubes are inside, with other shipment references or unallocated tubes listed separately. Those references reflect the confirmed revision; the Portal shows current progress. Scanning a printed identifier does not prove physical receipt.

**View packet** checks the current revision before offering **Print packet**. Reconnect or use **Try again** if it cannot be checked. If confirmation fails, the message appears in the open dialog and your entries remain available. Retain the packet or **Crosswalk CSV** so your organization can connect its own identifiers to the permanent tubes. The packet does not buy postage or replace the carrier label.

## Record dispatch and follow receipt

After handing each package to the carrier, record its carrier, tracking number and shipment time. Repeat for each physical shipment. Receipt progress shows tubes received per shipment, across the Job and for each sample. A sample split across containers remains partly received until all its expected tubes have been recorded; receiving the first package does not mark the entire sample received.

Use **Back to lab job** for the owning Job or **Shipping container** to move between its containers. If a tube cannot be matched confidently, a printed barcode cannot be read, or there is no compatible container, contact Phaeno with the Job and shipment references instead of guessing.
