# Prepare and ship samples

Each PSeq Job has one selected Sample type. Put another type in a separate order with its own containers, shipping packets, and carrier tracking labels. Sample entry begins after the price is accepted. Shipping and laboratory authorization begin after the exact sample list is finalized. Open the owning Job under **Lab services**, then use **Samples and shipping** within **Ordering and shipping**. Container preparation, tube matching, shipping inserts and dispatch stay on this Job. An organization administrator or an administrator of the selected Department performs these actions. Members can review their Department's shipments and use permitted print and download actions.

Phaeno selects the receiving destination for your samples. A Job starts with Phaeno's default destination, so a kit already at your location can be used without waiting for a separate routing decision. If Phaeno sends a new kit, its fulfillment team may update the destination before the first kit for that Job is dispatched and before sample packing begins. Review the destination in the Job's current instructions before sending samples; an issued shipping insert retains its saved address.

The six horizontal icons above the work area are **Confirm order**, **Samples**, **Kits**, **Containers**, **Match tubes**, and **Send**. Checkmarks show completed work, the current step is highlighted, and future steps are muted. Hover over an icon, focus it with the keyboard, or tap it to read its detailed explanation and status, including who must act when work remains. Press Escape, move keyboard focus away or tap outside to close the information. Icons provide information without opening work, including completed and future steps. **Your next step** below them explains the required task and who must act; use its action button for the required work. **After you send** separately tracks dispatched containers, receipt, laboratory progress and results. Partial sends can appear there while preparation continues for other containers.

**Send** includes shipping insert review and confirmation, printing the current revision, packing it inside the correct container, handing the package to the carrier and recording dispatch. It completes only when every required active shipment is recorded as sent. Confirming or printing an insert alone does not complete Send.

## Order and receive transportation kits

Transportation kits belong to your Customer/Department delivery location. Received, unused containers can be used for an eligible Job at that location, including containers originally requested while preparing a different Job. The originating Job remains part of the delivery history; cancelling it does not consume unused containers.

Use **Prepare containers** from the Job's **Actions**, or use the preparation area for tubes awaiting containers, and use **Container location** to choose where the received containers are stored. It appears beside container selection, with a plain location name when only one active location is available. Review available containers and kits still on the way. The recommendation uses the Sample type selected for this Job and shows its name; only usable Transportation kit designs linked to that type are offered. If more are needed, use **Order transportation kits** at the right of the **Transportation kits** heading, review the **Recommended kit configuration for this shipment** and **Kit receiving location**, and select **Confirm kit order**. You can adjust the number of compatible kit configurations before confirming. Each physical kit has its own container ID and an individually registered tube roster. Transportation kits and outbound delivery are included with the accepted laboratory order at no additional charge. Existing compatible received stock can satisfy the need without another order.

Use **Add a delivery location** or **Manage delivery locations** when an address needs setup or correction. Delivery locations belong to your organization and Department. Administrators can maintain them from the Department workspace. The confirmed order retains the address shown at confirmation even if the saved location changes later. When you open address setup from kit ordering, **Return to Lab Job** restores the Job and selected container and reopens the unsubmitted kit review. A default address is selected; otherwise choose an address explicitly. Returning from ordinary location inventory restores the Job without reopening kit review. No return action places an order.

**Kits ordered** means Phaeno has received the order for fulfillment. Reopening the page or repeating the same confirmation does not place a second order. Before any kits are dispatched, use **Cancel kit order** at the right of the **Transportation kits** heading to review the cancellation. Once dispatched, the kit number, carrier and tracking appear as **On the way**. This is provisional inventory and is not yet available for preparing samples.

When the kits arrive, select **Confirm kits received** at the right of the **Transportation kits** heading and acknowledge only those you have actually received. Each acknowledged container becomes **Available** at its delivery location. If delivery is partial, prepare only with the received containers while the remaining kits stay outstanding. Once receipt is complete, the delivery area shows **Kits received** without asking you to confirm arrival again. An organization or Department administrator records receipt; ordinary members can review the status.

Select **View shipping and receiving location** to open the selected location and review its **Transportation kits** inventory, tracking and receipt actions. Use this location view when the Job that originally requested the kits has been cancelled. Inventory distinguishes containers **On the way**, **Available**, **Assigned** to a Job, and **In use**. Refresh to see changes recorded by another user.

Preparation requires registered, received containers at the selected location. A container at another Customer, Department or location, an unreceived container, or one already assigned or in use cannot be claimed. Contact Phaeno if physical stock and its recorded location or receipt do not agree; entering a quantity does not register stock or transfer it between locations.

## Record the material in each physical tube

When matching a tube, enter **Material amount in this tube** and **Material unit** with its barcode. Enter the actual biological material being sent in that tube, such as 20 µL. Tube capacity, the number of tubes and a total for the sample are separate facts. Do not divide a sample total across tubes or assume they contain equal amounts. The entry must be positive, with up to six decimal places, and each new tube starts with empty amount fields.

The saved amount is a **customer declaration**. Phaeno retains who recorded it and when, includes it in the frozen shipment crosswalk and tube-list CSV, and uses it as the declared opening amount during accession. It is kept distinct from a laboratory measurement. Earlier shipments without a recorded declaration show **Unknown**.

For a matched tube with no amount, select **Record material amount**. Use **Edit tube** or **Correct tube** to change a barcode or amount before dispatch. Corrections retain history and require a reason when changing existing declared information or a confirmed shipping insert. A correction to a confirmed shipment issues a revised insert; print and pack the current revision. Every tube must have a declared amount and unit before a new shipping insert is confirmed or a shipment is recorded as sent.

## Configure the supplied containers

**Assign shipping containers** recommends compatible available stock at the selected location, using the fewest containers and then the least spare capacity. Each size shows its common name, SKU number and usable tube capacity. A catalog size alone does not establish that a physical container is available.

Open **Assign containers** at the right of the **Assign shipping containers** heading and scan the permanent barcode on each physical container you intend to use. Review its size, SKU, capacity and tube allocation. Each container has a numbered card (**Container 1**, **Container 2**, and so on), with **Container**, **Tubes**, and **Scan or enter container barcode** fields. The recommendation is labeled **Recommended containers — not yet assigned**; receiving a container makes it available but does not assign it to this Job. Barcode entry and preview do not reserve stock until you confirm. A row's **Remove** action removes that selection from the draft. It is hidden when the single selected container is the only compatible container available.

Use compatible sizes you actually have available. For example, 30 tubes can use a 20 and a 10, or six 5s. Smart choices avoid redundant containers; spare slots are allowed when needed for the available sizes. Three remaining tubes should use an available 5 rather than adding an available 10 or 20. The Portal checks recorded stock automatically, without manual availability quantities.

The preview shows tubes, containers, usable capacity, spare slots and unallocated tubes. **Confirm assignment** reserves the exact scanned containers and creates a shipment for each nonempty container. If another user has claimed a selected container, the save fails with an explanation and preserves your draft for correction. Partial preparation leaves the remaining tubes awaiting containers. Spare slots are empty capacity, not missing samples.

A sample's tubes may be split among shipments. Container choices do not change accepted sample counts or pricing. Assigned tubes and confirmed shipping inserts are not silently rearranged if configuration changes. Contact Phaeno when existing assignments or a dispatched shipment need reconciliation.

Adjust each row's tube count as needed. Counts must be whole numbers, fit each container and together equal the tubes allocated in the preview. The **Summary** stays visible while updating; confirmation becomes available when the current allocation has been checked.

## Scan and retain tube identities

Use **Shipping container** in the Job's **Samples and shipping** area to choose a container or remaining tubes awaiting containers. With several choices, select deliberately before using a shipment command; a single available choice is selected directly. The selected shipment reference remains visible. Switching container or returning to **Samples** asks before discarding an unsaved scan and is unavailable while saving. Open **Container, kit and receipt details** for supporting information without narrowing the scan area.

Before scanning starts, an organization or Department administrator can use **Reset container configuration** in **Container, kit and receipt details**. Review the confirmation: it returns **all containers and tubes for the Job** to container selection, not just the shipment you opened. Your finalized sample list stays intact, and the previous configurations remain in the records and audit history for support. Retired configurations stay out of normal container choices; an older direct link identifies them as history. Cancelling the confirmation changes nothing.

**Reset container configuration** is also unavailable while a barcode entry is unsaved or saving. Discard an unsaved entry before revising the plan; saving the first scan locks the plan as described below.

Before any tube is scanned, a successful reset releases the selected containers back to available inventory at their locations and returns the Job's tubes to preparation. Previous configurations remain in history. The action is locked once any container for the Job has a saved tube scan, bound tube roster, shipping insert, sample-return dispatch or sample receipt. Clearing a scan does not restore it. If another user starts work while your confirmation is open, refresh and review the current state. The finalized sample list, quote, original kit deliveries and their receipts remain intact.

Use a stable **Customer sample ID** that your organization can connect to its own sample record. Do not enter patient names, medical-record numbers, dates of birth or other direct identifiers.

Use **Match tubes** in **Samples and shipping** or the Job's **Actions** to match registered tubes in the selected container. One scan field opens inside the active tube row in the expanded sample. Container identity and overall progress stay above the list. For the displayed sample and tube number, scan its permanent barcode, enter the actual material amount and unit in that tube, then press Enter or **Save scan**. Each match is saved separately before the next sample and tube are revealed. A successful save updates the barcode and progress, expands the next unmatched tube’s sample, scrolls its row into view and focuses its inline scan field. Failed, unknown, ineligible, duplicate and already-used scans retain the entered barcode and current target for review and retry. The first saved match locks container reset.

Expand a sample to review its individual tubes, saved barcodes and container identities. The list keeps 10 samples per page; there is no separate Scan tubes view. While matching, saving reveals the next unmatched tube's sample and page. Reviewing another page or expanding a different sample does not change the active scan target or entered barcode. **Return to active tube** restores its sample and inline scan field, including the unfinished barcode. No barcode field is shown while the active sample is collapsed or on another page. The scan progress applies to the selected container; each sample's matched count covers all its active containers. **Done scanning** appears at the right of the **Samples and shipping** heading only after every tube in the selected container has a saved match. Leaving or changing containers preserves the existing unsaved-scan warning. Paging and dismissal are unavailable while saving. After the last match, the field closes and the same list remains open for review.

Use registered tubes belonging to the physical container assigned during preparation. The first successful tube scan changes that container from assigned to in use and locks reset; it does not choose a different container implicitly. A tube from another container, an unknown or duplicate barcode, or an already-used tube cannot advance the step. A failed scan keeps the entered value and current sample available for correction and retry. Do not cover, alter or remove permanent container or tube barcodes.

If the wrong tube was assigned, use **Edit tube** or **Correct tube** and record why the assignment changed. The earlier association remains in history. A correction after shipping insert confirmation, but before dispatch, voids that shipping insert and issues a corrected revision. Destroy unused copies of the old shipping insert and print the replacement.

## Confirm each shipment and print its shipping insert

Use the Job's **Actions** menu for **Selected shipment** commands, including **Review and confirm shipping insert**, **Review packing instructions**, **Print shipping insert**, **Download tube list (CSV)** and **Record shipment** when permitted. The shipment reference identifies the container affected; **This order** holds applicable order commands. If only one command is available, it appears directly. Your permissions and the complete shipment's stage determine availability. Completing one visible tube page does not complete the container. Authorized readers retain print and download access. Use **Print shipping insert** again for another copy of the current revision.

Within **Send**, review every physical tube in the container, then select **Review and confirm shipping insert**. Confirmation shows separate sample and tube counts and freezes the destination, instructions, manifest and crosswalk for that revision. A container can be partly filled when all its declared tubes are matched; spare capacity does not require extra tubes.

Print one receiving sheet for each physical container and place it inside that container. It shows the Customer, Job/Trial and shipment references, sample and tube totals, essential handling notes and two widely separated scan targets: the large **Scan to receive this shipment** PH-P- insert barcode and, when assigned, the physical container barcode. Follow the full preparation and packing instructions in the Portal before shipping. Follow the approved temperature-control method, amount and packing steps for the actual container. Instructions may call for regular ice, dry ice, cold packs, another method or no cooling. Do not substitute methods or multiply the amount by the number of samples. Contact Phaeno if the instructions are missing or conflict. **Full packing instructions and sample / tube list** on the insert page retains the confirmed manifest, every sample and tube barcode, and split-sample references; these details do not print with the receiving sheet. Use **Previous samples** and **Next samples** to review long manifests. Receiving-sheet totals always cover the complete container. Use **Download tube list (CSV)** to retain the complete crosswalk. Laboratory staff acknowledge container arrival in Receive shipments; the tubes are then accessioned individually. Customer barcode lookup alone does not record laboratory receipt.

When **Send** is current and the selected shipment's insert is ready, **Your next step** offers **Review packing and print**. It checks the current revision and displays the saved destination, common steps, sample requirements and actual container’s packing instructions. Review the cooling method and amount, then select **Print shipping insert**. The browser print dialog opens while keeping the Job page, selected container and saved tube matches intact. After the print dialog returns, explicitly confirm that the current insert was successfully printed and placed inside its correct container only when you have done both. Opening, closing or cancelling printing never makes that confirmation for you. If you cancelled printing, dismiss the follow-up and print again when ready. Print or revision-check errors also leave the work unacknowledged.

After your explicit printing-and-packing confirmation, **Your next step** changes to **Record shipment**. Your confirmation is remembered in this browser tab for this shipment's current insert. A corrected insert must be printed and confirmed again. You must still record the shipment after carrier handoff. **Send** stays unfinished until every required dispatch is recorded.

With several shipments, deliberately select the current container before printing or recording it; each has its own insert and confirmation. A sole available container remains selected directly. Reopen **Actions → Review packing instructions** at any time. Reprint the current insert through **Actions** for another copy; this does not issue a new revision. If the insert cannot be checked, review the message on the Job, reconnect if needed and select **Try again**. If insert confirmation fails, the message appears in the open dialog and your entries remain available. Retain the shipping insert or use **Download tube list (CSV)** so your organization can connect its own identifiers to the permanent tubes. The shipping insert does not buy postage or replace the carrier label.

## Record dispatch and follow receipt

After handing each package to the carrier, use **Record shipment** for its carrier, tracking number and shipment time. Repeat for each physical shipment. If you close the form after entering details, confirm whether to discard them; the form stays open while a save is in progress. **After you send** shows recorded dispatches, tracking and actual receipt; **Ordering and shipping** remains active for containers still being prepared. All required active shipments must have recorded dispatch before the customer shipping steps are complete. A later clarification or replacement request can still require your attention.

For each finalized sample, use the **Matched** and **Receipt** counts in **Samples**. Saved matches cover that sample's active shipments, while receipt is the actual recorded tube count. **Checking…** or **Not available** must not be read as zero. Per-container and Job-wide totals remain explicitly scoped in the scan and receipt details. A sample split across containers remains partly received until all its expected tubes have been recorded; receiving the first package does not mark the entire sample received.

Use **Samples** to return to the grouped Job overview or **Shipping container** to choose another container without leaving the Job. Existing standalone shipment bookmarks remain available, and Trial shipping keeps its existing entry point. If a tube cannot be matched confidently, a printed barcode cannot be read, or there is no compatible container, contact Phaeno with the Job and shipment references instead of guessing.

After a shipment is sent, select its **Shipping container** in **Samples and shipping**, then choose **Reprint shipping insert**. When several containers are available and none is selected, **Actions → Select container to reprint insert** takes you to the container selector. Reprinting opens the browser print dialog for the current insert without issuing a new revision or recording another shipment or receipt.


Shipping inserts, printable kit labels and retained packet copies show QR codes with readable identifiers. Phaeno-printed laboratory tube labels use DataMatrix and readable identifiers. Saved scan results show the identifier as text. Use a 2D scanner that supports both QR and DataMatrix where both workflows are performed. Existing manufacturer labels and older printed barcodes remain valid; do not relabel a tube or change its saved identity merely to match a display. A scan still requires the correct workflow and does not by itself confirm receipt or accession.

With one container, the tube count is assigned automatically up to its capacity and shown in a read-only text box. Any remaining tubes stay unallocated. With multiple containers, edit the tube counts to split the allocation. Returning to one container restores its automatic allocation.

Samples are ordered by ID alphabetically and digit by digit, rather than by numeric value. In the Job, biological-source groups stay together. Tube matching follows that sample order, with each sample’s tubes in tube-number order. Saving a match advances to the next unmatched tube.

Sample rows show matching progress as **1 of 1 tube matched** or **2 of 2 tubes matched**. This includes the tube total, so the row does not repeat a separate quantity while matching status is shown. Long sample IDs wrap; they are not shortened or changed.
