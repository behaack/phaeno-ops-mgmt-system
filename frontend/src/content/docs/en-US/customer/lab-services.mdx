# Request laboratory services

Customer organization administrators use Lab services to prepare a job, describe its samples, receive a job-specific quote, and authorize work.

## Create a draft

1. Open **Lab services** and select **Request lab service**.
2. In **Job details**, enter a required **Job name** that is unique within your organization. Choose whether all samples have the same **Biological source**. If they do, enter it once for the job; if sources vary, you will enter one for each sample. Enter the **Storage requirements** and **Safety declaration** shared by every sample, and any optional **Job notes**. Do not enter a patient name or identifier.
3. Select **Create job**. The portal assigns an immutable eight-character **Job number** using unambiguous uppercase letters and numbers.
4. The new job opens on its detail page. On **Samples & shipping**, select **Add sample** under **Samples**.
5. Enter that sample's non-patient identifier and number of tubes. If you said sources vary, also enter that sample's biological source; otherwise the job's shared source is used automatically. All samples are extracted RNA, use the job-level storage and safety information, and receive the same standard data-file set, so there is no material-type, unit, storage, safety, notes, analysis, or output selection in the sample form. Select **Add sample**.
6. Repeat **Add sample** for each physical sample. Use **Edit** if its draft information needs to change, or **Remove** to take an incorrect sample out of the draft.

The job remains a draft while samples are being added. Other members can see organization work but cannot create, edit, submit, or commercially approve it unless they are an organization administrator.

## Submit for review

Add at least one sample, then select **Submit for pricing** from the job detail page. Confirm that the request and sample identifiers contain no patient identifiers, PHI, or unnecessary personal data. Submission validates the current request and freezes an input revision. Phaeno can accept the information for quoting, request specific corrections, or reject work that cannot be performed.

When corrections are requested, create and submit a new revision. The prior revision remains in the job history; it is not silently overwritten.

## Review and accept a quote

Laboratory services are priced per job. Open **Quote & billing** to review the job's commercial status. Phaeno issues an itemized, immutable quote for the accepted scope. The quote is available only after its QuickBooks Online estimate has synchronized successfully.

Before accepting, confirm the sample list, standard service scope, price, currency, and expiration date. Only a Customer organization administrator can accept the current unexpired quote.

If the scope changes after acceptance, Phaeno issues a separate change quote. Added work does not begin until the applicable change is accepted.

## Ship and track samples

After accepting the quote, return to **Samples & shipping** in the job workspace. Follow the displayed submission instructions, match Phaeno-supplied tubes when the job includes a registered return kit, review the shipping packet, and record the carrier details after the package leaves your organization. Shipping remains part of the job; Customers do not use a separate Samples and shipping destination.

The Portal tracks receipt, accessioning, processing, external sequencing, data processing, scientific review, holds, and readiness for release. A job can remain in progress while different samples move through laboratory work.

If Phaeno needs replacement material or clarification, the job shows a Customer-safe action and the current schedule health. Internal scientific investigation details are not shown.

**Ready for release** means Phaeno's scientific review is complete. It does not mean a file is downloadable. Commercial publication, file scanning, and any payment or credit gate must still pass before a result appears on **Data & results**. Use **Timeline** to review the job's Customer-safe milestones.

## Open job data in the Data Library

Use **Open in Data Library** from **Data & results** to open the Data Library already scoped to this job. The job view shows only released result packages and files for that job and retains a path back to the job workspace. Use **View organization Data Library** to return to the organization’s separately assigned curated packages.

## Cancel work

A draft can be discarded before submission. Before Phaeno accepts work, an administrator can cancel eligible scope. After acceptance, submit a cancellation request. Phaeno records whether the request is approved, partially approved, or declined, and any financial adjustment remains linked through QuickBooks Online.
