# Trial history after Customer conversion

If your organization previously completed a Prospect Trial Project, **Trial projects** retains its approved scope, samples, customer-safe progress, results, and history in the original department. Active authorized members may view it. The same organization identity continues after Customer conversion.

**For Research Use Only. Not for use in diagnostic procedures.** Trial result archives include a manifest identifying the approved scope and files. Do not enter or send PHI or sample identity mappings through this workflow.

Conversion does not reopen a Trial or permit additional Trial submissions. Use the Customer laboratory-request workflow for new work. Trial results remain independent of Customer invoice, credit, or payment status.

Use **Retention and receipt** to review the release's original file policy and download history. Conversion does not restart or extend warning, retention, or grace dates. After complete release, earlier partial archives direct you to the complete package. Download controls show progress and explain unavailable files, including closed retention access; the workspace refreshes after a transfer. Withdrawn, held, or deleted files remain unavailable; retained metadata is not a promise of byte restoration. Contact Phaeno with the Trial number if a reissue is needed.
